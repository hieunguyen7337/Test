# 3. Research standard: reconstruct causality, not just events

The final guide is unacceptable if it merely says:

> A happened. Then B happened. Then C happened.

For every major arc, reconstruct:

> **starting problem → why the characters act → mechanism used → what directly happens → immediate consequence → permanent continuity consequence → what this changes about later options**

This project has already had summaries that contained many events but omitted **why** they happened. Do not repeat that failure.

A future model reading the Volume-Five guide must be able to answer questions such as:

- Why did Bai Ge choose that plan rather than another?
- Which system rule made the plan possible?
- Was a power temporary, permanent, borrowed, system-converted, a body core, a Divine-Key core, a residual authority, or only an external tool?
- What did each relevant character know **at that point**?
- Which history was active: original Current Era, rewritten Current Era, Previous Era, a reset run, or the paradox-generated branch?
- Did a familiar canon event occur for the same reason, a different reason, or only in public misinformation?
- If somebody survives, **what causal chain allowed the survival?**
- If somebody reaches the Current Era from the Previous Era, **how exactly?**
- If history updates, whose memories update, and which identity-protection rule still applies?
- If a relationship changes, what event causes the change and what is the exact end state?
- If a chapter title makes an extravagant claim, does the prose actually support it?

Every major claim should be traceable to actual novel evidence.

## System functions established by the end of Volume One

The immediate opening logic of Volume Two is therefore straightforward. Mei and Kiana experience **no visible time skip**: the Bai Ge who was secretly dying from Honkai corruption appears almost instantaneously healthy, radically stronger and more emotionally affected than before. Bai Ge, meanwhile, has just lived months of torture, family life, Herrscherhood, death and farewell that his present companions did not experience with him.

Volume One establishes the novel's governing formula: **Bai Ge enters a tragedy he remembers, turns fictional foreknowledge into a real relationship, and then changes history by refusing to let another person remain the price of the event.** The system converts those relationships and sacrifices into permanent mechanical growth, so the emotional rewrite of the Second Eruption becomes the literal power foundation from which Volume Two begins.

## Volume Three system/identity rules

## System, identity, information, and persistence mechanics established by Volume Three

Volume Three does not introduce a new historical-traversal mode, but it clarifies several system rules that future translation depends on.

### Affinity remains mechanically real

The relationship system is not merely a romance meter.

Volume Two established that sufficient affinity can make a character eligible for **targeted past traversal**. In Volume Three, **Kiana reaches MAX affinity**.

Therefore MAX is simultaneously:

- an emotional/romantic milestone;
- proof of Kiana's complete bond with Bai;
- and a mechanical signal that she is an obvious future historical-traversal target.

Volume Three does not launch that traversal, so the correct state is **eligibility established, traversal not yet used**.

### Temporal identity protection does not rewrite memories

The system's cognitive protection has four practical rules:

1. **Historical changes remain real.**
2. **Witnesses retain memories of the historical Bai they actually met.**
3. The system suppresses the easy cognitive link between that historical identity and present Bai.
4. Strong enough evidence can overcome the suppression.

This explains why the world can contain extensive records of Bai's historical interventions without every faction immediately solving his time-traveler identity.

It also explains why Otto's eventual recognition does not mean the system suddenly “fails.” Otto forces his way through by comparing unusually strong evidence.

### Recognition does not reveal the system

When Otto breaks through, he learns:

> **present Bai = historical Bai**

He does **not** learn:

> **Bai has a system that inserts him into past eras.**

Because Otto lacks that category of explanation, he constructs the closest in-world model he can: Bai must have some Sentience-like power that alters cognition.

Bai deliberately allows this misconception to survive because an enemy using the wrong model is strategically useful.

### Historical causal products persist independently of Bai's body

The return of Cecilia, Sirin and Bella reinforces the same rule established by Volumes One and Two:

- Bai's past body can disappear;
- the system can return him to the Current Era;
- but the people he saved continue living through the intervening years;
- promises, institutions, children and political consequences created by that history persist.

The fifteen-year reunion is direct proof.

### System powers and acquired world powers must remain separate

Future summaries should maintain at least these columns:

1. **system-granted permanent traits/rewards**  
   e.g. old fragments, Quantum, Time, Infinite Evolution Body;

2. **full physical/core-level Herrscher states acquired in the world**  
   e.g. Gem of Serenity, Gem of Desire;

3. **core states mediated through the Blank Key**  
   e.g. Reason/Wisdom and full Flame route;

4. **stigmata/body inheritances**  
   Kaslana, Schariac, Death/Seele-related;

5. **learned techniques**  
   Taixuan;

6. **constructed/equipment capability**  
   Project Bunny upgrades, pseudo-Star of Eden, Hyperion, Divine Keys;

7. **institutional authority**  
   Hyperion captaincy, St. Freya medical chief.

Flattening these into “Bai has X powers” destroys the causal logic of later volumes.

### Public knowledge is not equal to factual reality

By the end of Volume Three, at least three information layers can coexist:

- what **Bai and his household** know;
- what **St. Freya/AE allies** know;
- what **Otto/Schicksal and the wider world believe**.

The Wendy incident proves that Bai will deliberately engineer false public history. Otto's false Sentience theory proves that even correct observations can lead to incorrect explanations.

Future translation should therefore ask not only **“what is true?”** but **“who knows which truth?”**

## The causal architecture of Volume Four: system, memory, reset, and persistence

Volume Four is easy to misunderstand if it is read as a simple sequence of Previous-Era adventures. Its actual structure is governed by **four interacting mechanisms**: the system's historical traversal, temporary traversal traits, deliberate memory sealing/storage, and the `轮回之人` reset ability. The later parallel-Current-Era arc is a fifth mechanism created only after Bai deliberately breaks the normal traversal rules. These mechanics explain **why** the same Herrscher eras appear more than once, why Bai sometimes behaves as if he does not know facts that the player of *Honkai Impact 3rd* should know, why Elysia repeatedly reaches the same sacrifice despite wildly different histories, and why only Bai accumulates the full psychological burden of the loops.

### 1. Volume Four begins as one active system historical traversal

Bai does **not** abandon his original Current-Era body and permanently reincarnate into the Previous Era. As in the earlier simulator/traversal arcs, the system sends his operative self into real history while his proper Current-Era timeline is effectively waiting for the historical intervention to end. Changes made in the Previous Era are intended to become the history that leads into his original Current Era.

The protection supplied by the system is crucial. A death suffered by the historical/traversal body is not automatically the death of Bai's true Current-Era existence. Later, when he sees the paradox-created branch's Bai Ge repeatedly save people only to die for them, Bai explicitly thanks his own system: without it, his history-changing style would have produced exactly that outcome. The system gives him the freedom to spend a historical body as the price of changing tragedy and still return to the true Current Era when the traversal is properly completed.

This is the fundamental reason the final Origin solution can work. **The historical Bai Ge can die paying Elysia's price without erasing the true Bai Ge waiting at the end of the system traversal.**

### 2. The system gives Bai exactly three temporary traits for the Previous-Era traversal

The three-trait package can now be reconstructed completely. A much later system explanation explicitly says that the old historical-traversal mode granted **three traits**, and later passages independently identify the three traits operating throughout Volume Four:

| System trait | Function established by the novel | Status / continuity consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **`轮回之人` — Person of Reincarnation / Reincarnator** | Gives Bai access to the Previous-Era full-reset route. When Bai asks the system to “prepare reincarnation,” it manifests the **Time Hourglass (`时光沙漏`)**; flipping it returns the active historical run to its starting point. | This is the mechanical foundation of the 11 resets / 12 salvation runs. It is a traversal tool, not an automatic curse. The immediate post-clear reward screen does **not** separately list it as a permanent reward, so future translation should treat it as a PE-run trait unless the system later re-grants it. |
| **`永恒之心` — Eternal Heart** | Preserves Bai's **essential self and continuous rationality** despite repeated reincarnations and more than ten thousand subjective years of accumulated experience. The narrator explicitly says that even after ten reincarnations Bai's nature has not changed because of Eternal Heart. Later Bai reflects that without it, the accumulated longing and repeated lives would have driven him mad. | This explains why Bai can become exhausted, traumatized, obsessive about saving everyone, and overloaded by memory **without becoming a different personality**. A later arc explicitly notes that he no longer possesses Eternal Heart, confirming that it was a temporary traversal trait rather than an innate/permanent baseline. |
| **`奇迹` — Miracle** | `将不可能之事变为可能` — **turn an impossible thing into a possible one**. | Bai never activates/spends Miracle during the entire PE clear. The system later praises this fact: the “miracles” of Volume Four were achieved through Bai's own planning and power, not by consuming the trait. The fact that these three traits work on **different layers** is essential:

- **Reincarnator** controls whether Bai can restart the historical run.
- **Eternal Heart** keeps the person doing the restarting psychologically and morally continuous.
- **Miracle** is an emergency impossibility-breaker that Bai astonishingly never needs to invoke during the PE clear.

This triad explains why the system does not simply solve Volume Four for him. It provides **retry capacity, mental continuity, and a last-resort possibility**, but Bai still has to discover the Origin/Domination substitution mechanism himself.

A very important consequence is that `轮回之人` is **not an automatic Groundhog-Day curse**. The universe does not reset every time Bai loses. **Bai chooses to invoke the trait and physically flips the system-created hourglass.** That makes the repeated history an act of agency—and later, an act of self-inflicted burden.

For future volumes, the PE traits should be tracked independently from Bai's ordinary powers:

### 3. Bai deliberately seals Elysia-related foreknowledge during the first run

The first run contains a subtle memory rule that the earlier summary omitted. By the Eden rivalry section, the narrator explicitly says that **Bai has sealed his memories concerning Elysia**. This cannot mean he has erased the Elysia currently beside him: he plainly remembers naming her, living with her for years, loving her, travelling with her, and introducing her to other people.

The safest reading is therefore:

> **Bai suppresses/seals selected pre-existing Elysia-related foreknowledge from his old-world/game memories, while retaining the memories he is creating with the real Previous-Era Elysia.**

This distinction explains several otherwise strange parts of the volume. Bai knows the *Honkai* setting extremely well, but he does not simply enter the PE treating Elysia's deepest secret, Origin identity, relationship with Eden, and final decision as a solved script. He allows himself to meet **this** Elysia as a person rather than constantly responding to the canonical character he remembers. It also makes chapter 318's discovery consequential: Elysia's role in giving later Herrschers humanity becomes a causal conclusion they arrive at inside the rewritten history, not merely Bai reciting a spoiler he has consciously carried since chapter 173.

The source passage currently recovered proves the **fact of the seal**, but does not enumerate every memory placed behind it. Therefore a translator should not write “Bai forgot Elysia completely.” The demonstrated scope is selective: **old/meta Elysia knowledge is inaccessible; lived first-run Elysia memories remain active.**

This is memory operation **A** in Volume Four, and it must be kept separate from the later reset-memory problem.

### 4. What the Time Hourglass actually resets

At the end of the first successful civilization-saving run, Elysia still reaches the same conclusion: to give future Herrschers the possibility of humanity, she will use Origin to change Honkai's rules and sacrifice herself. Bai has already saved civilization extraordinarily well and desperately misses St. Freya. He explicitly says that nobody has the right to demand that he throw all of that success away.

He nevertheless does it, because **a victory in which Elysia still dies is not a victory he accepts**.

He tells the system to prepare reincarnation. The system responds and manifests an ornate **Time Hourglass (`时光沙漏`)**. Bai admits he had hoped to clear the PE without ever using the `轮回之人` trait. He then flips the hourglass.

The text defines the effect unambiguously:

> **Everything returns to the original beginning—tragedies and happy outcomes alike.**

Thus the hourglass is a **full active-run rollback**, not a local rewind of the last battle and not a resurrection spell. Bai deliberately sacrifices the entire successful civilization he has just built in order to retry history from the start.

The state model is:

| Category | After an hourglass reset |
|---|---|
| **World / historical events** | Return to the starting state of the PE traversal. Previous victories, rescues, institutions, relationships, and disasters must be rebuilt. |
| **Other people's ordinary active memories** | Do not remain normally accessible from run to run; they live the new run without consciously carrying the previous one. |
| **Bai's continuity** | Persists. He knows he has reset and can use prior-run information to change the next attempt. |
| **Bai's system and reset capability** | Persist as the meta-layer controlling the traversal. |
| **Hidden/sealed memory traces in other people** | Later shown to exist at least in Elysia: final release causes her sealed reincarnation memories to surface. They are **not** normally conscious during each run. |

This is memory operation **B**: **world-reset memory suppression**, distinct from Bai's deliberate first-run Elysia-foreknowledge seal.

### 5. Why Bai keeps resetting even after he can save the world

The repeated runs are not attempts to discover how to defeat ordinary Honkai. Bai already proves he can create a remarkably successful PE. The bottleneck is narrower and harder:

1. The Current Era Bai came from contains **human-aligned Herrschers** such as Mei, Sirin-related rewritten cases, and Bai himself.
2. Elysia deduces that this future is evidence that somebody in the PE changed the rules governing Herrschers.
3. Origin is the power capable of making that history-level change.
4. Elysia therefore concludes that **she** is the person who must make the miracle happen.
5. If Bai simply prevents her from acting, he risks deleting the causal reason future Herrschers can retain humanity.
6. If he allows her to act normally, she pays with her life.

So the real problem is not **“save Elysia.”** It is:

> **Preserve Elysia's civilization-changing miracle, preserve the Current Era that depends on that miracle, but change the identity of the person who pays for it.**

That is why brute force, imprisoning Elysia, winning every Herrscher battle, and conventional core theft all fail to solve the volume. A later chapter even recalls that in the **ninth reincarnation** Bai physically imprisoned Elysia in the Deep Place—blindfolded, bound with Judah's chains, and isolated specifically to stop her sacrifice. **She still succeeds in activating Origin and sacrificing herself.** Bai learns that loving Elysia cannot mean putting her in a cage; more mechanically, Origin cannot be defeated by ordinary physical confinement.

By chapter 344 the Time Hourglass has been flipped repeatedly—one, two, three, and eventually **ten times** in the narrated count. The system warns Bai that his mental state has reached its limit. The final farewell reveals the broader completed accounting as **11 reincarnations/resets and 12 salvation runs**.

### 6. Bai deliberately engineers hyperthymesia because loop knowledge is his weapon

Bai's problem after repeated resets is not merely emotional pain. It is information scale. Every run contains countless Honkai eruptions, civilian deaths, political failures, enemy timings, Herrscher appearances and opportunities to intervene. If he can remember them all precisely, the next run can be optimized.

He therefore turns memory itself into a weapon. During a later run he deliberately manipulates his MANTIS/fusion-warrior development so that he acquires **`超忆症` — hyperthymesia / extreme autobiographical memory**. The effect is effectively that **he cannot naturally forget**. He records an absurd number of Honkai-related incidents across the repeated histories—later prose describes the dataset on the scale of **tens of millions / more than thirty million incidents**.

This is why the later “perfect” runs can look almost precognitive. Bai is not receiving a new omniscience system cheat. He has personally lived the failures and memorized the schedule.

The cost is horrifying: the human mind has finite working capacity. Even dreams become dangerous because hyperthymesia records dream experience too. After one pleasant dream of an entire ordinary lifetime with Sakura consumes valuable memory capacity, Bai asks **Aponia to impose a Discipline that prevents him from dreaming**.

### 7. Fenghuang Down is an external memory archive, not simple deletion

Hyperthymesia creates the opposite problem from forgetting: Bai accumulates too much memory to keep every run in active consciousness.

He therefore uses **Fenghuang Down (`羽渡尘`)** to manage memory. Later prose explains that he periodically “clears” memories, but **never truly deletes them**. Misteln provides/helps maintain backup storage, and the memories are preserved inside Fenghuang Down rather than erased from existence.

The best model is:

- **active cognition** = only the information Bai can safely keep mentally loaded;
- **Fenghuang Down archive** = memories temporarily sealed/offloaded from active thought but preserved;
- **hyperthymesia** = makes Bai compulsively/automatically preserve lived detail and motivates him to reconstruct anything he loses;
- **reset continuity** = tells Bai that previous runs happened and lets him deliberately use their accumulated information;
- **other characters' loop memories** = ordinarily sealed/inaccessible until the final release.

This is memory operation **C**: **Bai's deliberate memory offloading**, which is neither his early selective Elysia-foreknowledge seal nor the hourglass's reset of everyone else's active history.

The final chapter makes this distinction explicit. Fenghuang Down breaks and the memories stored within it spill out like a visible historical record. Elysia, Kevin, MEI, Aponia, Sakura, Kalpas, Hua, Mobius and the others finally see **11 reincarnations and 12 salvations**. At the same moment, the reincarnation memories sealed deep inside Elysia also surface. Bai comments that the system has done something “extra”: he had wanted those memories to remain hidden.

### 8. Why chapters 371–381 require a parallel Current Era instead of simply returning home

After thousands of subjective years of failed solutions, Bai realizes that the authority he needs does not yet exist in the PE form required for his plan. He needs the **Current-Era Herrscher of Domination**, because its `支配` can control another Herrscher's **authority without extracting an ordinary physical core**. This is uniquely suited to Elysia because Origin, like Finality, is a special existence-authority rather than an ordinary removable core.

But the system traversal has a rule: Bai has **not yet ended the PE intervention**, so his true original “present” is still waiting at the point where he departed. Bai instead uses **`万物休眠`** to leap forward inside the still-active historical operation, and he overshoots to the Current-Era Domination period rather than returning normally to his original departure point.

That creates an impossible chronology. The world cannot reconcile “Bai is still historically in the PE traversal” with “Bai is physically present in a later Current Era that presupposes a different history.” Ordinary corrective adaptation is insufficient, so reality produces a **compatible parallel Current Era**.

This is not the system deliberately choosing an alternate-universe mission. It is a **temporal contradiction generated by Bai exploiting a nonstandard route**.

The branch contains its own Bai Ge, but the system is unique and **cannot be copied into parallel worlds**. The branch Bai therefore follows the same self-sacrificial instincts without the system's historical-body safety net and eventually dies saving Mei, Sirin, Bronya/Seele, Kiana and others. The system supplies original Bai with the branch counterpart's memories and emotions so he can operate coherently there.

His objective in that branch is narrow:

**obtain Domination → use branch Otto/PROMETHEUS's Imaginary-Tree route → return to the decisive PE moment → save Elysia → then let the system end the historical traversal normally and return to the original Current Era.**

This chain is the reason the branch arc exists. Without it, chapters 371–381 look like a random detour.

### 9. The final solution is a system-protected substitution at a much larger scale

The logic mirrors Bai's earlier X-10 solution in Volume Two: when a burden appears causally necessary, Bai does not merely erase the event—**he takes the burden himself**.

Domination lets him sever Elysia from Origin. Bai then uses Origin to make the rule-changing wish himself. The miracle survives; future Herrschers can still escape Honkai's absolute control. But because **Bai is now the user**, the life-price attaches to his historical traversal body rather than Elysia.

The system is what makes this substitution survivable in the broader narrative. Bai's PE body disintegrates irreversibly, but the system has already prepared his return to the original Current Era. He can therefore spend the historical body to buy Elysia's life.

After the wish is complete, Bai releases Domination and **returns Origin to Elysia**. Because the wish has already freed Herrschers—including Origin herself—from Honkai's old restrictions, Elysia receives Origin back in a liberated state rather than returning to the original sacrificial condition.

That is the causal skeleton of Volume Four:

**system gives retry capability → Bai voluntarily suppresses foreknowledge to live the first Elysia relationship genuinely → first world can be saved but Elysia still sacrifices herself → Bai invokes the hourglass → repeated full-history runs prove brute force cannot solve Origin → Bai engineers perfect memory and external memory storage to optimize retries → he learns he needs authority-level Domination → a forbidden forward jump creates a parallel Current Era where he can obtain it → False God/Imaginary Tree gives him the route back → Bai substitutes himself as Origin's user/payer → Elysia lives while the Current Era's human-Herrscher miracle remains intact.**

## Full chronological story reconstruction

The chronology below follows Faloo's displayed numbering and titles. Where a title is deliberately comic or metaphorical, the prose meaning is used instead of literalizing the title.

**Chapters 173–180 — Raven's island joke becomes the doorway into Elysia's childhood.** Volume Four opens exactly where Volume Three said it would: Raven discovers that Bai Ge is responsible for the destruction of the island/villa she valued so highly. Faloo then immediately pivots into the central Previous-Era intervention: chapter 174 has Bai thinking about giving a girl the cutest possible name, and chapter 175 is explicitly `你的名字是爱莉希雅` — **“Your name is Elysia.”** This is not merely Bai meeting the canonical adult Elysia earlier. The fanfiction makes him part of the construction of her personal identity: in this rewritten history, **Bai is the person who gives her the name Elysia.**

Recovered Chinese prose later in this childhood phase confirms that Bai and the young Elysia live together for **more than three years** in a small town, reject opportunities that would separate them, and become one another's primary family and emotional anchor. This makes the eventual adult romance qualitatively different from canon: Elysia's attachment to Bai predates Fire MOTH, the Flame-Chasers and almost every other major PE relationship. Chapter 178's title says Elysia “wants to become human,” but because the available prose around the exact transformation was incomplete, that title should **not** be inflated into a precise biological mechanism. It is safe to say the volume deliberately foregrounds Elysia's unusual origin and her desire for human belonging; it is not safe, on the title alone, to specify an exact species-change procedure.

By chapter 180 Bai and Elysia are travelling together through the Previous Era and reach that era's Nagazora. The relationship is already intimate enough that later jokes about proposals, marriage and jealousy are extensions of an established bond rather than sudden harem recruitment.

**The first-run memory seal is already active here.** Bai has not entered this relationship with his complete old-world Elysia script consciously loaded. Later narration, during the Eden conflict, explicitly remarks that he has **sealed his memories concerning Elysia**. Yet he obviously still remembers every lived day with the girl beside him. The distinction is important: the seal concerns selected pre-existing Elysia foreknowledge/meta-memory, not the relationship he is building in this run. It lets the real Elysia surprise him, lets her secrets remain hers until she chooses/reasons them out, and prevents Bai's franchise knowledge from replacing genuine first-hand attachment.

**Chapters 181–197 — Kevin, Dr. MEI, early Herrschers, Mobius, Fire MOTH and Vill-V.** Faloo's sequence places Kevin and Su jokes in chapter 181 and Dr. MEI's interest in Bai in chapter 182; chapter 183 asks why one would oppose the stars, chapter 184 gives Elysia the “fight for all that is beautiful” motif, and by chapters 185–186 a Previous-Era Herrscher of the Void is involved. Mobius appears in the following sequence, followed by Bai and Elysia's entry into Fire MOTH and then Vill-V's engineering material. Chapters 194–197 explicitly concern Vill-V assessing Bai/Elysia, Mobius mocking Vill-V, Vill-V's anti-Bai weapon model 007, and Vill-V's “if I want to, I can” confidence.

This early Herrscher-of-the-Void material matters for Bai's later power ledger. By chapter 318 his own full-body core audit includes **Void** as a genuine core-level authority, although Void was **not** part of the five-body-core configuration entering Volume Four. The safest inference is that the PE intervention provides the full Void component before chapter 318; it must not be confused with the old permanent **fragmentary Void authority** inherited from Volume One. The novel itself makes those categories coexist.

**Chapters 198–205 — the Third Eruption is rewritten, Hua is saved, and Bai becomes a PE fusion warrior.** Faloo explicitly marks chapter 198 as the Previous Era's Third Eruption, chapter 199 as “the Third Eruption, the rescued Hua,” and chapter 200 as Bai Ge and Elysia versus the Herrscher of Thunder and Sesa. Chapter 201 has Elysia complaining that Bai has again stolen her Herrscher kill. The critical historical divergence is therefore not subtle: **Hua's trajectory begins under a history in which Bai is physically present during the Third Eruption and actively prevents a canonical disaster from unfolding normally.**

Chapters 202–205 move into Mobius, Kevin and MANTIS research. Chapter 204 explicitly pairs **Bai versus Kevin** with the fusion-warrior operation, and chapter 205 calls him `融合战士白歌` (“fusion-warrior Bai Ge”) and gives Mobius a kiss scene. By Bai's own later self-description he is an **apocalyptic-class fusion warrior** before the later Chimera upgrade. Thus chapter 205 is the safe point at which his PE MANTIS/fusion-warrior status becomes established; the vastly more elaborate Chimera/Active Honkai Reaction state comes later and should not be back-projected here.

**Chapters 206–223 — Elysia, Fire MOTH politics, Sakura/Rin, and the Ice Herrscher.** The 206–209 titles mix Elysia comedy with her companion Honkai beast; the sequence then turns toward decisions about friendship versus hostility, institutional incompetence and Bai's rising authority. Sakura enters Bai's personal orbit by chapter 214; chapter 216 has Rin calling him **brother-in-law**, already framing Sakura/Bai as a recognizable romantic direction. Chapters 217–220 culminate in Bai asserting that when he gives an order, the world will make way, followed by the directory's explicit description that Fire MOTH's leadership has **fully submitted**. This does not necessarily mean Bai carries a conventional bureaucratic title above every MOTH office, but politically the organization is no longer canon Fire MOTH with Bai as a mere operative: he has become a dominant strategic authority.

The Ice-Herrscher sequence is unusually well cross-checkable. Faloo places the one-shot/defeat in chapters 221–223, while mirror prose shows Bai writing four characters — essentially **“very simple”** — in his mission assessment and Elysia joking that she has been unable to secure a Herrscher kill since the Second: Wind escaped her and Ice effectively spawned in front of Bai and was disposed of by him. This prose is important because the chapter titles alone sound like broad gag exaggeration; here the gag does reflect the underlying result. The **Ice core becomes part of Bai's later seven-core audit**.

Chapter 224's `请让我做小吧` (“please let me be the secondary wife”) is a useful example of why titles cannot be converted into major continuity. The recovered prose shows that it is a relatively peripheral Fire MOTH female staffer suddenly confessing to Bai. It is a harem comedy beat, **not** proof that a new central heroine becomes his formal wife in chapter 224.

**Chapters 225–231 — hope replacing tragedy, then Eden.** The directory explicitly frames chapter 225 as “tragedy is not the end, but the beginning of hope,” a mission statement for the volume. Chapters 226 onward move into Bai and Eden performing together, eating together, Eden's feelings about her own value, a scandal/rumour around them, a drunken Eden giving Bai gemstones, and finally an Elysia-versus-Eden emotional showdown. The intended relationship development is unmistakable: **Eden becomes a genuine romantic rival/partner candidate, not merely a famous singer Bai happens to meet.**

**Chapters 232–249 — the Deep Place, the Previous Era's Death Herrscher “Xier,” Pardo, Aponia, Abyss Flower, and mass rescue.** Chapter 232 explicitly places a Herrscher in the Deep Place/prison complex. Chapters 233–235 introduce the PE Death Herrscher whom the directory spells **`希尔`**, then describe Bai “taking out the core / taming the Death Herrscher Xier” and Xier becoming possessive enough to tell him to keep the pink fairy away. The wording should be handled carefully: “acquired/removed a core” does **not** automatically mean Bai installed that core into his own body. His later chapter-318 audit still calls him seven-core and explicitly distinguishes cores stored in Divine Keys from bodily cores.

This character is also a translation trap. Faloo's spelling here is **希尔**, whereas the familiar Current-Era Seele is **希儿**. Later Volume Four titles deliberately speak of the PE **White Xier and Black Xier**. A translator should therefore preserve her as a **Previous-Era Xier/Seele-analogue distinct from Current-Era Seele Vollerei** unless surrounding prose explicitly identifies them metaphysically. Do not silently merge their memories, bodies or personalities.

The next sequence moves through Klein, Bai's blood-based rescue of large numbers of people, Pardofelis, and Aponia's sanatorium. Chapter 241 explicitly associates the sanatorium episode with the Divine Key **Abyss Flower**; the following chapters place Bai in an increasingly personal relationship with Aponia and include his promise that Pardofelis will not die. Chapter 249 is especially important for Bai's medical/political role: the title explicitly combines **Shariac Holy Blood**, White Flower/Abyss Flower imagery and the rescue of **tens of millions**. Even allowing for Faloo's hyperbolic titling, later prose confirms that Shariac and healing-related capabilities remain part of Bai's actual toolkit.

**Chapters 250–266 — Eden, Vill-V, Griseo and Misteln are folded into Bai's rewritten social world.** Eden considers becoming a fusion warrior; Vill-V's yandere personality becomes more prominent; Bai and Eden have an overtly romantic candlelight sequence; and a subsequent chapter ends with a spectacular pink whale and pale-white dragon flying together over Fire MOTH. Griseo then receives an extended quiet relationship scene. Recovered prose shows her sitting on Bai's lap while painting, recognizing how much he works and trying to comfort him by patting his head. Her attachment is sincere and childlike at this stage. Later titles in the volume include Griseo asking whether Bai will wait for her to grow up, but that is **future-oriented romantic intent, not evidence that the child Griseo is being treated as an adult partner in the present scene.**

Misteln enters under dramatically rewritten circumstances. Faloo has her asking “Master, kill me,” then has Bai **give her the name Misteln Schariac**, after which she compares her strength with Dark Seele and fights Elysia. This Misteln should therefore be treated as Bai's PE-created/named Misteln continuity, not casually overwritten with every canonical Hare assumption. Her exact ontological relationship to Schariac stigmata is one of the points where retrieved chapter titles are insufficient to specify more than the fact that Bai names and claims responsibility for her.

Kevin asking Bai to lend him Shamash in chapter 260 is also significant for ownership language: in this history, **Bai can be the possessor/custodian of a Divine Key canon strongly associates with Kevin**, and Kevin may have to borrow it. Later, Kevin asks Bai to give him Shamash. Translators therefore should not assume “Kevin's Shamash” at every point in the PE chronology.

**Chapters 267–282 — the PE Herrscher of Sentience and Aponia/Kalpas relationship conflict.** Chapter 267 announces the Previous Era's Herrscher of Sentience; chapter 268 is a “perfect dream” involving weddings; chapter 269 pits Misteln against Sentience; chapter 270 reduces the Herrscher to an `阿巴阿巴` gag state. The broad result is safe: the Sentience incident does **not** reproduce canon as an irreversible annihilation. The Herrscher is neutralized inside Bai's increasingly absurd “save/capture rather than simply kill” pattern. Exact long-term custody or core ownership should remain open unless the relevant prose is available.

The following run frames Bai as the PE's most dazzling hero, has Sakura obtain a romantic advantage before Elysia, and creates a comic `白门` (“Bai Sect/Bai School”) motif with Elysia as an organizer/leader. This is primarily harem/fan-club/religion parody; it should not automatically be elevated into a world religion with independent geopolitical sovereignty. Chapter 276 is more continuity-relevant because Bai explicitly says he likes **Elysia most**, reinforcing her privileged position even as the romantic network expands.

Aponia then gets a concentrated relationship arc. Kalpas comments on Aponia's worthiness and on Bai, the women react badly to Aponia, and Aponia asks for a chance. This establishes a real place for Aponia in Bai's emotional orbit, although her precise label at the end of Volume Four is less explicit than Elysia, Sakura or Eden.

**Chapters 283–317 — Rock, Thousand/Domination, Binding, Tianyuan, Chimera and the Fifteen Flame-Chasers.** This is the volume's largest mechanical-growth block. Bai and the Flame-Chasers fight the Rock Herrscher; a white dragon and meteor appear; Elysia's emotional valuation of Bai over “the world” becomes explicit; Bai gains/uses something compared in the title to Durandal's “cheat”; and chapter 287 explicitly links this to the **Tianyuan path** as the Thousand Herrscher enters the story.

The Thousand-Herrscher material becomes a hunt in which Flame-Chasers compete over targets and even the Herrscher complains about how miserable its existence is. Dr. MEI is increasingly presented not just as a scientist but as someone capable of designing Herrscher-killing solutions. The following Chimera/Binding block is crucial: chapter 294 links the **Chimera Project** with the Herrscher of Binding; chapter 299 begins the Bai/Kevin Chimera plan; chapter 301 explicitly says the Tianyuan path was created here; chapter 302 celebrates an extremely advanced `人为崩落`; and chapter 303 pits Kevin against a nine-headed Chimera Honkai beast.

The Binding fight reaches chapter 308, whose title says **“seize the Binding core.”** Yet Bai's later first-person audit in chapter 318 still calls him a **seven-core Herrscher**, not eight-core or twelve-core. In the same conversation he jokes that if he ripped the Herrscher cores out of his Divine Keys and installed them, he could immediately become twelve-core. This gives a strong rule for the translator-facing ledger: **possessing/seizing a core is not synonymous with integrating that core into Bai's body.** Cores can be captured, stored, built into Divine Keys, loaded into the Blank Key, or otherwise held separately.

The **Fifteen Flame-Chasers** appear in Faloo's directory in two distinct ways that should both be preserved. Chapter **310** is titled simply **“Fifteen Flame-Chasers” (逐火十五英桀)**, while chapter **317** is explicitly **“Fifteen Flame-Chasers, Established!” (逐火十五英桀，成立！)**. Thus the number fifteen is unquestionably intentional, and chapter 317 is the explicit formal-establishment beat. The prose also gives the roster directly. Bai proposes himself and Elysia plus **Eden, Sakura, Dr. MEI, Aponia, Hua, Vill-V, Mobius, Kevin, Su, Kalpas, Kosma, Griseo, and Pardofelis**. Those are the formal fifteen. **Misteln and PE Xier are important associates but are not among the fifteen names Bai proposes.** Bai also states that the Flame-Chaser identity is primarily a shared heroic/familial identity rather than a grant of bureaucratic privilege. Do not silently “correct” the group back to the canonical Thirteen.

**Chapters 318–320 — Bai finally tells Elysia the truth.** This is one of the most important prose-confirmed scenes in the volume. Elysia discovers that Bai is from **50,000 years in the future**. Bai admits he had wanted to tell her earlier but had indulged her desire to solve his mystery herself. She reacts not with fear but delight at the romantic absurdity of a love spanning fifty millennia. She also asks about the names she has heard him mention in his sleep — Mei, Kiana and “Duck/Bronya” — revealing that his Current-Era relationships have been leaking into his PE domestic life long before the explicit confession.

Bai then reveals another secret: he is himself a Herrscher. His chapter-318 self-audit is exceptionally useful because it gives the cleanest mid-volume full-core inventory:

**Reason + Void + Herrscher of Flamescion (薪炎) + Life/Death + Azure Wind + Azure Wind + Ice = seven full cores.**

The double Wind is intentional and counts as two cores. This is **in addition to**, not a replacement for, the five old permanent fragmentary authorities from earlier volumes. Bai also says that removing the cores currently housed in Divine Keys could make him a twelve-core Herrscher, which proves those Key cores are being tracked separately from his body. At this point he also describes himself as an apocalyptic-class fusion warrior, a Tianyuan-level human, and a candidate for further Chimera enhancement.

Elysia's reaction creates the volume's central causal paradox. After Bai tells her about the Current Era and about Herrschers such as himself, Sirin and Mei retaining human hearts, she reasons that **someone in the Previous Era must have changed the rules of Honkai**. Because Origin has the exceptional ability to make such a world-level wish, she realizes that the person is probably herself. She also understands that such a miracle may require her life. Instead of recoiling, she is happy that her future action can protect Bai. This is why “saving Elysia” cannot simply mean stopping her from becoming Origin or preventing her from helping future Herrschers: Bai needs to preserve **the effect of Elysia's sacrifice while eliminating the sacrifice itself**.

Faloo follows this with chapter 319, `前文明和现文明共存计划` — a **Previous-Era and Current-Era coexistence plan** — and chapter 320, “beat the Herrscher of Finality once and then run.” The accessible titles establish the objective; they do not justify treating every detail of the coexistence mechanism as solved at this stage.

**Chapters 321–328 — Sakura, Rin, Corruption and the Eternal Elysian Realm.** Sakura's romance is made explicit in chapters 321–322 through “may this instant become eternity” and “I am your shrine maiden alone.” Chapter 323 then does something canonically enormous: it combines the **arrival of the Herrscher of Corruption** with a **confession from Rin**. In other words, Rin is not simply erased into the canonical tragedy that drives Sakura to disaster; Bai's history has decoupled Rin's continued personal existence from the normal Corruption catastrophe. Chapter 324 even characterizes Corruption as afraid to die and as receiving a suicidal “gift” from Honkai.

Chapters 326–328 create Bai and Elysia's `永世乐土`, best rendered **Eternal Elysian Realm** to distinguish it from the canon `往世乐土 / Elysian Realm`. There is a fairy administrator and even a chapter built around using its typewriter for a Werewolf-style game. The emotional function is therefore very different from canon's memorial structure for dead heroes: Bai is building a durable continuity-space **for people he is actively trying to keep alive**. It may preserve records/simulations as well, but translating it merely as the normal canon Elysian Realm would erase the fanfic-specific distinction.

**Chapters 329–344 — Finality, Origin and repeated destruction.** Chapter 329 combines an Elysia date with the arrival of Finality; 330 starts the “Salvation Battle”; 331 is Bai and Kevin versus the Herrscher of Finality; 332 jokingly says they will “dig out Finality's core”; 333 uses Taixu Sword God against her; and 334–335 have an awakened Finality explicitly remember Bai.

Chapter 332 is a particularly dangerous title to translate literally. Later prose in the same volume explicitly explains that **Origin and Finality are exceptional Herrschers who do not operate like ordinary core-bearing Herrschers: their existence itself is their authority.** Consequently, “taking out Finality's core” cannot simply be entered into the inventory ledger as “Bai obtains a Finality core.” At minimum it is an attempted/gag framing that later mechanics supersede.

Chapters 336–340 escalate into Bai's death/life rhetoric, his declaration that he will ignite the sea of stars, Griseo flying toward the stars in an Active Honkai Reaction state, and Elysia/world-naming material. Chapter 342 then explicitly identifies **Herrscher of Origin Elysia**, and chapter 343 has Bai call Elysia the reason he fell in love with the world. This is not a newly introduced casual Herrscher state: by chapter 318 Elysia had already understood that her Origin identity was the key to future human Herrschers.

Chapter 344 is not merely vague “replay-like” material: it is the payoff of the system trait **`轮回之人`**. At the end of the first civilization-saving history Bai tells the system, **“prepare reincarnation.”** The system answers and manifests a large **Time Hourglass (`时光沙漏`)**. Bai says explicitly that he had hoped to complete the PE without using this trait at all. He already has a world he could justifiably call saved; he also desperately wants to go home to St. Freya. What changes his decision is Elysia's continued willingness to die for the future.

Bai flips the hourglass only after promising her that **this time the highest-priority rescue target is not civilization but Elysia herself**. The prose then defines the reset: *everything* returns to the first starting point, whether tragedy or happiness. This means Bai knowingly deletes the successful history he just made—rescued people, victories, relationships and institutional progress—from the active timeline and undertakes the burden of building it again.

The retries are experimental rather than repetitive. Bai tests different ways of breaking the Elysia endpoint. He tries ordinary power, changing earlier causal conditions, stealing Origin, and even physical imprisonment. Later continuity explicitly recalls the **ninth reincarnation**, where Bai creates the Deep Place specifically as a prison for Elysia, blindfolds her and binds her with Judah so she cannot reach the sacrificial moment. **She still activates Origin and sacrifices herself.** This failure teaches both a mechanical and emotional lesson: Origin cannot be prevented by merely caging Elysia, and “saving” the person he loves by denying her every choice is not the answer either.

The hourglass is flipped repeatedly—one, two, three, and eventually **ten times in chapter 344's running count**. The system begins issuing mental-state warnings. Almost every failed run proves the same cruel fact: Bai can save the wider world, but Elysia still chooses the sacrifice because the Current Era's humanity-bearing Herrschers depend on the rule change. The final memory reveal later gives the completed accounting as **11 reincarnations/resets and 12 salvation runs**.

**Chapters 345–370 — later runs become an information-optimization project, not simply stronger replays.** Chapter 345 begins another world-spanning journey after the failed resets. The sequence deliberately repeats Herrscher stages seen before while refining the outcome: chapter 349 says **five Herrschers have been captured**, chapter 350 celebrates an efficient, loyal and heavily armed Fire MOTH, chapter 355 brings another Sixth-Herrscher phase, chapter 359 frames Rock as a **revenge** fight, and chapter 365 says Binding **returns**. These are later-run versions of eras Bai has already lived, not accidental duplicate encounters in one flat chronology.

The reason these runs become increasingly efficient is one of the most important causal details in Volume Four: **Bai weaponizes memory**. He eventually engineers his fusion-warrior/MANTIS development to acquire `超忆症`—hyperthymesia, an inability to naturally forget autobiographical experience. He then records the locations/timings/outcomes of an enormous number of Honkai incidents across the failed histories. Later prose puts the information set on the scale of **more than thirty million Honkai-related cases**. What looks like supernatural foreknowledge in a later run is often simply data he personally paid for by living the disaster before.

Hyperthymesia is not a free upgrade. His memory load becomes so extreme that even harmless dreams are a resource problem: dream-time can contain long subjective experiences which his condition then records permanently. After one ordinary-life dream with Sakura consumes still more mental capacity, Bai asks **Aponia to apply a Discipline that prevents him from dreaming**.

Bai's solution is **Fenghuang Down as externalized memory storage**. He periodically clears selected memories out of active consciousness, but the story later states that he **does not truly delete them**. They are backed up/sealed in Fenghuang Down. Misteln is horrified enough to wish she had never helped preserve the backups and tells him he should forget them; Bai's answer is effectively that he would simply record them again. This is why “Bai removed his memories” must never automatically be translated as “those events ceased to exist for him forever.”

This creates a three-layer memory system during the loops:

1. **Bai's currently loaded conscious memory**, pruned so he can function;
2. **Bai's archived loop memory in Fenghuang Down**, preserved but not always actively felt/thought about;
3. **other people's reset/sealed loop memories**, normally inaccessible during the new run, but later shown to remain recoverable at least for Elysia.

The full emotional cost is exposed only at the farewell: **11 reincarnations/resets and 12 salvations**, spanning more than **ten thousand subjective years** of accumulated road. The chapter-363 scene where Bai cries in his sleep is therefore not generic fatigue. His daily cheerfulness exists on top of histories he has intentionally moved out of active cognition because carrying all of them at once is unbearable. Eden, Aponia and Elysia care for him without yet understanding the complete scale of what he is carrying.

The late pass also confirms that the PE Xier continuity develops a **White/Black pair** in chapter 356. These are not automatically Current-Era White Seele and Dark Seele. The names, era and causal origin must remain separate in translation.

Griseo undergoes her own fusion-warrior/MANTIS evolution around chapter 360. Klein is alive late enough to confess in chapter 364. A Finality-associated companion Honkai beast appears in chapter 367, followed by Flame-Chasers fighting a **planetary-class** Honkai beast and then a Chimera described as beyond the “apocalyptic” class. The power scale of this rewritten PE has therefore moved far past the standard “the Flame-Chasers are losing one by one” structure.

Chapter 370 ends this phase with Bai **kissing Finality and going to the Current Era**. Finality is consequently not being treated as a faceless, permanently slain final boss. Whatever the complete loop history, the late PE contains a Finality capable of recognizing and personally interacting with Bai.

**Chapters 371–374 — this is not Bai's original Current Era.** The subsequent prose supplies one of Volume Four's most important time-travel rules. Bai had originally left the Current Era around Kiana's Herrscher-of-the-Void awakening. During the still-active Previous-Era historical intervention, however, he uses `万物休眠` to jump forward again **without formally ending the system's time-travel event**. He lands around the Current Era's Thousand/Herrscher-of-Domination crisis instead. The contradiction between “Bai is still in the past” and “Bai is physically present in the future” is too large for ordinary correction. A **compatible parallel world/timeline forms**.

This immediately resolves an apparent continuity trap in chapters 371–378. The Kiana, Otto, Kevin, Void Archives, Gray Serpent and others Bai meets here are **branch counterparts**. Their states do not automatically overwrite the original Volume-Three cast. The branch's Bai Ge had also altered many fates, but because a parallel world **cannot duplicate Bai's unique system**, that Bai ultimately died. The incoming system-holder receives/possesses the parallel Bai's memories and therefore understands the branch. The Thousand Herrscher can truthfully insist “Bai Ge is already dead” while the real system-bearing Bai is standing there, because she means that timeline's Bai.

Bai's target in this branch is not sentimental reunion but **Domination authority**. The prose explicitly distinguishes `支配` from his normal `夺取`: Bai ordinarily seizes a Herrscher's **core**, then can install/use it in his body or Blank Key; Domination can take/control another Herrscher's **authority without taking that Herrscher's core**. This distinction is essential because Elysia/Origin is itself an authority-existence and does not present a normal core for Bai simply to pull out.

The Current Era's Herrscher of Domination has also been strengthened in this fanfic's world logic because this era contains so many human-aligned Herrschers: its authority is particularly suited to dominating the powers of other Herrschers. Bai therefore concludes that Domination can solve the Elysia problem: use it on **Origin**, perform the necessary wish/miracle without making Elysia personally pay the canonical fatal cost, then return to the PE decision point.

Gray Serpent gives Bai a number of Thousand-Herrscher cores, but the prose emphasizes that the complete Herrscher is associated with **1,001**, with one still concealed during the acquisition process. Chapter 374's directory title, “obliterate Herrscher of Domination · Rabble,” summarizes the military side of this acquisition but should not be mistaken for Bai simply destroying the whole power source and walking away.

**Chapters 375–381 — Kiana, branch Otto, PROMETHEUS, False God and the Imaginary Tree.** Branch Kiana's emotional response is genuine: chapter 375 has her swear she will never again let Bai leave. But this is **parallel Kiana reacting to the return of a person whose branch counterpart died**. It is not evidence that the original Current-Era Kiana has already experienced Bai's Volume-Four absence and return.

The same rule applies even more strongly to Otto. Chapters 377–378 have this branch's Otto recognize Bai's plan to “rush the Tree” and offer him loyalty. That must **not** be carried into Volume Five as “original Otto is now Bai's loyal subordinate.” It is a branch-specific alliance constructed because Bai needs Otto's False God methodology.

PROMETHEUS is central to the workaround. Recovered prose says she can only influence Honkai in limited ways, but those “limited” actions include keeping the Current-Era Herrscher sequence aligned with the Previous Era, creating a back door for Finality power, and leaving routes into higher Honkai mechanisms. She can also effectively approve Bai's **False God Plan** from inside the Honkai-side system.

The False God transformation gives Bai something qualitatively different from an ordinary Herrscher upgrade: **literally unbounded/infinite energy from the Imaginary Tree**, but at the cost of becoming the Tree's controlled slave. He becomes the Current Era's Binding Herrscher for the operation and then a False God linked to the Tree. This is **temporary operational power**, not a new permanent “infinite HW” baseline. Bai and Otto's problem is that someone must subsequently defeat this version of Bai and use Void's `虚数重整化` — **Imaginary Renormalization** — to turn the infinite state finite and break the Tree's control.

Because no ordinary person can realistically defeat him, Bai uses the **1,001 Domination cores** to create 1,001 Bai bodies/clones, each with his memories and will. He temporarily distributes his normal Herrscher authorities, Honkai-beast capabilities, stigmata and Divine Keys among these selves, cuts the clone cores off from his direct control, and leaves his central body principally carrying the False God state. This is why chapter 379's `伪神白歌vs千人白歌` is not “one Bai against a Herrscher who looks like Bai”; it is **False God Bai versus an army of self-derived Bai Ge instances**.

The battle is severe. The False God's Tree link continuously restores him, and the first attacks destroy large numbers of clones. The surviving Bai instances progressively inherit abilities from destroyed ones and learn the False God's attack patterns. Ultimately Judah, gravity, Taixu techniques and other restraint methods create the opening; Moonlight Throne fire temporarily empties his available Honkai energy; Void's Imaginary Renormalization converts the False God's `无限` into `有限`; then Shamash, Seven Thunders, the Sword of Domination, Star of Eden and other maximum-output Divine Keys shatter the False God body and Tree control. Bai deliberately **loses the infinite False God authority and regains freedom**.

Chapter 381 then sends him to the Imaginary Tree itself to “change the world.” In context, the operative purpose is to gain the temporal/causal route needed to go back and resolve Elysia's sacrifice problem. “Reached the Imaginary Tree” should not be inflated into “Bai permanently becomes ruler of all branches” or “all parallel timelines merge.” The False God authority has specifically been broken so that he can approach the Tree as himself.

**Chapters 382–385 — Bai takes Elysia's price onto himself, and Origin is reborn free.** Faloo's final sequence is chapter 382 **“Return to the Previous Era, Elysia's Resolve,”** chapter 383 **“For You, I Am Willing to Fall into Godhood — Herrscher of Origin,”** chapter 384 **“May the Pure-White One Bless Her,”** and chapter 385 **“Elysia: I Will Go to the Future to Find You!”** The recovered Chinese prose resolves the mechanism directly.

Bai's Current-Era detour gave him the **Herrscher of Domination** mechanism precisely because Domination can take or control another Herrscher's **authority without needing to extract an ordinary core**. Origin and Finality are special cases: their existence itself is the authority. Back at Elysia's decisive moment, Bai therefore does something much more radical than ordinary core seizure—he **completely takes the Origin authority from Elysia, leaving none of it connected to her**. This breaks the link that would make Elysia pay for the wish.

Bai then uses Origin himself. The fanfic explains Origin as a power that changes **fate/rules**, not primarily a destructive power. A sufficiently enormous wish demands the user's **life** as payment unless that user's existential level surpasses Honkai. Bai has already ascended to the False-God/Imaginary-Tree-apostle level, so the price destroys him more slowly than it would an ordinary being, but even his historical body cannot escape it. He successfully changes the rules so Herrschers—including Elysia herself—are freed from Honkai's restrictions. The perfect outcome is achieved, but Bai's historical body begins irreversibly breaking apart.

Before that body disappears, Bai **releases Domination and returns Origin authority to Elysia**. This is decisive for the end-state ledger: Bai does **not** end Volume Four retaining Elysia's Origin authority, and there is no ordinary removable “Origin core” to add to his body count. Because Bai's rule-changing wish has already liberated Herrschers, the returning Origin no longer rebinds Elysia to Honkai. Instead she obtains a **new, freed Origin form**, marked by white angel-like wings and a golden laurel/crown. Bai blesses this “rebirth” and kisses her. His historical body is still doomed, but he explains that this is not the death of his true existence: the system is preparing to return him to his original Current-Era body/timespace, where he will wait for her.

The farewell also exposes the hidden scale of Bai's suffering. Fenghuang Down shatters and releases the memories he had stored inside it. Elysia, Kevin, MEI, Aponia, Sakura, Kalpas, Hua, Mobius and the other Flame-Chasers see the accumulated history: **11 reincarnations/resets and 12 salvations**. Elysia's own sealed loop memories return as well. Bai explains that he never truly deleted those painful memories; he stored them because his active mind could not continuously carry the accumulated memories of more than ten thousand subjective years of repeated catastrophe. The same reveal has Elysia exclaim about a “fifty-thousand-year time road”; the safest reconciliation is that **50,000 years refers to the historical span Bai crossed between the Previous and Current Eras**, whereas Bai himself describes the repeatedly lived/reset road as **more than 10,000 subjective years**. The Fifteen Flame-Chasers respond as a family centered on him. Bai's request before departure is simply to embrace them all, because after the extraordinarily long time-road he has walked, he has missed them as much as they have missed him.

### Bai Ge's power and system progression

**Starting state.** Nothing in Volume Four should erase the Volume-Three ledger supplied with this project: Bai begins with the five old permanent **fragmentary** authorities — Void, Death, Flame, Wind, Thunder — and separately the five full physical/core slots best tracked at the previous endpoint as **Life/Death + Flame + Reason + Wind + Wind**. His previously established evolved strength around 15,000 HW remains the entry benchmark; defeating a Kevin-pattern puppet in Volume Three did not equate to defeating the real full-power Kevin.

**Full physical cores by chapter 318: seven.** Bai explicitly identifies himself as a `七核律者`, then lists **Reason, Void, Herrscher of Flamescion (薪炎), Life-and-Death, Azure Wind ×2, Ice**. This is the best hard checkpoint in Volume Four. The two Winds remain two physical cores. “Life/Death” is treated as a single combined core/state in this audit. Most importantly, the older fragmentary powers still must not be counted as additional full cores.

**Captured cores and Divine-Key cores are separate.** In the same scene Bai says he could become **twelve-core** if he removed the Herrscher cores from his Divine Keys and installed them in himself. That line is decisive evidence against a common MTL-summary error: a chapter saying Bai “gets,” “seizes,” “digs out,” or “takes” a core does not mean his bodily core count increments.

**Fusion-warrior progression.** Chapter 205 makes Bai a fusion warrior. By chapter 318 he describes himself with the fanfic rank **末法级**, best kept on first occurrence as **End-of-Law / apocalyptic-class (末法级) MANTIS**, rather than pretending this is a standard canon rank. The later Chimera/Tianyuan sequence upgrades him much further, and by the False God battle his underlying body is an **ultimate Chimera** plus `天元之人` state. Active Honkai Reaction remains a biological/transformation category, not an extra Herrscher core.

**Chimera and Honkai-beast abilities.** By chapter 379 Otto is confronted with an ability list containing **tens of thousands of Honkai-beast abilities**, MANTIS/Active Honkai Reaction, quantumization, basic time capability and the Tianyuan state. These are biological/learned/system-supported capabilities and should not be translated as thousands of Herrscher authorities.

**Stigmata and marks by chapter 379.** The explicit ability ledger given to Otto includes **Shariac stigmata, Kaslana stigmata, a Herrscher-of-Death stigmata**, and the **Salvation** and **Eternity** marks/signets. Those coexist with his cores, Divine Keys and MANTIS biology; they are not synonyms for one another.

**Twelve Herrscher authority types by the False God operation.** Otto's chapter-379 list describes Bai as having **twelve kinds of Herrscher authority** and **twelve Divine Keys**. Do **not** rewrite this as “twelve bodily Herrscher cores.” Earlier prose already tells us many relevant cores reside inside Keys or other containers. “Authority types,” “physical cores,” “captured cores,” “pseudo-cores,” and “Divine-Key cores” are distinct bookkeeping columns in this fanfiction.

**Domination.** In the parallel Current Era Bai deliberately seeks `支配`. Unlike his ordinary `夺取`, Domination can interfere with another Herrscher's authority **without requiring core theft**. The 1,001 Thousand/Domination cores are subsequently used as Bai-clone bodies during the False God plan. This is a functional capability and a stock of cores, not evidence that Bai crams 1,001 physical cores into himself.

**Binding in the Current-Era False God plan.** PROMETHEUS facilitates Bai becoming the Current Era's Eleventh/Binding Herrscher as part of his connection to Honkai and the Tree. He unquestionably **uses Binding authority** during the operation. The later system settlement settles Bai's **overall retained body state at seventeen cores**, but does not print a seventeen-item core-by-core list. Therefore future continuity should not ask “is Bai still seventeen-core?”—he is. It should also not mechanically declare this particular branch Binding manifestation to be one named slot among the seventeen unless a later chapter identifies it. The source itself does not provide that mapping here.

**False God.** The **Tree-controlled infinite-energy False God battle state** is temporary and deliberately broken. Connection to the Imaginary Tree supplies literal `无限` power but subjects Bai's actions to the Tree. The 1,001 Bai clones use Void-based Imaginary Renormalization plus Divine Keys and other restraint/attack methods to turn the infinite condition finite and shatter the Tree's direct control. However, the final Origin chapter still describes Bai as someone who has **ascended to False-God level and become an Imaginary Tree apostle**, and that elevated existential level is explicitly why the Origin-life price consumes him more slowly. Therefore do not carry “infinite energy” or Tree enslavement forward, but also do not erase the fact that his existential level has been raised by the False-God ascent.

**Origin.** This is now fully resolved for the Volume-Four endpoint. Bai temporarily **completely takes Elysia's Origin authority through Domination**, uses it to change Honkai's rules, and bears the fatal cost with his historical body. He then **returns Origin to Elysia before leaving**. Elysia—not Bai—therefore holds Origin at the end of the historical sequence, now in a newly liberated/evolved form. Do not add a permanent Origin core to Bai's bodily core count: Origin is explicitly an existence-authority special case, not an ordinary removable core.

**HW.** No later stable numerical HW value was recovered with enough confidence to supersede the approximately **15,000 HW** entry state supplied by the prior continuity. The False God's “infinite” Tree supply is a temporary qualitative condition and should not be converted into an HW statistic.

| **Original Current-Era Mei, Thunder Queen, Kiana/K-423, Bronya, White/Dark Seele, Himeko, Rita, Sirin, Cecilia and Bella** | They are **not the cast of chapters 371–381**. Their Volume-Three handoff state therefore remains the safe state until Bai actually returns to his own Current Era in Volume Five. In particular, nothing in the branch removes Mei/Thunder Queen's personality distinction, White/Dark Seele's chosen coexistence, K-423 HoV's separate identity, or the established romances. |

**Fire MOTH.** This is one of the largest institutional rewrites. By chapter 220 its leadership has effectively submitted to Bai's strategic authority; chapter 310 already names the **Fifteen Flame-Chasers**, and chapter 317 explicitly declares them **established**, not thirteen; by the final-pass material Fire MOTH is described as efficient, loyal and enormously well armed, with captured Herrschers, advanced Divine Keys, MANTIS/Chimera technology and Bai's central influence. The canon model of an increasingly fragmented, dying organization cannot be used as a shortcut.

**St. Freya and Anti-Entropy.** The St. Freya/Anti-Entropy people seen during the branch workaround are not automatically Bai's original organizations. Therefore Volume Four itself does not justify replacing the Volume-Three St. Freya bloc with the branch state. The original strengthened St. Freya remains the baseline waiting for Bai's proper return in Volume Five.

**Schicksal/Otto.** The distinction is even more important here. Branch Otto cooperates with Bai and formally announces his resignation/transfer of the Overseer position to Theresa as part of the False God event. Original Otto entered Volume Four dangerous, misinformed about the system, and incorrectly treating Bai's historical cognitive interference as something Sentience-like. Nothing in the branch-only alliance safely overturns that original-world ledger.

**World Serpent/Kevin.** The “Kevin went into the sea again” chapter belongs to the alternate-Current-Era interlude. It must not be used to say that original Current-Era Kevin's Volume-Three containment was independently reset. PE Kevin, meanwhile, now possesses years of rewritten shared history with Bai. When the original Current Era later incorporates the altered PE, Kevin's memories may become extremely important, but the exact propagation point lies beyond chapter 385.

**New/reworked organizations and spaces.** The two biggest are the formal **Fifteen Flame-Chasers** and Bai/Elysia's **Eternal Elysian Realm**. `白门` also appears as a recurring “Bai Sect” joke/social phenomenon, but the accessible evidence does not justify treating it as equivalent to Fire MOTH, Schicksal or World Serpent as a formal global political faction.

## Altered history, causal rules, and system functions established in Volume Four

**Elysia's childhood is genuinely rewritten.** Bai does not merely meet adult Elysia while history otherwise proceeds normally. He reaches her childhood, gives her the name **Elysia**, lives with her for years and becomes her most foundational human bond. Everything she later does in Fire MOTH now occurs after a childhood shaped by Bai.

**The system's historical intervention is not a disposable simulation.** Volume Four depends on Bai treating changes to the PE as changes to the history that generated his future. His problem is precisely how to preserve the causal good produced by Elysia's original sacrifice while deleting its fatal cost. The branch Current Era appears only because Bai breaks the normal return logic by jumping forward while the historical intervention is still active.

**History can become too contradictory to self-correct.** `万物休眠` allows Bai to reach the future while his system-time-travel state is still “in the past.” Because he returns to a later CE point than the one he originally departed and would otherwise coexist impossibly with his own historical absence/presence, a compatible parallel timeline is produced. This is not normal system completion; it is an exploit/side effect.

**The system itself is unique across parallel worlds.** The branch can produce another Bai Ge and can reproduce many of his historical choices, but it does **not** duplicate the system. The branch Bai therefore lacks the original's critical advantage and dies. The system-bearing Bai can inherit/share the branch counterpart's relevant memories, giving him local context without making the two people literally the same continuous body.

**Parallel-branch memories must not be assigned to original people.** Parallel Kiana mourning a dead Bai and branch Otto pledging loyalty are real events for those branch individuals. They are not proof that original Kiana has already mourned him or original Otto has already converted sides. This distinction is essential when Volume Five opens.

**`夺取` and `支配` are different mechanics.** Bai's normal seizure method takes the physical Herrscher core and can load it into his body or another vessel. Domination can operate on an authority without taking its ordinary core. This distinction exists specifically because the final Elysia problem cannot be solved with ordinary core extraction.

**Origin and Finality are exceptional.** The volume's prose treats them as cases where **existence itself equals authority**. Therefore neither should be handled using the normal “kill Herrscher → remove core → add core to inventory” template. This retroactively warns against literalizing chapter 332's “pull out Finality's core” gag/title.

**Core possession, core integration and authority access are three separate states.** Chapter 318 is the clean proof: Bai is seven-core while simultaneously possessing Divine Keys whose internal cores could make him twelve-core if extracted and installed. Chapter 379 can therefore truthfully describe twelve types of authority without making him a twelve-body-core Herrscher.

**PROMETHEUS has limited but causally enormous system-level access to Honkai.** She cannot simply command everything, but can influence Herrscher sequencing, leave a Finality back door and facilitate the False God plan. This explains why future/current Herrscher history can be made to resemble PE sequencing without treating PROMETHEUS as omnipotent.

**False God is an exploit, not an evolution endpoint.** Bai borrows unbounded Imaginary-Tree power in order to reach the Tree, knowingly accepts temporary enslavement, and prepares the 1,001 selves to defeat him. The system/strategy therefore distinguishes **power acquisition** from **power worth keeping**. Infinite energy that costs free will is intentionally thrown away.

**The PE contains explicit full-history resets.** The Time Hourglass sends the entire historical run back to its starting point when flipped. Chapter 344 reaches ten resets and states that almost every attempt still ends with Elysia sacrificing herself to free future Herrschers. The final memory release upgrades the count to **11 resets/reincarnations and 12 salvations**. Bai retains the continuity of those experiences even when he periodically offloads their memories into Fenghuang Down. Later Herrscher reappearances should therefore be read as events in later runs where appropriate, not accidental duplicate encounters in one linear history.

**Several canonical deaths are genuinely erased or functionally prevented.** Hua is explicitly rescued in the Third Eruption; Rin remains alive through Corruption; Sakura therefore avoids the same tragedy chain; Klein appears alive late in the volume; Pardo is explicitly promised survival; Elysia's fatal Origin sacrifice is avoided; the official Faloo description itself advertises **all Flame-Chasers surviving**. That does not mean every canon scene simply vanishes — many events recur in altered form — but the death outcomes are not to be restored by translator assumption.

**Elysia's miracle remains causally necessary, but Elysia no longer pays for it.** Bai never solves the problem by deleting Origin's historical function. The future still requires the rule change that lets Herrschers retain humanity. His final solution changes **the user and payer**: Domination severs Origin from Elysia, Bai makes the wish himself, his historical body pays the life-price, and only afterward is the now-freed Origin authority returned to Elysia.

**Therefore Bai's earlier “Elysia isn't dead” statement is vindicated as a statement about mutable history.** It never required a secret physically living Elysia already waiting somewhere in 2014 before this intervention. Volume Four creates the historical conditions under which her death ceases to be inevitable.

## Immediate post-return system settlement: what Volume Four actually leaves Bai Ge with

Although Faloo places Bai's proper Current-Era return at the opening of Volume Five, the **system settlement screen is the direct conversion of the Volume-Four traversal into permanent rewards**. For future continuity work it is far more useful to record that screen here as **immediate later-confirmed handoff** than to force every future volume to reopen the first post-return chapter.

This settlement resolves several power-state uncertainties that cannot be solved from chapter 385 alone.

### The PE clear receives an SSSSS evaluation

The system's evaluation is not merely “Bai saved Elysia.” It grades multiple independent objectives that summarize what the successful final history achieved:

- **all Fifteen Flame-Chasers survive**;
- the major people associated with them survive;
- Bai defeats every Herrscher other than Origin in the relevant evaluation sense;
- **Previous-Era human survival exceeds 90%**;
- **Elysia/Origin survives while Current-Era Herrschers still retain humanity**;
- both the **Previous Era and Current Era continue to exist**, rather than one being sacrificed to preserve the other;
- Bai joins/creates the Flame-Chaser fellowship;
- he reaches and assaults the **Imaginary Tree**;
- he becomes a **False God** during the workaround;
- he subordinates/co-opts the parallel world's Otto;
- he saves the paradox-generated parallel world;
- he preserves/saves Previous-Era Herrschers instead of reducing all of them to dead cores;
- PE casualties are driven below an extraordinarily low threshold in the optimized history;
- planetary-class Honkai threats are cleared;
- even the Herrscher of Finality becomes a personal relationship/interaction rather than a faceless final extinction event.

The rating is therefore useful evidence for the **successful final history**, not just a joke score. It tells future translators which outcomes the system itself recognizes as part of the clear.

### Permanent completion state: seventeen-core Herrscher

The most important post-clear line is explicit:

> **`自身实力完全保留（十七核律者+究极奇美拉+天元之人）`**

In other words, the system retains Bai's own developed strength as:

- **Seventeen-core Herrscher (`十七核律者`)**
- **Ultimate Chimera (`究极奇美拉`)**
- **Tianyuan Human / Person of Tianyuan (`天元之人`)**

This supersedes the temptation to use chapter 318's **seven-core** self-audit as the final Volume-Four number. Seven cores is a valuable **mid-volume hard checkpoint**; **seventeen cores is the immediate post-clear retained state**.

The novel does not provide, in the currently recovered settlement passage, one clean seventeen-line inventory naming every one of those integrated cores. Future work therefore should **not re-open the question of whether Bai is really seventeen-core**—that count is settled by the system—but it also should not fabricate a precise seventeen-name list from inference. The safe hierarchy is:

1. **chapter 318:** seven bodily cores explicitly enumerated;
2. additional PE cores/Divine-Key/core resources and later-loop acquisitions continue to accumulate;
3. **post-clear system settlement:** Bai's retained developed body is explicitly **seventeen-core**.

This is a source-level omission of the individual seventeen-name enumeration, not a continuity hole in the summary. The authoritative final **count and category are known**.

### Residual transcendent authorities retained

The settlement separately awards:

- **Residual False-God Authority (`残·伪神权柄`)**
- **Residual Origin Authority (`残·始源权能`)**
- **Minute/Trace Finality Power (`微·终焉之力`)**

These three phrases resolve a subtle distinction that future summaries must preserve.

**False God:** Bai does not retain the enslaved Imaginary-Tree state with literal infinite energy. That battle shell is intentionally defeated. What survives is a **reduced residual authority** produced by the system's completion conversion.

**Origin:** Bai returns **full Origin** to Elysia before leaving the PE. He therefore does not steal Elysia's complete Origin existence. Separately, the system converts his achievement/experience into **residual Origin authority** for Bai.

**Finality:** Bai does not possess a normal Finality core. Finality is a special existence-authority. The reward is only a **minute amount of Finality power**, and the same settlement material notes that Bai also receives **time resistance** from Finality.

Thus a future translator can safely write:

> **Elysia owns full liberated Origin; Bai owns only a system-granted residual Origin authority. Bai is not full Finality, but carries a minute Finality trace and Finality-derived time resistance.**

### All Flame-Chaser signets are retained

The settlement awards **all Flame-Chaser signets/marks (`逐火英桀全部刻印`)**.

This is broader than the earlier Volume-Three/early-Volume-Four ledger, which only clearly tracked selected marks such as Salvation and Eternity. After the PE clear, Bai should be treated as carrying the **complete Flame-Chaser signet package** produced by the rewritten fifteen-member fellowship.

The system also grants affinity-derived personalized rewards. The explicitly visible examples are:

| Source person | Reward | Practical meaning in the settlement text |
|---|---|---|
| **Elysia** | **Flawless True Self (`无瑕真我`)** | **Charm +1000%** |
| **Eden** | **Golden (`黄金`)** | Comically realized as a **bank card / money resource** |
| **Aponia** | **Discipline (`戒律`)** | **Word-spirit / command (`言灵`)** capability |
| **Sakura** | **Setsuna (`刹那`)** | **Speed increase** |
| **Pardofelis** | **Empty Dreams (`空梦`)** | **Luck +10000** |
| **Griseo** | **Stars (`繁星`)** | **Painting skill MAX + ability to see through essence** |

The system interface continues beyond the visible portion, so these examples are **not an exhaustive list of every affinity reward**. Later prose explicitly cites other personal rewards such as **Vill-V's self/personality-division capability** as belonging to the same affinity-based reward class.

This is the correct way to handle the incompleteness: future work does **not** need to rediscover whether such a reward system exists or whether the visible six are the entire list. The settled rule is:

> **The PE settlement grants the entire developed combat state, all Flame-Chaser signets, and a long additional list of affinity-derived abilities/items; the prose only prints representative entries before the interface continues off-screen.**

### Fate of the three traversal traits after the clear

The three temporary PE traits do **not** all have the same post-clear fate.

- **Miracle** is explicitly retained. Because Bai never used it, the system converts it into a permanent skill/reward. Later Bai can consciously consider spending `奇迹`.
- **Eternal Heart** should **not** be carried as permanently retained. Later prose explicitly contrasts a later Bai with his PE self by noting that he **no longer has Eternal Heart**.
- **Person of Reincarnation** is not separately named on the immediate completion reward screen. The safe state is therefore **traversal-only/reset trait, not part of Bai's normal post-PE permanent kit**, unless a later system upgrade or new traversal explicitly grants an equivalent function.

This distinction prevents a particularly damaging future error: Bai's enormous later time powers do **not** mean he permanently carries the exact Volume-Four `轮回之人` hourglass trait, and his later emotional vulnerability does not contradict Volume Four because **Eternal Heart is no longer active**.

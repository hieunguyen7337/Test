# *People in Honkai Impact 3rd, Be Their Heroes* — Volume Three Full Story and Continuity Guide

## Scope, sources, and the exact volume boundary

The work discussed here is **《人在崩坏三，当她们的英雄》**, written by **黄金森林** (Huangjin Senlin / “Golden Forest”), commonly rendered in English as ***People in Honkai Impact 3rd, Be Their Heroes***. This guide is written for continuity-sensitive translation rather than as a loose plot synopsis: it records what actually changes in the fanfiction’s timeline, which relationships and powers remain active afterward, who knows which secrets, and which apparent canon events now occur for completely different reasons.

For chapter order and volume boundaries, **Faloo is treated as authoritative** because it is the publication source. Chinese mirror text such as **TRXS / Fan Novel Network** is used for chapter prose where Faloo material is difficult to access, while WTR-LAB is useful only as a secondary machine-translation/search aid. Mirror webpage numbers are not reliable substitutes for Faloo chapter numbers. Established English *Honkai Impact 3rd* names are used where the correspondence is clear, but the fanfiction’s own altered lore and terminology take priority over canon normalization.

The boundary is unambiguous:

**Volume Three = chapters 100–172, inclusive.**

Faloo places **“第三卷 / Volume Three”** before chapter 100, **“加入圣芙蕾雅学园，跟五女同居”** (“Joining St. Freya Academy, Living Together with Five Girls”), and places **“第四卷，前文明 / Volume Four, Previous Era”** immediately after chapter 172. Volume Four therefore begins with chapter 173, whose Raven-centered island-revenge joke directly follows a disaster caused near the end of Volume Three.

The major structure of Volume Three is:

|Story phase|Chapters|Central development|

A translator beginning Volume Four should understand one large shift immediately: **Volume Three turns Bai Ge from an unusually powerful individual surrounded by a small found family into the central military, medical, political, and emotional anchor of a new St. Freya power bloc.** By chapter 172, many tragedies that canon would treat as future crises—Himeko’s deterioration, Wendy’s abandonment, Seele’s imprisonment, Kiana’s Herrscher awakening, and the separation from Sirin/Cecilia/Bella—have already been rewritten or redirected.

## The causal architecture of Volume Three

Volume Three is much easier to misread if it is treated as a list of rescues and power-ups. Its actual structure is a **positive feedback loop**:

> **Bai Ge saves an individual → turns the one-off rescue into a reusable capability → embeds that capability inside St. Freya → the stronger institution gives him the leverage to save the next person and challenge Otto on better terms.**

This is the volume in which Bai stops being only a powerful anomaly moving between tragedies and becomes the center of a functioning alternative order.

### 1. Exact inherited state from Volume Two

The starting point must include **all** of the permanent Volume-Two rewards, not only the older five fragments.

Entering chapter 100, Bai Ge already permanently possesses:

| Category | Inherited state entering Volume Three | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| **Five old fragmentary authorities** | Void, Death, Flame, Wind, Thunder | Reduced authorities retained from the rewritten Second Eruption. They are not five full Current-Era physical cores. |
| **Complete Quantum authority** | Full control gained through Bai's successful X-10 substitution | Explains why the Sea-of-Quanta rescue could succeed and remains part of Bai's permanent movement/space toolkit afterward. |
| **Initial Time authority** | Genuine combat/physical time manipulation | Already used against Kevin during the chapter-99 escape; distinct from the system's historical-travel functions. |
| **Infinite Evolution Body** | Continuously developing physical body | One reason Bai's raw strength, endurance and regeneration can keep rising even before later full cores are added. |
| **Herrscher-of-Death stigmata** | Added to the earlier Kaslana and Schariac components | By this point Bai's fused-stigmata body should be understood as **Kaslana + Schariac + Death/Seele-related**, not merely the older two-lineage package. |
| **Schariac holy blood** | Extremely strong anti-Honkai blood property | Volume Three converts it from inherited resistance into a programmable medical/anti-Honkai technology. |
| **Kaslana constitution / absolute Honkai adaptability** | Exceptional body and Honkai compatibility | Makes later core use, regeneration and Divine-Key output survivable. |
| **Project Bunny 19C compatibility/control authority** | System reward from the second traversal | Helps explain why Bai can repair, command, redesign and integrate Project Bunny so naturally without replacing Bronya as its real partner. |
| **Heavenly Voice and other noncombat rewards** | Permanent but secondary | These remain part of Bai's system package even when not strategically central. |
| **Late-Volume-Two energy benchmark** | More than roughly **4,500 HW** tolerance | This is the baseline from which Volume Three later climbs toward roughly 15,000 HW. |

The chapter-100 Bai is therefore **not** merely “five fragmentary Herrscher powers plus good genetics.” He already combines fragmentary authorities, complete Quantum, basic Time, an evolution body, multiple stigmata lineages and extreme Honkai compatibility.

### 2. Volume Three mostly grows Bai through the world, not through a new historical run

Unlike Volumes One, Two and Four, Volume Three does **not** center on a new Past Simulator traversal with a fresh temporary-trait package and settlement screen.

The system remains important, but its role is quieter:

- it continues **affinity tracking**;
- Kiana reaches **MAX affinity**, which matters because targeted historical traversal is affinity-gated;
- its existing **temporal-identity protection** continues preventing ordinary people from naturally linking present teenage Bai to his historical manifestations;
- the permanent rewards from earlier traversals remain active;
- but most new Volume-Three growth comes from **physical cores, Divine Keys, scientific research, training, institutional authority, relationships and captured technology**.

This distinction matters for future power bookkeeping. A new ability appearing in Volume Three should not automatically be called “a system reward.”

### 3. Personal rescue becomes reusable infrastructure

The Himeko arc establishes the pattern most clearly.

Bai does not stop at:

> **Himeko is sick → Bai heals Himeko.**

Instead the chain becomes:

1. Himeko's accumulated Honkai damage exposes the limits of ordinary Valkyrie medicine.
2. Dr. Maki brings **HSN-b46** and the **Gem of Serenity**.
3. Bai obtains a full Life-and-Death state.
4. That full authority lets him precisely amplify and redesign the anti-Honkai properties of his Schariac holy blood.
5. Maki's serum research + Bai's modified holy blood becomes the basis for a stronger anti-Honkai treatment line.
6. The goal changes from “save Himeko” to **“make severe Honkai corrosion, artificial-stigmata damage and even some deadwalker transformations treatable conditions.”**
7. St. Freya can now train and deploy Valkyries under a radically different risk model.
8. Later, Theresa formalizes this institutional role by making Bai **St. Freya's medical chief (`医务长`)**.

The strategic consequence is enormous: St. Freya's Valkyries no longer need to treat crippling injury or Honkai erosion as necessarily career-ending or fatal. Their battlefield doctrine can become:

> **Stay alive long enough to return to Bai Ge.**

That is why Bai's healing ability is a political and military asset, not merely a character power.

### 4. Reason turns individual strength into scalable military power

Bai's Reason acquisition follows the same pattern.

The important chain is:

1. Otto comes to St. Freya expecting to manipulate Bai.
2. Bai refuses Otto's home-field advantage because he remembers exactly how Otto used hostages and terrain against him in 2000.
3. The confrontation therefore occurs inside Bai's growing St. Freya support network.
4. Bai outmaneuvers Otto and extracts assets/concessions rather than entering Schicksal as a controllable subordinate.
5. **Otto himself supplies Vermilion Knight: Eclipse / the Blank Key**—a decision that later becomes part of Otto's “I brought this on myself” frustration.
6. The Blank Key can mount otherwise unusable Previous-Era Herrscher cores.
7. Bai gains access to **Void Archives / the Wisdom Gem**, then loads the Previous-Era Reason core through the Blank Key.
8. He becomes a genuine Reason user without stripping Welt of his Current-Era core.
9. Welt remains alive and becomes Bai's teacher in how to use Reason correctly.
10. Reason then scales outward into pseudo-Divine Keys, Project Bunny weapon systems, Titans, floating gun batteries and infrastructure.

Thus Reason matters less because it gives Bai “one more element” and more because it lets a single fighter become an **industrial and logistical base**.

### 5. The House of the Hearth becomes a St. Freya institution

The House relocation is not a casual move.

The causal chain is:

1. Bai's renewed relationship with the now-mature Bronya reminds him that the House of the Hearth still physically exists and that the girls he raised remain part of his real rewritten history.
2. Bai returns with Bronya and Seele rather than leaving that family geographically separated.
3. He asks the girls whether they want to move to St. Freya and promises that they will be safe with him.
4. Theresa learns that the House contains **dozens of girls with unusually strong Honkai resistance** and enthusiastically accepts them as potential students/future Valkyries.
5. Bai does not merely pack luggage. He uses gravity/Reason-like capability to lift the **entire orphanage building**, opens a route with his fragmentary Void authority and stores the building itself inside Imaginary space for relocation.
6. Hyperion is used to move the girls in style and safety.
7. At St. Freya, Bai uses Life-and-Death authority to implant artificial stigmata with vastly reduced pain and corrosion risk.
8. The House therefore becomes both **family reunited** and a new, safer Valkyrie personnel pipeline.

This is another personal relationship becoming institutional capacity.

### 6. Public history and true history deliberately diverge

Volume Three repeatedly creates situations in which outside factions are acting on a **manufactured explanation**.

The Wendy operation is the clearest case:

1. Wendy has been crippled by the Gem of Desire and believes her suffering may be justified if it serves humanity.
2. Bai rejects the premise that her destruction is an acceptable institutional cost.
3. He heals Wendy and removes the Gem of Desire.
4. Merely making Wendy vanish would tell Otto that someone intervened and invite pursuit.
5. Bai therefore rewrites his own cells and sex until he is physically indistinguishable from Wendy.
6. He stages the expected “Wind Herrscher awakened and destroyed the Oceania branch” event while a storm blocks reliable observation.
7. The real Wendy is secretly transported to St. Freya.
8. “Wendy” then visibly flees far beyond normal pursuit, fixing the false story in Schicksal intelligence.
9. Bai uses the operation to reach the Fourth Divine Key satellite and steal its Previous-Era Wind core.
10. He later uses quantumization/stealth to prevent observers from tracing the false Wendy back to himself.

The world therefore contains two simultaneous truths:

- **public truth:** Wendy became the Wind Herrscher and escaped;
- **actual truth:** Wendy is healed at St. Freya, while Bai staged the entire incident and took both Wind assets.

Future translation must preserve which characters know which version.

### 7. Relationships change command structure and strategic allegiance

Volume Three's romance is not detachable “harem filler.” Several relationships directly affect faction state.

**Rita** is the clearest example:

1. She begins as Otto-associated S-rank personnel whose specialties include infiltration and assassination.
2. Because allowing such an operative to roam St. Freya unsupervised is absurd, she is placed under **Bai's direct household supervision**.
3. Rita accepts the arrangement and increasingly frames herself as Bai's maid, calling him **Master**.
4. Daily cohabitation and Bai's treatment of her shift the maid role from operational cover/assignment into a genuine personal identity and attachment.
5. She independently chooses him emotionally and kisses him.
6. Otto can later force her **body** to attack Bai only by exploiting a Godsbane-armor backdoor.
7. Her immediate horror after the forced attack proves that institutional control and personal loyalty have completely diverged.

Thus the correct causal description is:

> **Otto places an infiltrator in Bai's orbit → proximity converts the assignment into genuine loyalty → Otto's remaining hardware control can move Rita's body but cannot restore her allegiance.**

### 8. Otto increasingly loses because his model of Bai is wrong

Otto remains dangerous, but Volume Three changes the information war.

He makes several consecutive errors:

- he thinks St. Freya can still be treated as a subordinate institution;
- he hands over tools Bai converts into stronger Herrscher states;
- he does not know Bai's complete core inventory;
- he does not know Wendy's incident is staged;
- he does not understand the system's identity protection;
- he interprets cognitive interference as a hypothetical Sentience power;
- he tries to reproduce Second-Eruption trauma around K-423 without understanding that K-423's Gem of Freedom is causally descended from **Bai**, not Sirin.

The late fission-missile plot is therefore not random escalation. Otto deliberately tries to **recreate the psychological shape of 2000**—extreme danger, threatened loved ones, Herrscher awakening—because he thinks the historical pattern can be weaponized to awaken K-423.

Instead the plan exposes the thing Otto has misunderstood most badly: the new HoV inherits the memory/will of Bai's historical core and therefore awakens emotionally oriented **toward Bai**.

### 9. The K-423 HoV crisis is the endpoint of a causal chain begun in Volume One

The new HoV should never be introduced as a surprise duplicate of canon Sirin.

The chain is:

1. Bai replaces Sirin as the historical Herrscher of the Void in 2000.
2. Otto obtains Bai-derived Herrscher material/cores after the rewritten Second Eruption.
3. K-423 is still created because history preserves her existence through a new causal route.
4. Her Herrscher component therefore comes from **Bai's historical HoV material**.
5. The Gem of Freedom carries memory/will information from its previous bearer.
6. Otto's late crisis forces that core to generate a new personality.
7. The personality receives Bai-linked memories: torture, sacrifice, protecting enemies, saving Kiana, and repeatedly suffering for other people.
8. She does not conclude “humans hurt me, therefore humanity should die.”
9. She concludes **“the world keeps hurting Bai Ge; therefore the world does not deserve him.”**
10. Her destructive impulse is an apocalyptic form of devotion to Bai, not obedience to Honkai.
11. Bai resolves the crisis by redirecting the attachment rather than killing the personality.

This is the volume's strongest proof that **a Herrscher personality's origin, memory inheritance and emotional relationships determine its allegiance more strongly than the label 'Herrscher.'**

### 10. The 2000 family returns because Bai's old promise and authority reappear

Cecilia, Sirin and Bella do not materialize simply because the plot wants a reunion.

Bai had told the surviving 2000 family that he would return roughly fifteen years later.

During the K-423 HoV crisis, the **Gem of Freedom manifests the historical Void authority/signature connected to Bai**. Bella has spent approximately fifteen years waiting for the promised reunion. She senses the familiar authority and comes expecting that Bai has finally returned in the form she remembers.

Instead she finds K-423's newly awakened HoV trying to claim “Benares.”

That is why Bella's rejection matters so much:

> **“Benares” may be what Herrscher lore says she should be, but Bai gave her the identity Bella, a human form, pseudo-cores, family and freedom.**

Cecilia and Sirin are physically present as part of the same returning Second-Eruption family network. Their arrival is therefore the convergence of:

- Bai's fifteen-year promise,
- rewritten history that let them survive,
- the reappearance of a familiar historical Herrscher signature,
- and the late HoV crisis.

### 11. The system's cognitive identity protection has a precise scope

The system does **not** erase rewritten history.

People who lived through Bai's past interventions still possess those historical memories. What the system suppresses is the **cognitive link**:

> **“the present teenage Bai Ge standing before me = the historical Bai Ge I met in another era.”**

Characters can still:

- remember the historical person;
- notice that present Bai feels strangely familiar;
- see visual similarities;
- possess documents and recordings of him.

But their cognition is biased away from completing the identity match unless enough evidence breaks through.

Otto eventually defeats that protection not because the system stops working, but because he accumulates a uniquely strong evidence chain. Bai again intercepts fission missiles in exactly the self-sacrificial way the 2000 Herrscher did. Otto retrieves old footage, compares behavior and appearance, and finally forces the contradiction into conscious recognition.

Even then he cannot perceive the **system**. He therefore invents an in-world explanation: Bai must possess a Sentience-like cognition-manipulation authority.

Bai knows this is wrong and deliberately lets Otto keep the bad model.

This rule should be carried into Volume Four and beyond:

> **historical memory persists; temporal identity linkage is protected; sufficiently strong evidence can break through; observers who break through still do not automatically learn that a system exists.**

## Seele returns, Bai Ge enters St. Freya, and Kevin remains outside the household

### The Sea-of-Quanta rescue actually succeeds

Volume Three opens from the escape that closes chapter 99. The safest end-state is no longer merely “Bai Ge reached Seele.” **He successfully brings Seele out of the Sea of Quanta and back into the Current Era.** During part of the escape, Dark Seele is the personality at the front; Bai Ge recognizes the difference in demeanor and the way she interlocks her fingers with his.

Kevin attempts to prevent the escape. Bai Ge responds by stacking several kinds of acceleration: Wind, Thunder/electromagnetic acceleration, and even a form of **time acceleration** applied to himself. His final distraction is not physical power but information. He shocks Kevin with the declaration that **Elysia is not dead**, exploiting the one name capable of breaking Kevin’s concentration long enough for Bai Ge and Seele to pass through the quantum route.

This is the first important handoff from Volume Two: **Seele is no longer missing once Volume Three properly begins.** White Seele and Dark Seele still share one body, but both return to the world with Bai Ge and become members of his St. Freya household.

The “Elysia isn’t dead” claim should be carried literally without prematurely redefining it as “an Elysian Realm data copy is alive.” Volume Three never resolves it in that narrow way. Instead, the story immediately continues to treat Bai Ge’s ability to reach and change the Previous Era as the decisive variable, and Volume Four is explicitly the **Previous Era** volume centered on his encounter with Elysia. The statement functions as a promise that Elysia’s known death is not an immutable endpoint from Bai Ge’s perspective.

### Chapter 100: the five-girl St. Freya household

Chapter 100 formalizes the new status quo. Bai Ge joins **St. Freya Academy** and moves into the principal household with the five women referred to by the chapter title:

* **Raiden Mei**
* **Kiana / K-423**
* **Bronya Zaychik**
* **Seele Vollerei**
* **Murata Himeko**

Theresa and Fu Hua are important members of Bai Ge’s St. Freya circle, but they are not the “five girls” of the chapter title.

The early St. Freya chapters deliberately slow down into domestic comedy because Bai Ge has spent much of the previous two volumes repeatedly living entire alternate histories and nearly dying for people. The ordinary routine matters. He eats with Mei, deals with Kiana’s physical affection, spends time with Bronya and Seele, and begins training for the command role he wants aboard Hyperion.

By chapter 107, approximately **two months** have passed since his entry into St. Freya. His normal routine has become highly structured: mornings are devoted to battleship-captain training, afternoons often include sparring with Fu Hua, and the remaining time is divided among the household. He plays games with Bronya, shops with Seele, spends affectionate time with Kiana, and is already in an established romantic and sexual relationship with Mei. Among the Valkyries, the only prominent young man in the academy becomes an object of fascination and receives the “white prince of St. Freya” style of nickname.

### Welt recognizes the impossible survivor

Welt Yang’s reaction in chapter 104 is not simply because Bai Ge resembles someone from history. Welt **personally met the Second-Eruption Bai Ge** in the rewritten 2000 timeline. Seeing essentially the same person alive and young in the Current Era is therefore like seeing a ghost.

The encounter turns into politics rather than hostility. Welt and Bai Ge already share an enemy in Otto, and by chapter 106 Welt agrees to **leave his St. Freya teaching position and return to leadership of Anti-Entropy**, restoring a stronger institutional Anti-Entropy that can cooperate with Bai Ge and Theresa. Bai Ge even treats Welt as a family-adjacent ally rather than a remote faction leader, asking him for HOMU/Arahato merchandise for Bronya and discussing Bronya as someone highly suited to the Herrscher-of-Reason legacy.

This point is important later: **Welt remains alive and retains his own Current-Era identity after Bai Ge becomes a Herrscher of Reason.** Bai Ge’s later Reason power is not obtained by killing Welt or permanently stripping Welt of his Current-Era core.

### Kevin does not become a normal ally

Bai Ge’s chapter-98 “friend or enemy” choice does not instantly recruit Kevin. Volume Three repeatedly frames Kevin as still **contained/separated in the Sea of Quanta** rather than freely returning to World Serpent. Chapter 105 jokes that Bai Ge has effectively sentenced him to “life imprisonment,” and later chapters continue the antagonistic comedy about Kevin having spent more than fifteen centuries “at sea” and about Bai Ge’s instant-noodle provocation.

Bai Ge keeps using impossible future/Previous-Era knowledge against him. The most explicit later shock is the information that **Griseo was captured by Sa**. Kevin increasingly distrusts Bai Ge as a source because the information is too outrageous, yet he also cannot dismiss a man who repeatedly knows things Kevin himself does not.

The correct end-state is therefore: **Kevin is neither a settled enemy nor an ordinary member of Bai Ge’s alliance.** He is dangerous, contained, negotiable, and increasingly dependent on Bai Ge for information about people from the Previous Era.

### Cocolia is permanently removed as an active antagonist

The old uncertainty over whether Cocolia was merely “off-stage” can be resolved. Bai Ge does not leave her free to resume the Nagazora strategy. He states that he subjected her to a **“punishment of a thousand deaths” (千死之罚)** and then **permanently imprisoned her in a place no one else can find**.

That means the Cocolia-dependent canon plot is not waiting in reserve. She is alive in the technical sense that Bai Ge has imprisoned her rather than simply erasing her, but she is **fully neutralized as an active political driver** by the early St. Freya phase. This is why several original Cocolia-centered routes can be skipped or repurposed afterward.

## Himeko’s tragedy is broken: the Gem of Serenity and the Herrscher of Life and Death

### Himeko is hospitalized

The domestic calm breaks when Theresa reports that **Himeko has been hospitalized**. The problem is the accumulated cost of living as a Valkyrie with severe Honkai exposure and artificial-stigmata-related damage—the same biological trajectory that, in the game’s original history, makes Himeko’s future tragically short.

For Bai Ge, this is exactly the sort of “fixed tragedy” he entered the world to overthrow. The key difference from earlier volumes is that he now possesses enough biological authority to attack the problem directly rather than merely comfort the victim.

### Dr. Maki, HSN-b46, and the Gem of Serenity

The Himeko crisis brings **Dr. Maki (玛基博士)** into Bai Ge’s orbit. Maki possesses **HSN-b46**, a powerful anti-Honkai serum, and brings the **Gem of Serenity (静谧宝石)**.

HSN-b46 can neutralize Honkai energy and repair damaged tissue, but its ordinary form has a serious drawback for Valkyries: it can destroy or neutralize the Honkai-dependent systems on which artificial stigmata and Valkyrie combat capability rely, effectively saving the patient by stripping away the very power that made her a Valkyrie.

Bai Ge obtains the Gem of Serenity and reaches a much more complete state than the fragmentary Death authority he carried out of his first simulator run. The story now explicitly treats him as the **Herrscher of Life and Death (生死之律者)**. The wording matters. This is not merely the old “fragmentary Herrscher of Death authority” from Volume One. Volume Three gives him a full-scale Current-Era life/death core and greatly expands what he can do with cellular reconstruction, regeneration, morphology, healing, and body creation.

Maki himself is suffering from Honkai erosion. Bai Ge cures him, but refuses to let Maki treat death as a convenient escape from responsibility for earlier human experimentation. Instead he gives him an atonement condition: **save one million lives** and stop conducting the kind of human experiments that produced the tragedy in the first place.

### Schariac holy blood becomes a medical technology

The Gem of Serenity amplifies Bai Ge’s ability to manipulate his own inherited **Schariac holy blood**. In Volume One, holy blood was mainly a powerful anti-Honkai trait inherited through the simulator reward. In Volume Two, Bai Ge already learned to modify its anti-Honkai properties when curing Bronya.

Volume Three pushes the idea much further. With full Life-and-Death authority, Bai Ge can actively optimize the blood’s behavior. Even a tiny quantity can erase or neutralize surrounding Honkai energy on a remarkable scale. By combining the logic of Schariac holy blood with Maki’s HSN-b46 research, Bai Ge and the people around him develop the idea jokingly framed as **“Godslayer Spear Pro Max.”**

The practical goal is an anti-Honkai treatment that does **not** simply save a Valkyrie by turning her back into an ordinary human. It should remove Honkai corrosion, repair tissue, preserve combat capability, and eventually become strong enough that even severe deadwalker/zombie-like transformations may be reversible if enough biological information remains.

Himeko is therefore not merely “temporarily stabilized.” **Her canonical terminal trajectory has been broken.** St. Freya begins to treat Bai Ge as a general medical safety net: the first priority for wounded Valkyries becomes surviving long enough to return to him, because injuries and Honkai erosion that would once have ended a career—or a life—are increasingly reparable.

### Himeko becomes part of Bai Ge’s romantic circle

The medical rescue leads into a quieter emotional sequence. Himeko drinks privately with Bai Ge and exposes doubts about whether her path as a Valkyrie and teacher has amounted to anything meaningful. Bai Ge knows the version of Himeko who should have died protecting Kiana, so he refuses to let her dismiss herself as a failure or a woman who simply drank too much and wasted her life.

He tells her that what she has done matters, comforts her when she breaks down, and embraces her. Himeko later asks directly whether he likes her. **He says yes.**

Mei’s reaction is immediate and deliberately comedic—chapter 112 centers on her “I was here first” complaint—yet chapter 113 makes the outcome equally clear: **Mei chooses to accept the relationship rather than end her own.** Later Bai Ge thinks of Himeko plainly as one of “his women,” confirming that this was not a one-night emotional misunderstanding.

This is the first major demonstration in Volume Three that Bai Ge’s romantic network is going to become explicitly plural rather than remain an unresolved triangle.

## Bodies, alternate personalities, and Project Bunny

### Bai Ge can now rewrite living morphology almost at will

Chapters 114–116 initially play Bai Ge’s biological precision for comedy. He gives Kiana cat ears and a tail, and the gag later spreads through the Valkyrie dormitory. The underlying capability is serious: **Bai Ge can rewrite living tissue and physical morphology with extremely fine control.**

This is not illusion magic. Later, during the Wendy operation, the same authority lets him transform his own body at the cellular level into another person, including body proportions and sex. The catgirl comedy is therefore an early low-stakes demonstration of a power that becomes strategically decisive.

### Dark Seele is offered her own body

Bai Ge tells **Dark Seele** that he can create an independent body for her. White Seele does not interpret this as throwing away her other self; on the contrary, she wants Dark Seele to have the opportunity to live as her own person rather than remain permanently dependent on sharing one body.

Dark Seele declines **for now**, because White Seele still relies on her and she does not want to leave. She nevertheless keeps the option open and even treats the possibility of a future custom body as something Bai Ge owes her.

The correct end-of-Volume-Three state is therefore:

**White Seele and Dark Seele still share the same body by choice. Separation is physically possible for Bai Ge, but it has not happened.**

### Thunder Queen receives the same offer

Bai Ge raises the same possibility with **Thunder Queen Mei**, the distinct Herrscher/stigmata personality sharing Mei’s body. Thunder Queen is interested, while ordinary Mei is much less enthusiastic about giving the assertive alternate personality a separate body.

Bai Ge also explains the central technical limit: a body created through Life-and-Death authority does not automatically copy the original body’s **stigmata, Herrscher cores, or supernatural inheritance**. A newly created body could therefore leave Thunder Queen alive but without the power structure she currently uses.

No separation occurs in Volume Three. **Mei and Thunder Queen remain two distinguishable personalities in one body.**

### Project Bunny becomes increasingly autonomous

Project Bunny 19C also crosses a category boundary. It was previously Bronya’s combat partner/summon. In Volume Three it increasingly behaves like an independent personality and platform: it can speak, interact comedically with Bronya, and accept large packages of new equipment and transformations.

Later configurations include a stealth-fighter form as well as vehicle-like forms such as a supercar and motorcycle. Bai Ge’s Reason authority eventually turns Project Bunny into a terminal for a far larger constructed arsenal rather than merely Bronya’s original heavy weapon.

## Siegfried, Judgment of Shamash, Chi You, and Ji Lin

### Bai Ge meets Siegfried again and heals him

Siegfried re-enters the story through Anti-Entropy rather than as a remote missing father. He is spending time around **Raiden Ryoma**, and Bai Ge meets him directly.

This is a reunion with enormous hidden history behind it. Bai Ge personally fought Siegfried in 2000 while acting as the human-minded Second Herrscher. The system’s later cognitive interference prevents people from easily connecting the Current-Era Bai Ge to that historical Herrscher, so Siegfried does not simply begin the scene by treating the teenager before him as the same person.

Bai Ge uses Life-and-Death authority to **restore Siegfried’s damaged arm**, demonstrating again that “permanent” canon injuries are no longer reliable assumptions in this continuity.

### Shamash is borrowed, then entrusted to Kiana

Bai Ge gains operational access to **Judgment of Shamash (天火圣裁)** from Siegfried. His relationship to the weapon is important because he does not simply steal a Kaslana heirloom and declare it his own. He promises that it will ultimately be returned to a Kaslana.

During the Chi You sequence, Bai Ge uses the Divine Key at the kind of output associated with Kaslana self-sacrifice. His far greater regeneration and Herrscher compatibility make those attacks less suicidal for him than for an ordinary Kaslana.

After the later Otto confrontation, Bai Ge fulfills the spirit of his promise in a characteristically provocative way: **he gives the twin-pistol Judgment of Shamash to Kiana.** She is a Kaslana by identity and lineage material, and Bai Ge would rather trust her with it than simply hand it back to Siegfried.

Kiana also learns that Siegfried is alive and reachable. She does not abandon the home she has built at St. Freya to chase him immediately. Her relationship with Bai Ge, Mei, Bronya, Seele, and the academy has become a genuine present family rather than a temporary stop while she searches for her father.

### Chi You is destroyed

Chapters 120–122 move into the **Chi You / Ji Lin** conflict. Bai Ge escalates to overwhelming Shamash-class firepower and destroys Chi You.

The important outcome is not only victory over the beast. **Ji Lin returns to living existence.** The safest description is not “Bai Ge created a dead person ex nihilo from nothing.” Ji Lin still possesses a preserved/sealed supernatural and bodily substrate associated with the Chi You/Xuanyuan story. Bai Ge’s Life-and-Death ability restores/reawakens life in what remained.

For continuity purposes, however, the result is straightforward: **Ji Lin is alive after chapter 122.**

### Fu Hua reunites with Ji Lin

Bai Ge later returns toward St. Freya with Mei and Ji Lin. He is also carrying a **Cosmic Juggernaut (千界一乘) beacon** connected to the quantum/Previous-Era material.

Fu Hua’s reaction to Ji Lin is one of the volume’s strongest emotional payoffs. She had carried that ancient loss for millennia. Seeing Ji Lin alive forces even the normally controlled Fu Hua to break composure.

The restoration has wider meaning: Bai Ge is now not merely preventing future tragedies. He is beginning to return people whom the long-lived characters had already accepted as irretrievably lost.

### Kevin is given more impossible history

The same phase returns repeatedly to Kevin. Bai Ge tells him facts concerning **Griseo and Sa** that Kevin did not know, including the claim that Griseo was captured by Sa. Kevin increasingly treats Bai Ge as an infuriating liar because accepting the information means admitting that his own knowledge of Previous-Era survivors is incomplete.

Nevertheless, Kevin cannot simply ignore him. The pattern becomes important entering Volume Four: Bai Ge is the one Current-Era person capable of both **reaching the Previous Era through the system and weaponizing knowledge of that era against Kevin personally**.

## K-423’s true origin and Fu Hua’s choice

### Kiana is not biological Kiana

Volume Three makes the altered K-423 problem explicit rather than leaving it as the Volume-Two “maybe Bai Ge’s material was involved” joke.

Fu Hua reveals the central truth: the teenage Kiana is **K-423**, an artificial human produced by Otto using **Kaslana-family genetic material together with cells/material from the historical Herrscher of the Void**. Inside her lies the historical Void-Herrscher core identified by this fanfiction as the **Gem of Freedom (自由宝石)**.

In ordinary *Honkai Impact 3rd* history, that statement would point toward Sirin.

In this fanfiction’s rewritten history, it does not.

The historical Herrscher of the Void in 2000 was **Bai Ge**. Sirin never became the catastrophic Second Herrscher in the original manner because Bai Ge took that position. Otto therefore created K-423 using **Bai Ge-derived historical Herrscher cells/core material plus Kaslana material**.

This resolves the Volume-Two mystery. It also explains why Bai Ge’s joking “call Dad” language had a real causal basis without making him Kiana’s ordinary biological father. **K-423 is a laboratory-created composite, not a naturally conceived daughter.** Bai Ge is one of the genetic/Herrscher-material sources used to create her.

### “Kiana Kaslana” remains her real identity in the emotional sense

Bai Ge does not use the revelation to downgrade Kiana into “a clone” or “fake Kiana.” Chapter 127’s declaration that he will always love **Kiana Kaslana** is an identity statement as much as a romantic one.

Biological Kiana—the child Bai Ge saved in 2000 and whom the reader knows will become **Durandal**—is a different person. But K-423’s history, memories, relationships, choices, and place in the household are her own. Bai Ge treats “Kiana Kaslana” as the person she has become rather than a title that only biological Kiana is permitted to possess.

By the later volume, Kiana is able to continue living under that chosen identity rather than being psychologically erased by the K-423 revelation.

### Fu Hua breaks with Otto’s side

Fu Hua’s own institutional ambiguity also resolves. She admits that she had been acting as **Otto’s infiltrator inside St. Freya**, but by this point the rewritten Second Eruption, Bai Ge’s conduct, Ji Lin’s return, and Otto’s repeated manipulations have made that position untenable.

Fu Hua chooses to **leave Schicksal’s side and remain with St. Freya**.

She also begins teaching Bai Ge elements of **Taixuan Sword Qi / the Taixuan Sword God tradition** and gives him a **Fenghuang Down feather**. Her role becomes that of an ancient mentor and ally rather than Otto’s concealed asset.

This is a major institutional gain for Theresa: St. Freya now contains not only Bai Ge and human-aligned Herrschers, but Fu Hua acting on its side by choice.

## Otto comes to St. Freya: the Blank Key and Bai Ge’s Herrscher of Reason state

### Bai Ge reverses the Second-Eruption power dynamic

Otto wants to meet Bai Ge and initially expects the usual political geography: the interesting person comes to **Schicksal headquarters**, where Otto controls the surveillance, forces, exits, and hostages.

Bai Ge refuses.

His experience in 2000 taught him exactly how Otto wins confrontations. He therefore turns the invitation around and tells Otto to come to **St. Freya**, where Bai Ge has Theresa, Mei, Fu Hua, St. Freya’s Valkyries, allied Welt/Anti-Entropy support, and his own increasingly overwhelming power.

The change is symbolic. In the rewritten Second Eruption, Bai Ge was terrifyingly strong but vulnerable because the girls around him could be taken hostage. In Volume Three he has built an institution around those relationships. Otto can no longer isolate him from everyone he cares about.

### Vermilion Knight: Eclipse is the Blank Key route

An important terminology/power-system point is clarified here. **Vermilion Knight: Eclipse (真红骑士·月蚀)** is treated by this fanfiction as an application/form of the **Blank Key (空白之键)**, Previous-Era armor capable of accepting Herrscher cores and temporarily granting corresponding full Herrscher capability to the wearer.

Bai Ge obtains/possesses this armor through Otto’s side. It becomes the mechanism through which several of his later “full Herrscher” states can coexist with the five **fragmentary** authorities he already carries from the simulator.

This distinction is essential:

**Fragmentary authority ≠ physical/full core ≠ full Herrscher state mediated through the Blank Key.**

The novel sometimes casually counts all of these in “core” language, so a translator should follow the scene’s mechanics rather than flattening every reference into one inventory category.

### Void Archives and the Wisdom Gem give Bai Ge Reason

Chapter 131’s declaration **“At This Moment, I Am the Herrscher of Reason”** is genuine, but the mechanism is not Welt surrendering his Current-Era Reason core.

The causal chain begins with **Otto himself giving Bai the Previous-Era armor Vermilion Knight: Eclipse / the Blank Key** during the St. Freya confrontation. Bai later explicitly mocks Otto for this mistake: without the Blank Key, the dead/inert Previous-Era core associated with Void Archives would not be nearly as useful to him.

The Blank Key is the bridge because it can **mount a Herrscher core and let the wearer operate the corresponding full Herrscher state**. Bai therefore obtains **Void Archives (虚空万藏)** and accesses the Previous-Era Reason core associated with it, called here the **Wisdom Gem (智慧宝石)**, then loads that core through the Blank Key.

The result is a genuine **Herrscher-of-Reason state** without killing Welt or stealing Welt's Current-Era core.

This explains several later facts at once:

- Welt remains alive and politically active.
- Bai can ask Welt to **teach him how to use Reason properly**, including the principles behind Star of Eden.
- Bai can reproduce weapons and technological systems from understood structures.
- Project Bunny can become a terminal for much larger constructed arsenals.
- Bai can later create pseudo-Divine-Key weapons, floating gun batteries and entire groups of Titan mechs during combat.

Reason therefore changes Bai's strategic category. He is no longer merely one powerful fighter; he can **manufacture military capability and infrastructure at scale**.

The St. Freya confrontation also leaves Bai with a package of material gains—including roughly **300 current B-rank Valkyrie battlesuits**—that strengthens Theresa's academy independently of Bai's personal power.

### Otto loses more than the confrontation

The meeting becomes another Otto self-defeat. Bai Ge acquires assets and allies instead of being placed under Schicksal control. Among the gains associated with this stage are large quantities of Valkyrie equipment—including **hundreds of B-rank Valkyrie suits**—and the increasingly permanent transfer of Rita into Bai Ge’s immediate sphere.

Bai Ge also entrusts Judgment of Shamash to Kiana after this sequence, fulfilling his Kaslana-return promise in a way Otto and Siegfried did not anticipate.

Otto leaves with a dangerously incomplete intelligence picture. He can identify some of Bai Ge’s visible full Herrscher states, but he does not understand the complete inventory and fails to account correctly for the hidden Life-and-Death core and later Wind acquisitions.

## Big Bronya, the House of the Hearth, and Hyperion

### Bronya receives an adult “Big Bronya” body

Chapters 133–137 are a concentrated Bronya upgrade arc. The jokes about “four-turbo Bronya,” Project Bunny apologizing to its boss, “8 > 50,000,” and **Big Bronya (大鸭鸭)** are not merely costume gags. Bronya's body is genuinely pushed into a **mature/adult physical form**.

The mechanism is biological, not cosmetic. Bai's Life-and-Death authority can directly manipulate living cells, and Volume Two had already established that—given enough deliberate biological adjustment—he could accelerate Bronya toward the mature body associated with her later/post-Honkai form. At that earlier point Bai resisted simply forcing a child to mature because he preferred that she grow naturally. Volume Three revisits that possibility after their Current-Era relationship and body context have changed.

The Big-Bronya state should therefore be treated as a **Life-and-Death-driven accelerated maturation / biological super-evolution**, not an illusion, costume, or ordinary passage of years. Later prose directly treats the mature body as real.

The transformation also pays off an old playful promise/bet between Bai and Bronya about what would happen if she grew into that adult form. Their relationship becomes explicitly physically/romantically adult only after this mature-body context is established.

The fanfiction deliberately preserves two relationship layers at once:

1. Bronya genuinely remembers Bai Ge as **Father Bai Ge**, the man who raised her at the House of the Hearth.
2. The mature Current-Era Bronya develops explicitly romantic and possessive feelings toward him.

The novel does not erase the first category when it develops the second. A translator should therefore **preserve the deliberate dissonance** rather than retconning the child-era House-of-the-Hearth bond into “they were always romantic” or dismissing all later adult romantic coding as purely filial.

### Project Bunny becomes a modular weapons system

Bai Ge’s Reason power combines with Project Bunny’s growing autonomy. It gains new movement forms, stealth-flight capability, and access to a much larger integrated arsenal.

Bai Ge constructs a **pseudo-Star of Eden** for Bronya. With that weapon plus Project Bunny’s upgrades, her practical combat effectiveness rises into roughly **S-rank territory**, and the story can joke that she has become something like a pseudo-Herrscher of Reason even though she has not simply inherited Welt’s full canonical role at this point.

### The House of the Hearth relocates to St. Freya

Bai's renewed closeness with the now-mature Bronya reminds him that the **House of the Hearth** he created during the targeted-past traversal still exists as a real place filled with girls who remember him.

He returns there with Bronya and Seele, not merely for nostalgia but because the family remains geographically and institutionally divided.

Bai asks the girls whether they want to move to **St. Freya** and promises that they will be safe with him. They agree because Father Bai Ge has returned and because St. Freya offers a future beyond remaining isolated in the old orphanage.

Theresa's reaction is strategically enthusiastic rather than reluctant. Once she understands that the House includes **dozens of girls with strong Honkai resistance**, she sees a potential future Valkyrie cohort—especially now that Bai can make the artificial-stigmata process much safer.

The move itself demonstrates how casually Bai now combines authorities:

1. he uses gravity/constructed power to **lift the entire House-of-the-Hearth building** rather than abandoning the home;
2. using his fragmentary Void authority, he opens access to Imaginary space and **stores the building itself inside**;
3. Hyperion comes to transport the girls;
4. at St. Freya he restores/reinstalls their home;
5. he then uses full Life-and-Death control to implant artificial stigmata while minimizing the pain, corrosion and biological damage normally associated with the procedure.

The move therefore preserves both meanings of “home”: the girls keep the physical House Bai created for them, but it is now embedded inside his Current-Era St. Freya world.

This changes St. Freya's future personnel pipeline. Theresa no longer has to treat Valkyrie training as a system that inevitably consumes large numbers of young women through artificial-stigmata side effects and Honkai erosion. Bai can make the process far safer and can repair many failures afterward.

### Bai Ge becomes Hyperion's certified captain and St. Freya's medical chief

The captain question is fully resolved in Volume Three.

Bai spends the early St. Freya period training for command rather than simply demanding the player-character role because he knows the ship is a real military responsibility. He eventually **passes the S-class battleship-captain examination**. Theresa then keeps the promise associated with the controlled Third Eruption and formally assigns **Hyperion** to him.

Bai puts on the white captain's uniform and takes the command chair. Hyperion is no longer only a ship he nostalgically recognizes from the game; it is a military asset for which he has actual authority and responsibility.

The House-of-the-Hearth return simultaneously formalizes his other institutional role. Theresa makes Bai **St. Freya's medical chief (`医务长`)** because his Life-and-Death authority, modified holy blood and Maki/HSN-b46 research have made him the academy's most important medical resource.

These two roles reinforce one another:

- **Captain:** Bai can move people, command Hyperion, deploy firepower and organize missions.
- **Medical chief:** he can keep the people he deploys alive and convert injuries/erosion that once meant retirement or death into recoverable conditions.

The end-state is therefore not merely “Bai wants to become captain” or “Bai is a powerful student.” By the House-of-the-Hearth consolidation, **Bai is Hyperion's recognized captain and St. Freya's medical chief**.

He immediately begins thinking beyond Hyperion's existing specifications: Reason authority can manufacture and upgrade weapons, so the ship itself becomes another platform Bai can improve.

This is one of the most important role changes in the early novel. Bai has literally become the **Captain** whose position he once associated with the player, while simultaneously becoming the institution's medical safety net.

## Rita moves from Otto-associated infiltrator to Bai Ge's genuine maid and lover

Rita Rossweisse does not simply decide one morning to move into Bai's dormitory.

She enters St. Freya still institutionally entangled with Schicksal and is an **S-rank operative specializing in infiltration and assassination**. Leaving such a person unsupervised inside Theresa's increasingly anti-Otto academy would be irrational. The practical solution is to place Rita under **Bai's direct household supervision**, because he is one of the few people strong enough to stop her immediately if she acts against St. Freya.

Rita accepts the arrangement and increasingly frames her new position in the language she knows best: **maid service**. She begins calling Bai **“Master.”**

That creates a deliberate irony. What begins partly as containment/surveillance becomes the mechanism by which Otto loses her personally:

1. Rita lives in Bai's household rather than remaining an emotionally distant Schicksal operative.
2. She serves him daily and observes how he treats Mei, Kiana, Bronya, Seele and St. Freya.
3. Bai treats her as a person rather than disposable institutional property.
4. The “maid assignment” becomes a role Rita genuinely enjoys and identifies with.
5. Chapters 139–146 use truth-or-dare, domestic comedy and Rita's failing composure to show emotional distance collapsing.
6. Durandal's disbelief that Rita “has a man” confirms that the shift is visible even from Schicksal's side.
7. By chapter 154, **Rita independently kisses Bai**, making her personal choice explicit.

Rita's perception also becomes operationally important. During the Wendy affair, she does not fully accept Schicksal's public story; her knowledge of Bai and “woman's intuition” make her suspect his involvement even when surveillance says otherwise.

Her institutional status remains more complicated than her feelings. That distinction becomes decisive in the final Otto crisis. Otto uses a **backdoor embedded in high-generation Godsbane armor** to seize control of Rita's body and force her to attack Bai.

The attack therefore proves the opposite of betrayal:

- Otto can still exploit Schicksal hardware;
- he can make Rita's body move;
- but he cannot make her personally choose him again.

Once the backdoor is removed, Rita is horrified by what her body did and directs her anger toward Otto. Chapter 172's request that Bai “punish me severely” is **trauma and guilt over coerced violence**, not evidence that she chose Otto over Bai.

## Wendy is saved through one of Bai Ge’s largest deception operations

### Wendy’s original condition

Wendy has been made the host of the **Gem of Desire (渴望宝石)**. The implantation leaves her legs badly damaged and keeps her in severe pain, closely resembling the canon route toward the abandoned Wind-Herrscher tragedy.

Bai Ge has already contacted her secretly before the full operation, at one point appearing almost like a “ghost.” Wendy initially believes that enduring the suffering is worthwhile if her sacrifice serves a larger purpose.

Bai Ge eventually rejects that premise completely. As with Himeko, he refuses to treat a young woman’s destruction as the acceptable price of an institution’s plan.

### Bai Ge heals Wendy and takes the Gem of Desire

Using the full Life-and-Death authority, Bai Ge **repairs Wendy’s legs and removes the cellular damage and pain** created by the gem. He takes the **Gem of Desire** himself.

The real Wendy is then secretly evacuated back toward Theresa/St. Freya using Project Bunny’s transport capability.

This is already enough to break the canon tragedy—but Bai Ge goes further because merely saving Wendy would reveal immediately that someone powerful interfered.

### Bai Ge physically becomes “Wendy” because a simple rescue would expose the intervention

Healing Wendy is only half the problem.

If Wendy simply disappears after her Gem is removed, Otto and Schicksal immediately know that a powerful third party interfered. They can search for her, investigate St. Freya, and reconstruct who took the core.

Bai therefore decides to give enemy intelligence the **story it already expects**.

He rewrites his own body cell by cell until he is physically indistinguishable from Wendy. This includes face, body proportions, biological characteristics and sex.

**It is not an illusion or simple disguise.**

Before revealing the false Wendy, Bai creates a huge Wind-Herrscher-scale storm that prevents useful satellite observation and destroys the Oceania facility. The real Wendy is placed aboard Project Bunny and sent secretly to Theresa/St. Freya.

Only then does Bai allow the world to observe “Wendy.”

The staged causal story is:

> Gem host suffers → Wind Herrscher awakens → Oceania branch is destroyed → new Herrscher visibly flees.

That is exactly plausible enough for Schicksal to stop asking the right question.

The two histories are therefore:

**Public history:** Wendy became the Wind Herrscher, destroyed the Oceania branch and escaped.

**Actual history:** Bai healed Wendy, removed the Gem of Desire, secretly evacuated her, physically became Wendy, destroyed the branch himself and manufactured the visible Herrscher escape.

Bai then exploits the false identity operationally. As “Wendy,” he can continue flying away from Earth rather than returning toward St. Freya, reaches the Fourth Divine Key satellite, takes its Previous-Era Wind core, and later uses quantum/stealth methods so observers do not see the fake Herrscher quietly return to Bai's home.

This public-history/true-history split must remain active because Otto and other factions make later decisions using the false version.

### Wendy joins Bai Ge’s Hyperion side

The real Wendy does not disappear from the narrative after being saved. Once safe, she is eager to belong to Bai Ge’s side, and Bai Ge can now organize a Valkyrie squad under his authority as **Hyperion’s captain**.

Wendy therefore enters the future St. Freya/Hyperion roster as a living ally rather than a sacrificed Herrscher host.

### Bai Ge steals a second Wind core

The Gem of Desire is only the first Wind asset.

Bai Ge subsequently reaches the fanfiction’s **Fourth Divine Key environmental satellite**, named **苍穹绝响** in the text, and removes its **Previous-Era Wind-Herrscher core**.

The canonical Fourth Divine Key does not have a securely established official English proper name, so this fanfic-specific name should not be forcibly replaced with a canon label. A safe translation is to retain **Cangqiong Juexiang (苍穹绝响)** on first occurrence, optionally with a literal gloss such as “Final Echo of the Firmament/Sky.”

By combining the Current-Era Gem of Desire with the Previous-Era Wind core, Bai Ge becomes a **dual-core Wind Herrscher**.

### Five-core Bai Ge returns

After the Wind acquisitions, Bai Ge describes himself as having become a **five-core Herrscher again**, now substantially stronger than the five-core historical form he possessed during the rewritten Second Eruption.

The relevant full/core-level inventory at this stage is best understood as:

* **Life and Death** — Gem of Serenity
* **Flame** — full Flame capability/core used through the Blank Key route
* **Reason** — Previous-Era Reason/Wisdom core through Void Archives + Blank Key
* **Wind** — Gem of Desire
* **Wind** — Previous-Era Wind core from Cangqiong Juexiang

This is separate from the **fragmentary Void, Death, Flame, Wind, and Thunder authorities** he permanently retained from Volume One. The fragmentary package still exists, but it should not be counted one-for-one as five additional full physical cores.

Bai Ge flies to the **Moon** after the Wind breakthrough and even collects a jar of lunar soil as a souvenir, emphasizing how casual enormous mobility feats have become for him.

Most importantly, he deliberately hides the scale of this growth. **Otto continues planning against an obsolete model of Bai Ge.**

## Kiana becomes an acknowledged lover

Kiana’s relationship arc occupies chapters 149–158 and removes the ambiguity that characterized the first two volumes.

### The date

Chapter 149 is an actual **Bai Ge–Kiana date**. Strangers read them as a couple, and Kiana herself can no longer pretend that her feelings are simply friendship or playful attachment.

The emotional obstacle is Mei. Kiana loves Mei as a close companion and knows Mei was Bai Ge’s first established girlfriend. Chapter 150 therefore has Kiana internally acknowledge that pursuing Bai Ge means she is going to “wrong” Mei.

### Kiana reaches MAX affinity

Chapter 151 registers **Kiana affinity MAX** through the system.

This is mechanically important as well as romantic. Volume Two established affinity as a condition for **targeted past traversal**. Kiana therefore becomes an obvious eligible future target even though Volume Three itself does not immediately launch a Kiana-targeted historical run.

### Mei gives explicit consent

Bai Ge promises Kiana that he will remain with her. Mei eventually recognizes that Kiana’s feelings cannot be dismissed or suppressed without hurting everyone involved.

Chapter 156 is unusually explicit: **Mei allows Kiana to “join.”**

Chapter 157 then removes any remaining ambiguity from Bai Ge’s side:

**“I love you, Kiana.”**

By the end of this sequence, Kiana is an **acknowledged reciprocal romantic partner**, not merely a girl with an unresolved crush. Mei has consciously consented to the expanded relationship structure.

This does not mean all jealousy disappears. The story continues to use “shura field” comedy. It means that the core relationship is now consensually plural rather than secretly competitive.

## The evolved five-core state and the Kevin-type armed puppet

### Bai Ge reaches roughly 15,000 HW

Chapter 159 gives the clearest late-Volume-Three power scale.

After another **羽化**-described evolution/ascension stage, Bai Ge’s five-core state reaches approximately **15,000 HW** of Honkai-energy capacity, accompanied by regeneration several times faster than before.

The important practical consequence is that attacks which once functioned like suicidal or once-per-battle ultimates are becoming repeatable techniques. A high-output Shamash / An-Utu-class release no longer empties him in the way it would have during earlier arcs.

### The opponent is a Kevin-pattern puppet, not Kevin

The enemy in chapter 159 is a **Kevin-type armed puppet**, not Kevin Kaslana himself.

This distinction matters for later power scaling. Defeating the puppet does not mean Bai Ge has cleanly defeated the actual Kevin at maximum power. Nevertheless, the puppet’s own combat analysis is extremely favorable to Bai Ge: he is judged to have surpassed most Flame-Chasers, and Kevin without his higher **业魔 / Yemo** state would have difficulty suppressing him.

### Reason authority becomes battlefield manufacturing

Bai Ge’s Reason power is fully integrated into combat by this point. He produces **Moonlight-Throne-style floating guns**, launches autonomous pursuit weapons, and constructs **dozens of Titan mechs** to reinforce another battlefield while still fighting the puppet himself.

This is a major qualitative change. Bai Ge is no longer merely one extremely strong fighter. He can create military assets during combat and therefore project force across several locations at once.

### Raven finally gets her island—and immediately loses it

Chapter 155 had just established Raven’s long-running dream payoff: she has finally saved enough money and intends to **retire**, having acquired the villa/island she wanted.

Then the late Wind-scale battle happens.

Chapter 160’s collateral damage destroys the island.

The timing is deliberately cruel comedy. Raven spends years chasing financial security, reaches it, and almost immediately loses the property because of Bai Ge’s Herrscher-scale battle.

She does **not** yet know that Bai Ge is responsible when Volume Three ends. Her discovery occurs in chapter 173, the **first chapter of Volume Four**.

### Otto and World Serpent’s transaction fails

Chapter 162 has Otto effectively demand that **World Serpent give him a refund**, linking the failed Kevin-puppet operation to an Otto–World Serpent transaction or supplied weapon capability.

The exact commercial joke is less important than the factional fact: **Otto is willing to work with World Serpent assets against Bai Ge, and even that escalation fails.**

## Otto finally recognizes the historical Bai Ge—and misunderstands why nobody did sooner

### The system has been blocking identity linkage, not deleting history

One of the apparent cross-volume paradoxes finally receives an explicit explanation.

Many people met a historical Bai Ge during one of his rewritten eras, yet present teenage Bai can walk around St. Freya without every witness immediately declaring, “This is the same person.”

The system applies an **identity-protection / cognitive-restriction mechanism** to people whose bond/knowledge is insufficient.

Its scope is specific:

- it does **not** erase the rewritten historical event;
- it does **not** erase a person's memories of historical Bai;
- it does **not** necessarily prevent someone from noticing visual or behavioral similarity;
- it biases cognition against completing the final identity equation:
  **present Bai = the Bai from that historical traversal**.

This is why witnesses can remember the 2000 Herrscher in detail while failing to identify the teenager standing in front of them.

The protection is not absolute mind control. Sufficiently strong evidence can overcome it.

### Otto breaks through because behavior + archival evidence become impossible to ignore

The breakthrough occurs during Otto's late fission-missile escalation.

Bai once again chooses to intercept mass-destruction weapons rather than save only himself. To Otto, the behavior is unnervingly specific: it recreates the defining moral image of the 2000 Herrscher who destroyed himself protecting people from Otto's own missile strategy.

That behavioral match pushes Otto to re-open historical records.

He compares old footage with the present Bai:

- same name,
- same white hair and golden-eyed appearance,
- same pattern of impossible historical appearances,
- and, most importantly, the same self-sacrificial behavioral signature.

The accumulated contradiction finally becomes stronger than the system's cognitive bias, and Otto consciously identifies present Bai as the **historical 2000 Herrscher of the Void**.

The revelation does not turn St. Freya against Bai. By now the historical identity only confirms what his allies already know morally: the “Herrscher enemy” Otto once ordered people to kill was also the person who protected those same Valkyries from Otto's weapons.

### Otto’s “Herrscher of Sentience” deduction is wrong

Otto also notices that some form of cognitive interference has prevented the connection for years. He detects Bai Ge’s enormous current Honkai output and tries to construct a rational model from incomplete information.

He concludes that Bai Ge must possess **Herrscher-of-Sentience-like ability / an awareness-related core** capable of altering cognition.

This is **Otto’s false inference**, not narrator truth.

The interference comes from Bai Ge’s system, not from a newly acquired Sentience core. Bai Ge realizes that Otto has built a useful wrong explanation and sarcastically allows him to believe it.

This is another example of the information asymmetry governing late Volume Three: Otto is still dangerous, but increasingly every plan he makes begins from a false model of what Bai Ge actually is.

## Otto’s fission-missile trap and the awakening of K-423’s Herrscher of the Void

### Otto deliberately repeats the shape of the Second Eruption

Otto’s late plan uses **three Honkai-energy fission missiles against St. Freya**.

The attack is not merely an attempt to kill Bai Ge. Otto wants to reproduce the psychological conditions of the rewritten Second Eruption and force the **Gem of Freedom inside K-423** to awaken its Herrscher personality under extreme life-and-death pressure.

The irony is deliberate: Otto is trying to weaponize the very history Bai Ge created while failing to understand how deeply K-423’s core is connected to Bai Ge himself.

Bai Ge intercepts the missiles rather than allowing St. Freya to be destroyed. Unlike 2000, however, he is now vastly stronger, backed by a full institution, and no longer dependent on a temporary Battle Continuation trait to survive the act.

### Rita’s attack is forced through Otto’s armor backdoor

Otto also exploits a control mechanism hidden in high-generation Godsbane armor. **Rita’s body is forced to attack Bai Ge against her will.**

Her immediate horror makes the distinction clear. She does not choose to betray him, and once released she turns her anger toward Otto.

This is the proper context for chapter 172. Rita’s later request to be punished is a guilt response to having injured the person she loves while being physically controlled. Bai Ge does not interpret the act as voluntary treachery.

### The Herrscher inside K-423 is not Sirin

The final Herrscher-of-the-Void arc was one of the most dangerous points to misread when only chapter titles were available. The prose resolves it decisively.

**The Herrscher of the Void awakening inside K-423 is not the surviving Sirin.**

Sirin is a separate living person in this altered timeline.

K-423’s internal Herrscher personality is born from the **Gem of Freedom**, the historical Void core/material connected to Bai Ge’s 2000 Herrscher state. Because K-423 was created from Bai Ge-derived Herrscher material plus Kaslana material, the new personality is causally descended from **Bai Ge’s historical Herrscher self**, not from Sirin’s original canon route.

### Cecilia, Sirin, and Bella return because the old Herrscher signature reappears at the promised time

The Volume-One promise is finally paid off before Volume Three ends.

**Cecilia is alive. Sirin is alive. Bella/Benares is alive. They re-enter Bai Ge's Current-Era story physically.**

Their appearance is causally tied to the late HoV crisis rather than being a disconnected cameo.

Fifteen years earlier, Bai told Bella and the surviving family to live, protect one another and wait for the time when he would return. Bella has therefore spent the intervening years carrying a specific expectation: **when Bai's historical Herrscher presence appears again, it may be the promised reunion.**

K-423's HoV awakening activates the **Gem of Freedom**, the Void core/material historically associated with Bai. That produces a familiar authority/signature.

Bella senses it and comes expecting Bai.

Instead she finds a newly awakened HoV personality inside K-423 trying to treat “Benares” as her servant.

Cecilia and Sirin are physically present as part of this returning 2000-era family network. Their presence turns the crisis into the payoff of the rewritten Second Eruption:

- the people Bai saved really survived the intervening fifteen years;
- the promise to meet again was remembered;
- the historical core itself becomes the beacon that draws past and present together.

This is not a memory-space cameo or symbolic vision. It is a Current-Era reunion created by altered history.

### Bella rejects the new HoV’s claim over Benares

The new Kiana-HoV personality behaves as though the Benares relationship from Herrscher lore should belong to her. Bella’s response establishes the timeline difference perfectly.

Bella rejects the command.

She is **Bai Ge’s Bella**, not the servant of K-423’s Herrscher personality. Bai Ge gave Benares a human body, four pseudo-cores, a name, family status, and freedom. She spent approximately fifteen years surviving under the promise that she would meet him again.

By now Bella is vastly more experienced and powerful than the newly awakened HoV, and she treats the girl’s attempt to claim Benares as fundamentally mistaken.

### Why K-423’s HoV is obsessed with Bai Ge

Sirin supplies the missing mechanism.

Herrscher cores can carry **memory, will and personality information**. K-423's HoV is not a returned Sirin because the core from which the personality forms is no longer Sirin's historical route. It is the **Gem of Freedom associated with Bai's rewritten 2000 Herrscher self**.

The newly generated personality therefore inherits Bai-linked historical information.

That information includes the most emotionally extreme parts of his 2000 life:

- Babylon torture;
- taking experiments in place of Sirin and the others;
- protecting the girls;
- shielding Valkyries who had just been fighting him;
- absorbing Otto's missile attacks;
- saving Alexandra and unborn Bronya;
- spending his final strength healing biological Kiana;
- and repeatedly accepting suffering because he refuses to let other people become the price.

The HoV also understands that the world contains beauty. Her conclusion is therefore **not** the canon-like “humanity hurt me, so humanity should be destroyed.”

Her logic is more possessive:

> **The world may be beautiful, but it repeatedly consumes and hurts Bai Ge. Therefore this world does not deserve him.**

By chapter 169 the devotion becomes apocalyptic: **destroy the world for Bai so that nothing can hurt him again.**

This is a key fanfic rule. The HoV personality's morality is being generated from **Bai's inherited memories filtered through a newly born Herrscher consciousness**. She is not Bai himself, but she begins with his pain and converts it into an extreme protective conclusion he would never endorse.

### Bai Ge resolves the crisis through relationship, not execution

Bai Ge does what he has repeatedly done with Herrscher personalities since Mei: he refuses the assumption that the only possible solution is to kill the person in front of him.

He redirects the HoV’s destructive attachment away from “destroy everything for him” and toward **loving him and living with him instead**.

Chapter 170’s joke—essentially “Why don’t you just love me instead?”—is the comic surface of a serious continuity result. K-423’s Herrscher personality is **not killed, erased, or exorcised**. Her motive is redirected into a human relationship framework.

This extends the novel’s central Herrscher thesis:

**Herrscher consciousness is not identical to obedience to Honkai. Emotional bonds can transform the direction of Herrscher will, even when the will itself remains powerful and distinct.**

## Bai Ge’s power and system progression in Volume Three

### The bookkeeping problem: fragments versus full cores

The single most important power-system distinction entering later volumes is that Bai Ge possesses **several overlapping categories of power**.

At the start of Volume Three he carries **two earlier reward layers**, not only the Volume-One package.

**From Volume One:**

* fragmentary Void authority
* fragmentary Death authority
* fragmentary Flame authority
* fragmentary Wind authority
* fragmentary Thunder authority
* Kaslana constitution / stigmata inheritance
* Schariac stigmata and holy blood
* extreme/absolute Honkai adaptability
* personal-limit/body growth rewards

**Added permanently in Volume Two:**

* **Complete Quantum authority**
* **Initial Time authority**
* **Infinite Evolution Body**
* **Herrscher-of-Death stigmata**, joining the fused Kaslana–Schariac structure
* Project Bunny 19C operation/control compatibility
* Heavenly Voice and other secondary system rewards

By late Volume Two his stigmata/body description should therefore be read as **Kaslana + Schariac + Death/Seele-related**, and his body already tolerates more than roughly **4,500 HW** before Volume Three's new cores are acquired.

The five old elemental/Herrscher abilities are **persistent fragments of the historical five-core run**. Quantum and Time are separate permanent authorities added by the second traversal. None of those categories should be mistaken for the full Current-Era physical/core-level states Volume Three subsequently adds.

Volume Three then adds **full Current-Era/physical-core Herrscher states**, some mediated through the Blank Key. These categories must remain separate.

### Full/core-level progression

### Why “five-core” should not be read as ten cores

Bai Ge can still invoke old fragmentary Void/Thunder/etc. abilities while also operating the five full/core-level states above. The text is loose with shorthand because Bai Ge himself thinks in terms of “what Herrscher powers can I use?” rather than maintaining a game-style inventory spreadsheet.

For translation, the safest model is:

**Historical simulator fragments = permanent reduced authorities.**

**Current full cores / Blank-Key cores = later full-strength Herrscher states.**

Do not add the two categories together mechanically and claim Bai Ge physically contains ten independent cores unless a later chapter explicitly says so.

## Character and relationship state at the end of Volume Three

### Bai Ge

Bai Ge is no longer merely the anomalous boy St. Freya agreed to admit.

By chapter 172 he is:

* **Hyperion’s S-class-certified captain**
* **St. Freya’s medical chief (`医务长`)** and most important medical asset
* an evolved five-core Herrscher-level combatant with roughly 15,000 HW capacity
* a full Herrscher of Life and Death
* a full Reason user through the Blank Key/Void Archives route
* a dual-core Wind Herrscher
* a practitioner learning Taixuan techniques from Fu Hua
* the historical 2000 Herrscher whom Otto finally identifies
* the father figure of the House of the Hearth
* Mei and Kiana’s acknowledged romantic partner, with Himeko and Rita also explicitly inside his romantic orbit
* the person around whom St. Freya, Anti-Entropy, surviving Second-Eruption characters, and even Kevin increasingly organize their decisions

His morality remains the same beneath the power increase. Bai Ge will use terrifying force against people he considers culpable, as Cocolia’s punishment demonstrates, but he continues to define himself through protecting individuals rather than serving abstract factional categories.

### Raiden Mei

Mei remains Bai Ge’s **first established girlfriend** and a cooperative Herrscher of Thunder.

Her relationship with him is no longer based merely on the original fake-dating arrangement; by Volume Three they are openly and physically intimate. Mei is jealous when Himeko and later Kiana enter Bai Ge’s romantic circle, but she chooses negotiated acceptance rather than destroying her relationships with the other women.

Most importantly, she explicitly permits Kiana to join the relationship.

### Thunder Queen Mei

Thunder Queen remains a **distinct consciousness/personality inside Mei’s body**. She is more aggressive, domineering, and openly possessive toward Bai Ge than ordinary Mei.

Bai Ge can create her a separate body, but no split occurs in Volume Three because a manufactured body would not automatically carry Mei’s Herrscher/stigmata power structure.

### Kiana / K-423

Kiana’s identity is now both more complicated and more secure.

She is **K-423**, an artificial person created using Kaslana material plus cells/material from the rewritten historical Herrscher of the Void—Bai Ge. Her body contains the **Gem of Freedom**, which later generates an independent HoV personality carrying Bai Ge-linked memories.

She is not biological Kiana/Durandal.

At the same time, Bai Ge refuses to reduce her to an experiment number. **Kiana Kaslana is her chosen and emotionally real identity.**

Romantically, she reaches MAX affinity, dates Bai Ge, receives Mei’s consent to join the relationship, and hears Bai Ge explicitly say that he loves her.

She also receives Judgment of Shamash from Bai Ge and knows Siegfried is alive without abandoning her St. Freya home to chase him immediately.

### K-423’s Herrscher of the Void personality

The late HoV is a separate personality **inside K-423**, born from the Gem of Freedom.

She is not the surviving Sirin.

Because the core descends from Bai Ge’s historical Void-Herrscher state, she inherits Bai Ge-linked memories and becomes fiercely attached to him. Her initial conclusion is that a world which repeatedly made Bai Ge suffer should be destroyed for his sake.

Bai Ge redirects rather than destroys her. Entering Volume Four, she should remain on the active personality roster rather than being treated as a defeated one-off boss.

### Bronya Zaychik

Bronya is physically healthy, emotionally expressive, able to walk normally, and now presented in the mature **Big Bronya** body.

Her combat package has exploded: autonomous Project Bunny, stealth/vehicle transformations, larger Reason-linked arsenal, and pseudo-Star of Eden give her roughly S-rank practical firepower.

Her relationship with Bai Ge must preserve both layers:

* **Father Bai Ge** from the rewritten House-of-the-Hearth childhood
* later adult romantic/possessive attachment in the Current Era

The story deliberately holds both at once.

### Seele Vollerei

Seele is fully rescued from the Sea of Quanta and lives in Bai Ge’s St. Freya household.

She remembers the House of the Hearth as her genuine childhood home and continues to regard Bai Ge as Father Bai Ge / the central protector of that family.

### Dark Seele

Dark Seele is a known, accepted member of the family rather than an evil hidden invader.

She remains in Seele’s body **by choice**. Bai Ge can create a separate body for her, and she keeps that future option open, but she declines immediate separation because White Seele still needs her.

### Murata Himeko

Himeko is alive and no longer on the untouched canon route toward death from accumulated Honkai damage.

Bai Ge’s Life-and-Death authority and the new anti-Honkai treatment line break that health trajectory. She remains a teacher/Valkyrie figure but is also now openly emotionally and romantically attached to Bai Ge.

### Theresa Apocalypse

Theresa is the institutional center holding St. Freya together, but Bai Ge has transformed what the academy can actually do.

She trusts him enough to hand him **Hyperion**, formally appoints him **St. Freya's medical chief**, relies on his medical abilities to preserve injured Valkyries, accepts the House-of-the-Hearth girls, and increasingly runs St. Freya as an independent power rather than an obedient Schicksal school.

### Fu Hua

Fu Hua has abandoned the role of Otto’s hidden St. Freya infiltrator and chooses **St. Freya**.

Ji Lin’s restoration gives her back a person she had mourned for millennia. She also becomes one of Bai Ge’s teachers, instructing him in Taixuan techniques and giving him a Fenghuang Down feather.

### Ji Lin

Ji Lin is **alive** after the Chi You operation and returns with Bai Ge and Mei to St. Freya.

Her presence is a permanent alteration of Fu Hua’s Current-Era history, not a temporary vision.

### Welt Yang

Welt returns to **Anti-Entropy leadership** after agreeing to cooperate with Bai Ge against Otto.

He remains alive and active after Bai Ge gains Reason power because Bai Ge’s Reason state comes through the **Previous-Era Wisdom core / Void Archives + Blank Key route**, not by permanently taking Welt’s Current-Era core.

Welt becomes both an external political ally and a technical mentor for Bai Ge’s Reason authority.

### Kevin Kaslana

Kevin has personally met Bai Ge but is still **contained/separated rather than a normal ally**.

Bai Ge repeatedly destabilizes him with Elysia, Griseo, and Sa information. The Kevin-type puppet fought in chapter 159 is not Kevin himself.

Kevin’s emotional investment in the Previous Era makes Volume Four’s Elysia intervention directly relevant to him even if he is not physically accompanying Bai Ge.

### Otto Apocalypse

Otto remains the principal strategic enemy, but his situation deteriorates dramatically.

He loses Cocolia as a useful subordinate path, sees Welt/AE align with St. Freya, loses Fu Hua’s loyalty, sees Rita emotionally defect, loses Herrscher-core assets, fails to control Wendy’s fate, receives false intelligence about Bai Ge’s Wind growth, and watches a World-Serpent-linked Kevin puppet fail.

He finally recognizes Bai Ge as the historical 2000 Herrscher of the Void, but misattributes the system’s cognitive interference to a supposed Sentience power. Bai Ge deliberately lets him keep the false theory.

Otto remains dangerous because he still has infrastructure, hidden control mechanisms, missiles, Divine-Key technology, and a willingness to manipulate people such as Rita and K-423. He is **weakened informationally, not harmless**.

### Rita Rossweisse

Rita functions as Bai Ge’s maid, calls him Master, and becomes an explicit romantic partner by the kiss/decision sequence.

She remains institutionally entangled with Schicksal long enough for Otto to exploit a Godsbane-armor backdoor and force her to attack Bai Ge. Her chapter-172 guilt is therefore **not betrayal**.

Entering Volume Four, her personal loyalty to Bai Ge is much clearer than her residual formal paperwork/status with Schicksal.

### Durandal / biological Kiana

Durandal is the **biological Kiana** whom Bai Ge saved as a small child in 2000.

She appears/intrudes into Rita’s Volume-Three storyline and reacts to Rita’s changed romantic priorities. The important translator distinction remains:

**Durandal = biological Kiana.**

**The household Kiana = K-423.**

Do not merge them merely because both legitimately carry Kaslana identity in different senses.

Siegfried is alive and directly encountered by Bai Ge in Volume Three. Bai Ge repairs his lost/damaged arm.

Judgment of Shamash passes through Bai Ge’s use and is ultimately entrusted to Kiana rather than simply returned to Siegfried.

### Cecilia Schariac

Cecilia’s Volume-One survival is fully paid off: she **reunites with Bai Ge in the Current Era** during the late HoV crisis.

Her maternal bond with Bai Ge remains part of the altered history. Her presence also proves that the Second-Eruption family created by the simulator was not erased during the intervening years.

### Sirin

Sirin is alive and physically distinct from K-423’s HoV personality.

She returns in the Current Era with Cecilia and Bella and helps explain why the new HoV is so attached to Bai Ge: K-423’s Gem of Freedom carries memory/will information from Bai Ge’s historical Herrscher core.

This distinction is absolutely essential for future translation:

**Sirin ≠ K-423’s HoV personality in this fanfiction.**

Bella survived the fifteen-year interval and returns with Cecilia/Sirin.

She is still the Benares whom Bai Ge gave a human body and four pseudo-cores to, but her chosen name and identity are **Bella**.

She rejects K-423 HoV’s attempt to command “Benares,” explicitly affirming that her master/family bond is with Bai Ge. By this point she has had roughly fifteen years to develop and is much stronger and more experienced than a freshly awakened Herrscher personality.

Schicksal remains enormous, but Otto’s personal faction is increasingly hollowed out by defections and bad intelligence.

Fu Hua is no longer his infiltrator. Rita’s personal loyalty has shifted. Wendy is gone from his control. Core assets have been taken. His model of Bai Ge’s powers is repeatedly obsolete or deliberately false.

Otto compensates through control backdoors, strategic weapons, manipulation of K-423’s Herrscher core, and transactions with World Serpent.

### World Serpent

World Serpent remains active even though **Kevin himself is not freely operating with it**.

Gray Serpent and Raven already know Bai Ge from altered history. A Kevin-type armed puppet enters an Otto-linked operation, and Otto’s “refund” complaint directly ties the failed scheme to World Serpent.

Raven’s attempted retirement makes her own position inside the organization less stable.

### Cocolia’s side

Cocolia’s independent plot line is effectively finished as an active threat because **Bai Ge has permanently imprisoned her**.

Her former dependents and resources can still matter, but translators should not assume she remains available to recreate canon schemes.

### Herrscher/Honkai alignment

By the end of Volume Three, the story has demolished the equation:

**Herrscher = Honkai’s obedient enemy of humanity.**

Bai Ge is a multi-core Herrscher-like protector. Mei is a Herrscher of Thunder aligned with human relationships. K-423’s HoV begins with an apocalyptic motive but that motive is devotion to Bai Ge rather than obedience to Honkai, and it is redirected without killing her.

St. Freya’s policy from Volume Two—judge Herrschers by actual intent and behavior—has been repeatedly vindicated.

## System, identity, information, and persistence mechanics established by Volume Three

Volume Three does not introduce a new historical-traversal mode, but it clarifies several system rules that future translation depends on.

### Affinity remains mechanically real

The relationship system is not merely a romance meter.

Volume Two established that sufficient affinity can make a character eligible for **targeted past traversal**. In Volume Three, **Kiana reaches MAX affinity**.

Therefore MAX is simultaneously:

- an emotional/romantic milestone;
- proof of Kiana's complete bond with Bai;
- and a mechanical signal that she is an obvious future historical-traversal target.

Volume Three does not launch that traversal, so the correct state is **eligibility established, traversal not yet used**.

### Temporal identity protection does not rewrite memories

The system's cognitive protection has four practical rules:

1. **Historical changes remain real.**
2. **Witnesses retain memories of the historical Bai they actually met.**
3. The system suppresses the easy cognitive link between that historical identity and present Bai.
4. Strong enough evidence can overcome the suppression.

This explains why the world can contain extensive records of Bai's historical interventions without every faction immediately solving his time-traveler identity.

It also explains why Otto's eventual recognition does not mean the system suddenly “fails.” Otto forces his way through by comparing unusually strong evidence.

### Recognition does not reveal the system

When Otto breaks through, he learns:

> **present Bai = historical Bai**

He does **not** learn:

> **Bai has a system that inserts him into past eras.**

Because Otto lacks that category of explanation, he constructs the closest in-world model he can: Bai must have some Sentience-like power that alters cognition.

Bai deliberately allows this misconception to survive because an enemy using the wrong model is strategically useful.

### Historical causal products persist independently of Bai's body

The return of Cecilia, Sirin and Bella reinforces the same rule established by Volumes One and Two:

- Bai's past body can disappear;
- the system can return him to the Current Era;
- but the people he saved continue living through the intervening years;
- promises, institutions, children and political consequences created by that history persist.

The fifteen-year reunion is direct proof.

### System powers and acquired world powers must remain separate

Future summaries should maintain at least these columns:

1. **system-granted permanent traits/rewards**  
   e.g. old fragments, Quantum, Time, Infinite Evolution Body;

2. **full physical/core-level Herrscher states acquired in the world**  
   e.g. Gem of Serenity, Gem of Desire;

3. **core states mediated through the Blank Key**  
   e.g. Reason/Wisdom and full Flame route;

4. **stigmata/body inheritances**  
   Kaslana, Schariac, Death/Seele-related;

5. **learned techniques**  
   Taixuan;

6. **constructed/equipment capability**  
   Project Bunny upgrades, pseudo-Star of Eden, Hyperion, Divine Keys;

7. **institutional authority**  
   Hyperion captaincy, St. Freya medical chief.

Flattening these into “Bai has X powers” destroys the causal logic of later volumes.

### Public knowledge is not equal to factual reality

By the end of Volume Three, at least three information layers can coexist:

- what **Bai and his household** know;
- what **St. Freya/AE allies** know;
- what **Otto/Schicksal and the wider world believe**.

The Wendy incident proves that Bai will deliberately engineer false public history. Otto's false Sentience theory proves that even correct observations can lead to incorrect explanations.

Future translation should therefore ask not only **“what is true?”** but **“who knows which truth?”**

## Altered history, public history, and the world adapting to Bai Ge

Volume Two established the rule that the world can preserve recognizable event-shapes while changing their causes around Bai Ge. Volume Three shows that Bai Ge’s interventions have become too deep for canon to simply “correct” him away.

Several examples are essential:

### K-423 still exists, but her origin is rewritten

K-423 remains the teenage Kiana known to the household, but because Sirin never became the historical 2000 HoV, Otto builds K-423 from **Bai Ge-derived historical Herrscher material instead**.

The world preserves K-423 while rewriting the causal route that creates her.

### A Wind-Herrscher incident still happens, but Wendy is not the Herrscher everyone sees

The world receives the public appearance of “Wendy, the Wind Herrscher.”

In reality Bai Ge healed Wendy, evacuated her, transformed himself into her body, and staged the entire visible event.

Canon’s event-shape survives as **deliberate misinformation created by Bai Ge**.

### The HoV crisis still happens, but its personality is Bai-Ge-derived

K-423 still produces a Herrscher-of-the-Void crisis.

Yet the personality is not canon Sirin resurrected through the same causal chain. It is born from Bai Ge’s Gem of Freedom and inherits memories tied to his historical suffering. Its motive becomes “destroy the world for Bai Ge,” not a straightforward recreation of Sirin’s hatred.

### The Second-Eruption survivors really do reach 2015-era Bai Ge

Sirin, Cecilia, and Bella survive because of Bai Ge’s historical intervention and finally return to him in the Current Era.

The system-created relationships have therefore survived across roughly fifteen years of altered world history.

### The system protects Bai Ge’s temporal identity

The system’s cognitive restriction explains why people can possess the rewritten historical memories without immediately identifying teenage Bai Ge as the 2000 Herrscher.

Otto eventually breaks through by comparing present behavior and archived visual evidence, but even then he misunderstands the mechanism and invents a false Sentience-Herrscher explanation.

The best general model entering Volume Four is therefore:

> \*\*Bai Ge is no longer an outsider making temporary deviations from canon. He is one of the world’s central causal facts. History may still preserve recognizable structures, but it must now build those structures around what Bai Ge has already changed.\*\*

## Final translator-facing synthesis

Volume One taught Bai Ge that he could **enter tragedies and take the suffering that should have belonged to other people**.

Volume Two taught the world to **remember the relationships he created while doing so**.

Volume Three turns those interventions into institutions. Himeko's rescue becomes a medical program; Reason becomes scalable industry; the House becomes a personnel pipeline; and Bai's personal authority becomes formal captaincy and medical command.

Seele is no longer trapped in the Sea of Quanta. Himeko is no longer walking toward an unavoidable Honkai-corrosion death. Ji Lin is alive again. Wendy is not abandoned as the Wind Herrscher; Bai Ge manufactures that public history while secretly saving her. Bronya is no longer a disabled child shaped by Cocolia but an adult combatant whose real childhood home is Bai Ge’s House of the Hearth. Fu Hua is no longer Otto’s hidden operative inside St. Freya. Welt returns to Anti-Entropy and cooperates with Bai Ge. Hyperion is no longer merely the ship Bai Ge admired as a player—**he is now its captain**.

Even K-423’s deepest “canon” fact has been rewritten. She still exists, but the historical Herrscher material used to create her comes from **Bai Ge**, because Bai Ge replaced Sirin as the Second Eruption’s Void Herrscher. When her HoV personality awakens, it therefore inherits memories associated with Bai Ge and concludes that the world should be destroyed not for Honkai, but for him.

And then the story pays off the promise made at the end of Volume One: **Cecilia, Sirin, and Bella return to Bai Ge in the Current Era.** Sirin is alive. Bella is alive. The people who waited roughly fifteen years were not erased by the simulator’s end.

By chapter 172, Bai Ge has become too deeply embedded in history for the world to restore a version of events where he was merely a visitor. He is a historical Herrscher, a Current-Era multi-core Herrscher, a father, lover, medical miracle-worker, captain, ally of Anti-Entropy, enemy of Otto, and the person who can still reach the Previous Era itself.

That is the exact position from which **Volume Four: Previous Era** begins.

# *People in Honkai Impact 3rd, Be Their Heroes* — Volume Four Full Story and Continuity Guide

## Scope, sources, and exact volume boundary

**Volume Four is Faloo chapters 173–385 inclusive, under the official volume heading `第四卷，前文明` (“Volume Four, Previous Era”). Volume Five begins with Faloo chapter 386, `重回现文明，与众女的重逢` (“Return to the Current Era, Reunion with the Girls”).** Faloo therefore fixes the boundary; mirror URL/page numbers must not be used as chapter numbers. The official directory explicitly places chapter 173 after Volume Three, carries Volume Four through chapter 385, and then starts Volume Five at chapter 386.

There is an additional numbering complication important for future translation alignment. Faloo's displayed directory itself contains visible numbering irregularities inside Volume Four, including duplicate displayed chapter numbers around **284** and **305**, plus apparent typographical corruption in some other numeral strings. The safest reference system is therefore **Faloo's chapter label/title pair**, not an inferred “Nth mirror page” or a mechanically normalized sequence. The nominal numeral span 173–385 is 213 numbers, but that does **not** mean every mirror will have a one-to-one 213-page offset.

The continuity baseline has now been checked directly against the Project's finalized `volume_1_summary(2).md`, `volume_2_summary(2).md`, and `volume_3_summary(3).md`. Bai Ge enters Volume Four already as Hyperion's certified captain, with the five old permanent **fragmentary authorities** kept separate from his later full/core-level states. The correct Volume-Three full-state handoff is **Life/Death + Flame + Reason + Wind + Wind**, alongside the older fragmentary Void, Death, Flame, Wind, and Thunder authorities. The established Current-Era relationship, identity, and faction states from Volumes One–Three remain binding unless Volume Four itself changes them.

**Source method.** Faloo is treated as authoritative for the publication structure, volume boundary, and displayed chapter-number/title pairs. Chinese prose mirrors such as TRXS and FFXS are used to recover chapter text when Faloo's prose is inaccessible. Because Faloo itself contains duplicated displayed numbers inside this volume—notably duplicated **284** and **305**—the safest citation convention for future translation work is **displayed Faloo chapter number + chapter title**, not a mechanically normalized sequence and not a mirror page number. The Project summaries for Volumes One–Three have been read separately and used to correct cross-volume assumptions in this guide.

The single most important conclusion is that Volume Four pays off Bai Ge's earlier **“Elysia isn't dead”** promise by changing the Previous Era itself. He does not discover a secret already-living 2014 Elysia. He enters her childhood, becomes one of the foundational people in her life, repeatedly rewrites the Previous Era, and eventually solves the one problem that survives every otherwise successful run: Elysia's Origin miracle is still needed to free future Herrschers from Honkai, but Elysia herself need not be the one who dies to pay its price. The final solution is exact rather than merely thematic: Bai obtains Current-Era **Domination**, uses the Imaginary Tree route to return to the decisive Previous-Era moment, **completely takes Elysia's Origin authority away from her**, performs the rule-changing wish himself, bears the lethal price with his historical body, and then returns Origin to a newly freed Elysia before the system sends his true/current self home. Elysia survives and chapter 385 ends with her promise that she will go to the future to find him.

## The causal architecture of Volume Four: system, memory, reset, and persistence

Volume Four is easy to misunderstand if it is read as a simple sequence of Previous-Era adventures. Its actual structure is governed by **four interacting mechanisms**: the system's historical traversal, temporary traversal traits, deliberate memory sealing/storage, and the `轮回之人` reset ability. The later parallel-Current-Era arc is a fifth mechanism created only after Bai deliberately breaks the normal traversal rules. These mechanics explain **why** the same Herrscher eras appear more than once, why Bai sometimes behaves as if he does not know facts that the player of *Honkai Impact 3rd* should know, why Elysia repeatedly reaches the same sacrifice despite wildly different histories, and why only Bai accumulates the full psychological burden of the loops.

### 1. Volume Four begins as one active system historical traversal

Bai does **not** abandon his original Current-Era body and permanently reincarnate into the Previous Era. As in the earlier simulator/traversal arcs, the system sends his operative self into real history while his proper Current-Era timeline is effectively waiting for the historical intervention to end. Changes made in the Previous Era are intended to become the history that leads into his original Current Era.

The protection supplied by the system is crucial. A death suffered by the historical/traversal body is not automatically the death of Bai's true Current-Era existence. Later, when he sees the paradox-created branch's Bai Ge repeatedly save people only to die for them, Bai explicitly thanks his own system: without it, his history-changing style would have produced exactly that outcome. The system gives him the freedom to spend a historical body as the price of changing tragedy and still return to the true Current Era when the traversal is properly completed.

This is the fundamental reason the final Origin solution can work. **The historical Bai Ge can die paying Elysia's price without erasing the true Bai Ge waiting at the end of the system traversal.**

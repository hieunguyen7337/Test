# *People in Honkai Impact 3rd, Be Their Heroes* — Volume One Full Story and Continuity Guide

## Scope, sources, and the exact volume boundary

The work discussed here is **《人在崩坏三，当她们的英雄》**, written by **黄金森林** (Huangjin Senlin / “Golden Forest”), commonly rendered in English as ***People in Honkai Impact 3rd, Be Their Heroes***. This guide is written as continuity-sensitive translation context rather than as a loose synopsis: it records what actually changes in the fanfiction's timeline, which relationships and powers survive Bai Ge's historical traversal, which people know which facts, and which apparently familiar *Honkai Impact 3rd* events now have different causes.

For chapter order and volume boundaries, **Faloo is treated as authoritative** because it is the publication source. Chinese mirror text such as **TRXS / Fan Novel Network** is useful for chapter prose where Faloo material is difficult to access, while WTR-LAB is useful only as a secondary machine-translation/search aid. Mirror webpage numbers are not reliable substitutes for Faloo chapter numbers. Established English *Honkai Impact 3rd* names are used where the correspondence is clear, but the fanfiction's own altered lore takes priority over canon assumptions.

Faloo does not visibly label the opening block “第一卷 / Volume One”; chapters 1–52 are listed under **正文 / Main Text**. The next labeled section is **第二卷 / Volume Two**, beginning at chapter 53. For continuity purposes the boundary is therefore exact:

**Volume One = chapters 1–52, inclusive.**

The final sequence is chapter 48 “We Will Meet Again,” chapter 49's attack on Schicksal and rescue attempt involving biological Kiana, chapter 50's Bai Ge–Otto confrontation, chapter 51 “Otto: You Won, Herrscher of the Void Bai Ge,” and chapter 52 “Bai Ge Returns to the Original Timeline.”

The major structure of Volume One is:

| Story phase | Chapters | Central development |
|---|---:|---|
| Nagazora / Chiba Academy | 1–26 | Bai Ge transmigrates into the 2014 Current Era, befriends Mei and Kiana, uncovers Cocolia's plan, prevents the attempted Third Eruption, and then begins dying from Honkai corruption. |
| Past Simulator / rewritten Second Eruption | 27–51 | The system sends Bai Ge to Babylon Labs in 1999–2000. He takes the experiments meant for Sirin and the other girls, becomes the historical Herrscher of the Void, acquires four additional full Herrscher cores on the Moon, saves major canon casualties, defeats Otto's plans, and rewrites the Second Eruption. |
| Return to the Current Era | 52 | Less than one external second after departure, Bai Ge returns to Mei's home healed and carrying a permanent reward package: five **fragmentary** Herrscher authorities, fused Kaslana–Schariac stigmata, holy blood, Kaslana constitution, absolute Honkai adaptability, and other rewards. |

The most important distinction to preserve throughout the guide is that **Bai Ge's historical five-core Herrscher body is temporary, while the rewards he carries back to 2014 are reduced/fragmentary authorities and inherited body traits rather than those same five full physical cores**.

## The causal architecture of Volume One

Volume One establishes the rules that every later historical rewrite depends on. Its plot can look like two separate stories—Nagazora in 2014 and the Second Eruption in 1999–2000—but causally they are one closed chain:

> **Bai Ge intervenes in Mei's attempted awakening while still an ordinary human → that intervention fatally exposes him to Honkai → the completed system gives him a historical traversal as his only immediate survival route → the traversal lets him spend a protected historical body to replace other people's tragedies → those substitutions rewrite the real past → the system converts what he became and whom he bonded with into permanent Current-Era rewards → those rewards cure the Honkai corruption that forced him into the traversal in the first place → the rewritten 2000 history becomes the actual background of the 2014 world he returns to.**

The volume is therefore not “a simulation side story.” It is the novel's first demonstration that **Bai Ge can make himself part of the causal history that produced his own present**.

### 1. Bai's system exists before it is fully operational

When Bai first arrives in 2014, the system can already do limited support work.

It can:

- communicate with him;
- identify the setting/time;
- fabricate a legitimate Current-Era identity and background;
- provide material convenience through that identity.

What it **cannot yet do** is give Bai meaningful combat power. Its main functionality is still loading.

That delay is important. Bai's entire Nagazora intervention is performed by a mostly ordinary human relying on:

- game/setting foreknowledge;
- money and social positioning;
- Kiana's combat ability;
- stolen/recovered ME Corp weapons;
- and his willingness to take risks.

His near-fatal Honkai corruption is therefore a real consequence of choosing to intervene **before** his power system is ready.

### 2. The first historical traversal is physically real for Bai but system-protected

When the system finally finishes loading, it reveals the **Past Simulator / historical traversal**.

The word “simulator” is potentially misleading if taken literally. The source establishes several rules before Bai enters the Second Eruption:

1. Bai is sent into an earlier historical period through a **local historical body/identity**.
2. He can physically suffer, form relationships, gain powers and die there.
3. **Death of the historical body does not kill Current-Era Bai.**
4. **Historical correction has unusually weak leverage over Bai himself.**
5. However long he remains in the historical run, his proper Current-Era departure point is effectively waiting for him; when he returns, **less than one external second has passed**.
6. The system can provide **temporary run-specific traits** suited to the selected scenario.
7. What Bai changes can propagate into real history.
8. The completed run can be converted into **permanent Current-Era rewards**.

The two protections in points 3 and 4 are the key to Bai's entire style of intervention.

Without them, deliberately becoming the person who is tortured, Herrscherized, bombarded and finally killed would simply erase the protagonist.

With them, Bai can spend the historical body as a **causal substitute** for another person's tragedy and still return to 2014.

Volume Four later makes this protection even more explicit when Bai observes that a parallel-world version of himself without the system repeatedly dies for the people he saves. The system is therefore not merely a reward dispenser; it is the safety architecture that lets Bai wager a historical life without permanently deleting his true/current existence.

### 3. The six Second-Eruption traits form one survival package

The first traversal unusually gives Bai **six traits**, because the system recognizes that the Second Eruption is far above his normal combat level:

- **Honkai Energy Adaptability EX**
- **“Honkai's Biological/Favored Son” EX**
- **Pain Insensitivity B**
- **Extreme Pain Insensitivity A**
- **Battle Continuation EX**
- **Unyielding Will A**

These should not be read as six unrelated power-ups.

They solve successive stages of the same impossible mission:

> **Adaptability** lets Babylon inject him without immediately destroying him → **Favored Son** makes Herrscher selection plausible → the two **pain-reduction traits** let him continue enduring experiments → **Unyielding Will** lets his own identity survive Herrscherization → **Battle Continuation** lets the already-dead historical body keep operating long enough to finish the rewrite.

The traits belong to the traversal unless the settlement later converts something separately. Chapter 52 does **not** grant these six traits wholesale to Current-Era Bai.

### 4. The system inserts Bai into history using a fabricated starting relationship, but later bonds are genuine

At Babylon, Bai appears as a child designated **X1**.

The system supplies a plausible local history in which Sirin, Avrora, Agata and Galina already remember him as a childhood companion.

This creates two layers that future translation must keep separate:

- **initial insertion memories/background** — system-fabricated so Bai can enter the period coherently;
- **everything they live together after his arrival** — genuine experience that becomes part of the rewritten world.

Bai himself initially feels the difference. He knows the supposed earlier childhood memories were created by the system and therefore feels a degree of emotional distance.

That distance disappears only after the girls discover what he has been enduring and beg him to stop sacrificing himself for them.

At that point Bai accepts that, regardless of how the system created the starting position, **the family relationship they have built since his arrival is real**.

This distinction becomes crucial later in the series. The system can manufacture an entry point; it does not manufacture the emotional meaning of months or years subsequently lived together.

### 5. Substitution is the first volume's central causal technique

Volume Two later gives the concept a formal fixed-point explanation through X-10, but Volume One already demonstrates the technique repeatedly.

Bai's instinctive solution to tragedy is not always:

> “prevent the dangerous event from existing.”

It is often:

> **“if the event seems to require a victim, make me the victim instead.”**

The pattern appears throughout the Second-Eruption rewrite:

1. **Babylon experiments** should fall on Sirin and the other girls → Bai becomes the sole high-value experimental subject.
2. **The Second Herrscher role** should belong to Sirin → Bai engineers his own Herrscherization and takes the role.
3. **The catastrophic Second Eruption** should kill large numbers of people → Bai deliberately becomes a human-aligned Herrscher instead of executing Honkai's mission.
4. **Otto's fission saturation** should kill fighters and civilians → Bai stays behind and spends his cores/body shielding them.
5. **Buluoke Town / Alexandra / unborn Bronya** should be destroyed by the redirected missile → Bai absorbs the blast into his own spatial domain.
6. **Biological Kiana** should die or remain critically wounded after Otto's HOMU bomb → Bai spends his remaining Death authority healing her while his own historical body is already dying.

Volume Two later reveals that this was not merely a personality motif. It is the first successful form of a **fate-defeating method**:

> **Bai can preserve a causally important event while changing who pays its cost.**

Volume Four ultimately scales the same method up to Elysia's Origin sacrifice.

### 6. Bai's historical Herrscherization is deliberate after Babylon unknowingly supplies the conditions

Babylon believes it is escalating torture on an abnormally durable child.

Bai realizes it is actually assembling the requirements for Herrscherization:

- extreme Honkai compatibility;
- increasing Honkai-energy saturation;
- genuine hatred/negative emotion;
- a body repeatedly pushed beyond ordinary limits.

He therefore continues accepting experiments for **two simultaneous reasons**:

1. to keep Sirin, Avrora, Agata and Galina off the tables;
2. to accumulate enough conditions to become strong enough to break Babylon open.

The eventual Herrscher awakening is thus neither a random system reward nor purely Babylon's success.

It is a three-party causal result:

> **system traits make Bai viable → Babylon supplies energy and trauma → Bai consciously refuses to stop the process once he understands what it can become.**

### 7. “Human-aligned Herrscher” is an allegiance/mechanics description, not a separate standard title

When Bai becomes the historical Herrscher of the Void, a Herrscher consciousness does arise.

**Unyielding Will A** helps Bai's own personality dominate it.

The fanfic also invokes the Elysia/Origin-derived possibility that a Herrscher can preserve humanity and reject Honkai's command.

Later Volume Four clarifies the deep historical cause: Elysia's Origin miracle is what makes human-aligned Herrschers possible in the Current Era, and Bai eventually ensures that miracle remains in history while changing who pays for it.

Thus Volume One's language around `人之律者` should be understood as:

> **a Herrscher whose human self remains dominant and chooses people over Honkai.**

It should **not** be normalized into “Bai literally holds Elysia's unique Herrscher-of-Origin title.”

Much later continuity also strengthens the same interpretation: Origin/Elysia's influence is described as helping protect Bai's humanity from the spiritual pressure of multiple Herrscher cores. That is later-confirmed cosmology, not something the Volume-One cast fully understands in 2000.

### 8. The Moon changes Bai from one-core HoV to five-core historical Herrscher

Bai begins his Herrscher state with his own **Void core**.

On the Moon, the Will/God of Honkai provides Previous-Era information and exposes four sealed authorities/cores:

- **Thunder — Gem of Conquest**
- **Wind — Gem of Desire**
- **Death — Gem of Serenity**
- **Flame — Gem of Haste**

Bai absorbs all four.

The exact historical body inventory becomes:

> **Void + Thunder + Wind + Death + Flame = five full Herrscher cores.**

This is the body the story calls a **five-core Herrscher**.

Nothing about Bella changes that count.

### 9. Bella's four pseudo-cores are created from Bai's energy, not removed from his five-core inventory

Bai creates:

- pseudo-Flame;
- pseudo-Death;
- pseudo-Wind;
- pseudo-Thunder.

He gives all four to Benares/Bella and constructs a human body for her with Death authority.

These are **new pseudo-cores**.

They are not the Moon's four full cores being transferred out of Bai.

Therefore, immediately after Bella's creation:

- Bai still has **five full historical cores**;
- Bella has **four separate pseudo-cores**.

This distinction remains important in Volume Three, where Bella returns still carrying the identity and power structure Bai created for her.

### 10. Otto's core accounting has two different moments and only one authoritative final total

Otto's hostage strategy forces Bai to begin physically extracting his own cores. The first explicitly removed core is **Conquest/Thunder**.

This intermediate scene can make a naive arithmetic reconstruction produce five cores in Otto's possession if later remnants are added mechanically.

The novel itself supplies the authoritative final ledger in chapter 51:

> **after Bai's historical body disappears, exactly four cracked Herrscher cores/gems remain as Otto's final gain.**

Therefore future bookkeeping must distinguish:

- **intermediate extraction/possession during the hostage confrontation**
from
- **Otto's net final historical haul after Bai's death**.

The final answer is **four**, because chapter 51 directly says so.

Do not “correct” the novel into five by summing scenes that the story itself resolves differently.

### 11. Battle Continuation does not heal Bai; it lets a dead historical body finish its work

After the fission barrage and the Buluoke interception, Bai's body has effectively reached death.

**Battle Continuation EX** then grants approximately five additional hours of functional action.

This should not be translated as regeneration or revival.

The correct model is:

> **the historical body is already medically/existentially dead; the temporary trait keeps it operational until the remaining duration expires.**

That borrowed time is what lets Bai:

- settle Sirin and the girls' future;
- establish Cecilia's maternal bond;
- order Bella to live freely;
- assault Schicksal infrastructure;
- attempt to remove biological Kiana from Otto's reach;
- heal Kiana after the HOMU bomb;
- force Otto's Kallen oath;
- and leave the altered era with his final promise intact.

When the trait expires, the historical body dissolves.

Current-Era Bai survives because the system had already guaranteed before the traversal that **historical death does not kill his present self**.

### 12. Chapter 52 is both a return and a settlement screen

Bai returns to exactly the point from which he departed.

Although he lived for months in 1999–2000, **less than one second has passed in Mei's home**.

Three different things happen at once:

1. his Current-Era body returns to its normal teenage form;
2. the Honkai corruption that was about to turn him into a deadwalker is completely gone;
3. the system settles the historical run into permanent rewards.

The settlement explicitly gives:

- fragmentary Void;
- fragmentary Death;
- fragmentary Flame;
- fragmentary Wind;
- fragmentary Thunder;
- fused Schariac + Kaslana stigmata;
- Schariac holy blood;
- Kaslana constitution/physical quality;
- Super Gaming Talent;
- personal-limit breakthrough;
- absolute Honkai-energy adaptability;
- and additional minor rewards indicated by the interface's ellipsis.

The system itself explains the reward logic:

- the **five fragmentary authorities** derive from Bai having become a five-core Herrscher;
- the stigmata/blood/body/miscellaneous traits are drawn from the people with whom he formed strong bonds, including **Sirin, Cecilia and biological Kiana/Durandal**.

Thus chapter 52 establishes a general rule later traversals reuse:

> **the system does not simply return whatever historical body Bai happened to possess; it converts historical achievements and relationships into a curated permanent Current-Era package.**

### 13. Historical full cores and permanent fragments are intentionally different layers

This is the single most important power-bookkeeping rule entering Volume Two.

**Historical Bai:**
- five full physical cores;
- enormous core-backed energy;
- run-specific temporary traits;
- historical Herrscher body.

**Current-Era Bai after chapter 52:**
- five **fragmentary** authorities;
- fused stigmata/bloodline/body rewards;
- absolute Honkai adaptability;
- stronger physical potential;
- no five-core historical body;
- no automatic retention of the six temporary traversal traits.

Later volumes add complete Quantum, Time, full physical cores, Blank-Key states and other power layers on top of this starting package. They do not retroactively make chapter-52 Bai a full five-core Current-Era Herrscher.

### 14. The rewritten Second Eruption becomes the real past of the Current Era

Bai does not return to an untouched 2014 that merely gives him personal memories of an alternate branch.

The historical rewrite becomes shared history.

Consequences include:

- Sirin survives.
- Avrora, Agata and Galina survive.
- Cecilia survives.
- Siegfried survives and breaks with Otto.
- Theresa remembers Babylon and Otto's betrayal.
- Welt/Anti-Entropy remember Bai's historical Herrscher.
- many Valkyries survive because Bai shielded them.
- Alexandra survives and later gives birth to Bronya.
- Cheng Lixue survives the altered Second-Eruption outcome.
- Bella survives as Bai's created family member.
- Otto retains historical Herrscher material and a radically altered strategic history.
- biological Kiana remains alive under Otto's custody/protection conditions rather than following the exact original route.

The Current Era therefore has a new historical foundation before Volume Two begins.

### 15. Corrective adaptation preserves important people without restoring their original causes

A potential paradox appears immediately:

> If Sirin never became the original catastrophic Second Herrscher, why does K-423 still exist in 2014?

Volume Two later gives the explicit rule: important people/events can exert enough causal weight that history reconstructs a **different path** to preserve them.

K-423 is the cleanest example.

Original-like causal recipe:
> biological Kiana/Kaslana material + Sirin/HoV material.

Rewritten recipe:
> biological Kiana/Durandal's Kaslana material + **Bai Ge's historical HoV material**.

The existence of a familiar person therefore does **not** prove familiar history survived unchanged.

This rule also explains why Alexandra can survive while Bronya still eventually becomes the Ural Silver Wolf through a different kidnapping/child-soldier route.

Volume One creates these altered causes; Volume Two names the mechanism.

### 16. Rewritten historical memory persists, but cross-era identity recognition is separately protected

A second apparent paradox is:

> If Welt, Theresa, Otto, Siegfried and others remember historical Bai Ge, why do they not all immediately identify teenage Current-Era Bai?

Volume Three supplies the missing system rule.

The system protects the cognitive link:

> **present Bai Ge = historical traversal Bai Ge**

without deleting the underlying history.

People can still:

- remember the historical Herrscher;
- remember what he did;
- possess records/footage;
- experience familiarity;
- react to his old powers.

But ordinary cognition is pushed away from automatically completing the identity equation.

Enough evidence can break through. Otto eventually does so in Volume Three, then incorrectly concludes that Bai must possess a Sentience-like cognition power because Otto still cannot perceive the system itself.

This is a **later-confirmed clarification** of Volume One, not knowledge that the 2000 cast consciously has during the volume.

### 17. Volume One's causal model, carried forward

By chapter 52 the novel has established the following system/history model:

| Rule | Meaning established by Volume One |
|---|---|
| **Current identity fabrication** | The system can manufacture a legitimate social/legal starting identity for Bai. |
| **Local historical identity/body** | A traversal can place Bai into an age/body/background appropriate to the target era. |
| **Historical-body protection** | Death in the traversal does not kill Bai's proper Current-Era existence. |
| **Weak corrective leverage over Bai** | The source explicitly says historical correction affects Bai unusually little, making him capable of breaking outcomes ordinary people cannot. |
| **External-time suspension/compression** | Months in history can pass while less than one second passes at Bai's Current-Era departure point. |
| **Temporary traits** | The system can grant run-only capabilities specifically shaped for the scenario. |
| **Real-history propagation** | Bai's interventions become the shared past, not a private dream branch. |
| **Causal substitution** | Bai repeatedly moves a tragedy's cost from the intended victim onto himself; Volume Two later formalizes this as a way to defeat fixed fate. |
| **Corrective adaptation — explicitly named later** | History may preserve important people/events through new causes instead of restoring the old cause. |
| **Historical reward conversion** | Powers achieved and relationships formed during the run can become permanent Current-Era authorities/body traits/rewards. |
| **Cross-era identity protection — explicitly clarified later** | Historical memories remain, while automatic recognition that current Bai is the same traversal figure can be suppressed. |

This is the foundation on which Volume Two's targeted traversal and explicit fate mechanics are built.

## Bai Ge's arrival, Mei, Kiana, and the aborted Third Eruption

### The protagonist and his starting situation

Bai Ge's story begins in his original world with an act that establishes essentially his entire character arc: **he dies saving a little girl from an oncoming bus**. He subsequently awakens on a rooftop, hears the familiar “Captain on the bridge” reference, and discovers that he has transmigrated into the world of *Honkai Impact 3rd*. His system identifies the location and time as **Nagazora City in 2014, shortly before the Third Eruption**. Unlike many system protagonists, Bai Ge does **not** immediately receive overwhelming combat power; the system's main package is still loading, leaving him physically an ordinary human with no useful Honkai resistance.

The system does, however, fabricate a legitimate identity and background for him. Bai Ge deliberately chooses to be a **wealthy orphan and transfer student at Chiba Academy**, giving himself financial independence without parents whose existence would complicate the transmigration. He is white-haired and golden-eyed, is treated as exceptionally attractive, and rapidly receives the school nickname roughly equivalent to “Chiba Academy's Prince.” More importantly, he possesses his old-world knowledge of *Honkai Impact 3rd*. He knows what will happen to Kiana, Mei, Himeko, the Flame-Chasers and many others, and almost immediately decides that merely surviving is unacceptable: he wants to **change the tragedies he remembers**.

This attitude is the thematic core of Volume One. Bai Ge is not primarily motivated by defeating Honkai or becoming powerful. His repeated instinct is **to place himself between another person and the tragedy that should happen to them**. The story then repeatedly tests how far he is willing to take that principle.

### Raiden Mei before the eruption

At Chiba Academy he encounters **Raiden Mei**, seventeen years old and already living through the collapse of her former life. Her father, Raiden Ryoma, has been arrested following the scandal surrounding ME Corp, the family's status has collapsed, and the classmates who once surrounded Mei because of her wealth and social position have turned against her. She is isolated and regularly bullied.

Bai Ge's first major intervention is deceptively small: **he speaks normally and kindly to her**. Because practically everyone around Mei has either abandoned or targeted her, ordinary decency has an outsized emotional impact. When Bai Ge later hears that girls are harassing her on the rooftop, he rushes there and physically gets between Mei and her tormentors, taking a bucket of filthy water that was intended for her. He condemns the girls for attacking Mei over accusations against her father and reminds them that many of them had previously benefited from their relationship with her.

Mei responds by giving Bai Ge her homemade lunch, establishing a motif that remains important: **Mei's cooking becomes one of the first symbols of “home” in Bai Ge's new life**. Bai Ge, who approached her with foreknowledge of what she would suffer, increasingly stops thinking of her as merely a game character he must save and begins responding to the real person in front of him.

Outside school, Bai Ge investigates the situation. He obtains a phone, computer and other necessities, observes ME Corp, and concludes that **Cocolia's people have effectively taken control of the company**. Bai Ge knows that Raiden Ryoma's supposed financial wrongdoing is entangled with anti-Herrscher research and Cocolia's scheme rather than being a straightforward case of personal corruption.

The bullying then reaches a dangerous turning point. After swimming class, Mei's clothes are stolen and she is locked in the women's changing room wearing only her swimsuit. Her despair produces visible purple electricity: **the power inside her is beginning to answer her negative emotions**. Bai Ge arrives before the situation can become a full awakening, kicks open the door, covers her with his coat, and comforts her. Mei clings to him and, significantly, allows him to begin calling her simply **“Mei” rather than “Raiden-san/classmate Raiden.”**

### The fake relationship that becomes emotionally real

Realizing that he cannot physically be beside Mei every moment, Bai Ge develops a social deterrent. Because he is one of the most popular students in school, he proposes that he and Mei **pretend to be dating**. Anyone targeting Mei would then be directly challenging him and risking social backlash. Mei agrees. In return, and also simply because she wants to, she continues making his lunches.

Bai Ge then announces publicly in class that **Raiden Mei is his girlfriend** and explicitly warns everyone not to bully her. He also makes the girls responsible for the changing-room incident apologize. The arrangement works: harassment falls drastically, although it does not disappear entirely.

The crucial asymmetry is that while Bai Ge continues calling the relationship a protective fiction, **Mei's feelings stop being fictional very quickly**. Alone at home, she repeatedly thinks about his actions and begins developing unmistakable romantic attachment. This matters later when Cocolia's operatives deliberately weaponize the ambiguity of the fake relationship against her.

### Kiana Kaslana joins the group

The next major person to enter Bai Ge's life is **Kiana Kaslana**, appearing as a first-year transfer student. Bai Ge immediately recognizes her. Kiana independently encounters classmates bullying Mei and intervenes herself, demonstrating that she is already the kind of person who will throw herself into danger for a stranger.

Bai Ge initially introduces himself as Mei's boyfriend, but he soon privately explains to Kiana that the relationship is fake and exists to protect Mei. Kiana accepts the arrangement and begins helping him protect her. The result is the creation of Volume One's first genuine “family” unit: **Bai Ge, Mei and Kiana begin spending their lunches together, joking, eating Mei's cooking and gradually trusting one another.**

Kiana is not merely at Chiba Academy for school. She has been investigating Honkai-related activity and is secretly fighting Honkai beasts and zombies at night while also pursuing clues concerning **Siegfried Kaslana**. Bai Ge discovers her injuries and tends to them. His attitude toward her is unusually protective because his meta-knowledge means that every time he sees the cheerful girl in front of him he is also thinking about the suffering awaiting Kiana/K-423 in the original story.

The trio's intimacy grows through deliberately mundane scenes. Mei feeds them; Bai Ge and Kiana even fall asleep with their heads in Mei's lap. Mei, however, begins noticing that Bai Ge's warmth is not exclusive to her. She experiences jealousy toward Kiana for the first time, but makes an important moral decision: **she will not turn her love for Bai Ge into possessiveness that damages her friendship with Kiana.**

At the same time, another voice has begun appearing inside Mei. It has a more imperious, “queen-like” personality and mocks Mei's weakness and tendency to cry. This is not ultimately treated by the fanfic as an ordinary Honkai-created Herrscher persona. It will be identified as **the awakened consciousness of Mei's stigmata**, which has interacted abnormally with the Gem of Conquest.

Bai Ge also notices that the remaining bullies are strangely persistent. Even after being humiliated repeatedly, they continue targeting Mei. He gives Mei noise-canceling headphones so that she can block their insults. The story then reveals what Bai Ge suspects: **the bullies are being paid by an unknown adult woman to keep Mei psychologically distressed and are themselves being threatened if they stop.**

That woman is tied to Cocolia.

### Infiltrating ME Corp and discovering Project-MEI

After discovering Kiana's nightly Honkai activities, Bai Ge recruits her into a break-in at **ME Corp**. Kiana handles physical security while Bai Ge uses his story knowledge and observations to direct their search. Deep inside the facility they find an armory containing three weapons associated here with Raiden Ryoma's preparations: **Pulse Katana Type-19, Ion Resonance Greatsword, and Plasma Kagehide**.

More importantly, they find a recorded message from **Raiden Ryoma**. The fanfic's version of events is that Mei possessed or awakened a dangerous stigmata. Under a proposal originating with Cocolia, **Project-MEI implanted the Gem of Conquest into Mei**, ostensibly to suppress the stigmata. Cocolia's real objective was to turn Mei into the vessel for the Herrscher of Thunder and use her to generate the Third Eruption. Ryoma eventually understood that he had been manipulated and diverted enormous ME Corp resources into weapons and anti-Herrscher preparations intended to stop the coming disaster and save his daughter. Cocolia then exploited those financial irregularities to remove him.

This discovery changes the trio's problem from “protect Mei from school bullying” to **“prevent the deliberate creation of the Third Herrscher.”** Bai Ge and Kiana take the weapons and prepare to rescue her. At essentially the same time, Nagazora's Honkai readings begin spiking.

### Cocolia forces the awakening

The narrative then switches to Cocolia's side and confirms the plot. **She deliberately arranged the campaign of harassment against Mei** so that prolonged despair would destabilize her and encourage Herrscher awakening through the Gem of Conquest. Her underlying objective is to obtain a controllable Herrscher, whose power she hopes can ultimately assist with rescuing her daughter from the Sea of Quanta. Bai Ge and Kiana's friendship has wrecked her psychological strategy by giving Mei reasons to live, so Cocolia escalates to kidnapping.

Mei is abducted and held in an abandoned building. Her captors deliberately attack the emotional foundations Bai Ge and Kiana created. One of the principal bullies tells Mei that everybody knows her relationship with Bai Ge is fake and suggests that Bai Ge may actually prefer Kiana. The tactic nearly strikes exactly where Cocolia wants—but Mei reaches a very important conclusion. **She admits internally that she loves Bai Ge, but refuses to hate Kiana or destroy their friendship over him.** Bai Ge and Kiana are the two people who brought light back into her life; she will not answer that kindness with betrayal.

The captors then destroy Bai Ge's gifted headphones, reveal more of Cocolia's role in framing Ryoma and orchestrating the bullying, and threaten to maim or kill Bai Ge and Kiana. That final threat changes the direction of Mei's emotions. Until this point she has primarily wished for someone to save her. Now she desperately wants **the power to save them**.

The internal queen answers.

Mei's stigmata consciousness seizes control, manifests overwhelming lightning power, kills the principal tormentor and destroys Cocolia's operatives. The important distinction for later continuity is that this entity is **not simply “evil Herrscher Mei.”** She regards herself as Mei, possesses her own temperament, and is fiercely interested in Bai Ge's choice.

### Bai Ge chooses Mei over “the world”

Bai Ge and Kiana reach the site after Mei's transformation. The Thunder Queen watches them because she wants an answer to a classic Herrscher dilemma: now that Mei is apparently an enemy of humanity, will the people who claimed to care for her immediately choose “saving the world” over saving her?

Bai Ge charges with a sword and initially looks as though he is attacking Mei. In reality he has noticed **a Titan mech concealed through quantum-related technology behind her**. He pushes or embraces Mei out of the attack line, intercepts the Titan, and fights it despite still being an ordinary human. The weapon he actually uses in this fight is the **Pulse Katana Type-19**, even though earlier discussion around the armory can make it sound as though another weapon might become his preferred choice.

Bai Ge exploits the Thunder authority's electromagnetic effects to interfere with the Titan and attacks its vulnerable eye/core area. Kiana, wielding the **Ion Resonance Greatsword**, destroys additional Titans. When Cocolia deploys still more machines, the Thunder Queen ends the fight by destroying them with mass lightning.

She then pins Bai Ge against a wall and openly identifies herself as a Herrscher-like agent of Honkai. She essentially asks whether he now regrets saving her.

He does not.

Bai Ge insists that whatever power or identity she carries, she is still **Raiden Mei and his companion**. His position is not an abstract declaration that Herrschers are harmless; it is the much more personal claim that he knows the person before him and will not reduce her to a category. He even treats annihilating humanity as far less important than returning home and eating dinner together.

Immediately afterward, Bai Ge collapses. This reveals the cost of everything he has just done: **he has essentially zero natural Honkai adaptability** and has been standing in an environment saturated with enough Honkai energy to produce a Herrscher.

For the Thunder Queen, this resolves the test. Bai Ge was given the apparent choice between the world and Mei and chose Mei without hesitation. She therefore makes her own reciprocal choice: **she chooses Bai Ge and Mei's human life over the mission imposed by Honkai**. She relinquishes the enormous Honkai-energy accumulation, gives up active Herrscher authority, and returns control of the body to ordinary Mei. Nagazora's Honkai concentration falls out of the catastrophic range and Cocolia's planned Third Eruption is aborted.

The fanfic explains the abnormality through Mei's stigmata. The Gem of Conquest was supposed to suppress the stigmata while facilitating Herrscher creation, but the stigmata consciousness instead effectively **invaded the gem's role and displaced the expected Honkai-generated personality**. The text also invokes the special legacy associated with **Elysia**, allowing a human-aligned will to reject Honkai's command. This is fanfic cosmology and should be preserved as such rather than silently rewritten to match game canon.

### The wider factions react

The failed eruption immediately attracts every major faction.

**Cocolia** is furious. Her controlled Third Eruption has collapsed because Mei formed emotional connections she could not break. She orders **Bronya** toward Nagazora and remains interested in obtaining the Third Herrscher later.

**Otto Apocalypse** reacts very differently. He interprets what happened to Mei as proof that a Herrscher consciousness can choose human emotional bonds over Honkai. This is extraordinarily valuable to his plans surrounding **K-423/Kiana**. Bai Ge is therefore no longer merely a random civilian associated with Kiana; Otto regards his emotional influence as something that could help K-423 overcome the Herrscher of the Void personality. Otto instructs Amber to contact Theresa and draw attention to Nagazora.

**World Serpent** also notices. Gray Serpent has Raven observe rather than expose the organization, making Bai Ge and the Nagazora anomaly an object of surveillance. The story associates Raven here with **Seven Thunders of Retribution**.

### The victory hides Bai Ge's impending death

Back at Mei's home, the immediate crisis seems over. Mei sees Ryoma's recording and learns why she was targeted. She is horrified to discover that people died while the other consciousness controlled her body, but Bai Ge and Kiana emphasize that she had been kidnapped, psychologically tortured and threatened and that Cocolia's operatives created the situation.

The three then do exactly what Bai Ge had promised the Thunder Queen: **they go home and live normally**. They eat Mei's cooking, play games and have a sleepover. Bai Ge gives Mei the **Pulse Katana Type-19** so she will have a means of protecting herself. The ordinary domestic atmosphere is deliberately contrasted with the catastrophe they just prevented.

But Bai Ge is secretly dying. The enormous Honkai exposure has begun turning his body toward a zombie/deadwalker state. He has no innate resistance sufficient to stop it and estimates that he will lose himself before morning. Neither Mei nor Kiana realizes how severe his condition is.

At precisely this point, the system finally finishes loading.

Its true central function is a **Past Simulator**: Bai Ge can enter earlier historical periods physically, receive temporary random traits appropriate to the run, form real relationships and alter events. The crucial rule is that these simulations are **not disposable dreams**. Changes can propagate into history. Conversely, dying during a simulated past does not kill his original present-day body. This mechanic becomes the foundation of the rest of the novel.

Faced with certain death in the present, Bai Ge initiates his first run.

The selected scenario is **the Second Eruption and Babylon Labs**.

## The Babylon Labs rewrite and Bai Ge's transformation into the Second Herrscher

### The simulator's temporary traits

The system equips Bai Ge with an extraordinary set of traits for the Second Eruption run. The exact concepts matter because they explain virtually every impossible feat he performs later:

| Simulator trait | Practical effect in Volume One |
|---|---|
| **Honkai Energy Adaptability EX** | Honkai compatibility exceeding ordinary human and Valkyrie limits; allows him to survive Babylon's experiments and ultimately Herrscherize. |
| **“Honkai's Biological Son” EX** | Makes him extraordinarily likely to be selected or recognized as a Herrscher candidate. |
| **Pain Insensitivity B** | Greatly reduces perceived pain. |
| **Extreme Pain Insensitivity A** | Further extreme reduction in pain perception. |
| **Battle Continuation EX** | Allows his body to continue acting after what would otherwise be death; becomes decisive at the end of the simulation. |
| **Unyielding Will A** | Helps him preserve his identity and overpower the newly generated Herrscher personality. |

The system explicitly reassures him that **death in the past scenario will not kill the present Bai Ge**. It adds two equally important protections: **historical correction has minimal influence over him**, and however long he remains in the historical traversal, the Current-Era time to which he returns will still be the departure moment. It also provides a local historical identity/body so that he can exist naturally within the era.

Those three guarantees—historical-body safety, low corrective leverage, and external-time suspension—are what make Bai willing to enter a scenario he openly expects may kill the traversal body.

There is a small text-continuity issue useful for translators: one line around the scenario setup uses **白枫** instead of **白歌**. Context makes it overwhelmingly clear that this is a stray naming/typing error rather than a separate person. It should not be introduced as a new character.

### Babylon Labs and the four girls

Bai Ge appears as a child test subject at **Babylon Labs in Siberia**, a Schicksal facility outwardly associated with medical/Honkai research but internally conducting brutal human experimentation related to artificial stigmata. He is designated **X1** and housed with four girls central to the rewritten history:

**Sirin (西琳), Avrora (阿芙罗拉), Agata (阿加塔), and Galina (加莉娜).**

The simulator has inserted Bai Ge into their history using fabricated memories presenting him as a long-standing childhood companion. Initially, Bai Ge himself feels a subtle emotional distance because he knows those memories are artificial. The girls do not; to them, his existence and their relationship with him are completely genuine.

The researchers quickly discover that Bai Ge's body can tolerate absurd quantities of Honkai energy. Instead of seeing this as reason to protect him, they identify him as a uniquely valuable experimental specimen. Bai Ge recognizes the leverage that gives him.

He makes a bargain through extreme coercion: **stop experimenting on Sirin, Avrora, Agata and Galina, and use him instead.** Because the laboratory values him so highly, he threatens to destroy the specimen they want—by smashing his head into a wall, biting his tongue or otherwise committing suicide—whenever the researchers refuse his demands.

The bargain works.

### Bai Ge turns himself into the price for everyone else's safety

The girls initially do not understand what Bai Ge has done. They simply discover that they are no longer being summoned for experiments. Bai Ge tells them that they will eventually escape and promises that no matter what happens, **he will make sure they live**.

The next morning they see him being taken away instead and understand the cost: Bai Ge has effectively **offered his own body to Babylon in exchange for theirs**.

He then exploits his value even further. After each experiment he demands small improvements: soft beds, thick blankets, warm and nutritious food, winter clothing, a night-light because Galina is afraid of the dark, books because Sirin has nightmares, and similar comforts. The purchasing pattern becomes so bizarre that **Otto himself notices Babylon Labs suddenly ordering children's necessities rather than its usual experimental supplies and wonders why the facility appears to have developed a conscience.**

These requests are not comic filler. They establish the precise way Sirin and the others come to understand Bai Ge later: **everything resembling a normal childhood inside Babylon was purchased with his body**.

The researchers resent being manipulated by a child and retaliate by escalating the experiments while observing only one rule: do not kill their valuable X1 specimen. Bai Ge endures cutting, burning, freezing, electrocution, Honkai-energy injections and progressively more destructive procedures. His pain-reduction traits make survival psychologically possible, while Honkai Adaptability EX prevents the energy itself from simply destroying him.

As a direct consequence, **Sirin, Avrora, Agata and Galina remain alive**. In the fanfic's rewritten Second Eruption, the suffering that would have fallen on several children is concentrated almost entirely on Bai Ge.

The five children become a family. Their beds are pushed together; the girls sleep around him; Bai Ge learns their individual personalities. Agata is comparatively lively and energetic, Avrora gentler, Galina timid, and Sirin especially perceptive of Bai Ge's condition and motives.

Eventually they see his body without his usual attempt to conceal the damage. They discover the accumulated wounds and realize the horrifying truth: their food, blankets, books and safety exist because Bai Ge repeatedly allowed adults to torture him. All four beg him to stop. They offer to return everything and even volunteer to become test subjects again if that will spare him.

That reaction eliminates Bai Ge's lingering psychological distance. He acknowledges that although the simulator fabricated their supposed shared childhood, **the feelings they have built since his arrival are real**. He stops thinking of them as characters he is saving because he knows the plot and accepts them straightforwardly as family.

He promises that when they escape they will live together somewhere **warm, bright and filled with flowers**. That promised home becomes Sirin's central emotional dream and remains important through the end of the volume.

### The experiments are also part of Bai Ge's plan

Bai Ge nevertheless refuses to stop the experiments, and there is another reason beyond shielding the girls.

He has realized that Babylon is unwittingly giving him the ingredients required for **Herrscherization**: extraordinary Honkai adaptability, vast quantities of accumulated Honkai energy, and more than enough genuine negative emotion produced by torture and hatred. He deliberately keeps enduring until those conditions mature.

His physical condition finally collapses. He can barely walk back to the cell. Sirin and the others begin physically attacking guards with their bare hands whenever anyone attempts to take Bai Ge away, despite having no realistic chance of success.

Babylon eventually raises an injection to **500 HW**, with Bai Ge already at approximately ninety percent Honkai erosion. The researchers expect him either to die or become a zombie.

Instead his body begins regenerating.

The facility's instruments register his internal Honkai level soaring to approximately **2,000 HW**, spatial distortion appears throughout the laboratory, and the terrified researchers realize X1 is **becoming a Herrscher**.

### Bai Ge becomes the Herrscher of the Void

Herrscherization reconstructs Bai Ge's body, eliminating his accumulated injuries and foreign material. A Herrscher-of-the-Void personality does emerge, but **Unyielding Will A**, together with the fanfic's invoked blessing/legacy of Elysia, allows Bai Ge's own personality to suppress it and take control of the authority.

**Later-confirmed cosmology:** Volume Four establishes that Elysia/Origin's historical miracle is what gives later Herrschers the possibility of retaining humanity and resisting Honkai's absolute command. Much later prose also explicitly describes Origin/Elysia's influence as protecting Bai's human nature against the pressure of multiple Herrscher cores. This later explanation clarifies the mechanism operating here; it should not be back-written as knowledge Bai or Babylon already possesses in this scene.

He emerges with white hair, golden eyes, Herrscher armor, golden wings and authority over space. The narration conceptually calls him a current-era **“Herrscher of Human”**, meaning a Herrscher whose human self controls the power rather than serving Honkai. This should not be mistaken for a separate standard canonical Herrscher title; it is the fanfiction's Elysia-inspired classification of Bai Ge's condition.

His very first large-scale use of the Void authority establishes that he has not become a destroyer. Rather than immediately killing the scientists, **he opens portals in Babylon's cells and evacuates the child test subjects outside**. To the abused children, the white-haired, golden-winged figure who has opened their prisons looks literally like an angel.

Babylon's Schicksal guards respond with force. Dozens of Valkyries and close to a hundred Schicksal combat machines deploy against him even though the rescued children are immediately nearby. Missiles are launched toward Bai Ge. He opens spatial portals, swallows the incoming barrage, then redirects the same missiles back into the Schicksal force, devastating the machines and guard units.

**Benares**, his newly born accompanying Judgment-class Honkai beast, then arrives. Bai Ge orders the dragon into battle, and its Honkai-energy breath wipes out the remaining opposition and tears through part of Babylon Tower.

Bai Ge sends the general population of rescued child experiments to safer towns across Siberia. He keeps Sirin, Avrora, Agata and Galina together in a protected cave behind an Imaginary/spatial barrier and promises Sirin he will come back.

He then returns to Babylon alone and turns the experiments back upon their creators. He deliberately subjects the researchers to Honkai-energy corruption and the kinds of suffering they inflicted on children. By the time he leaves, those responsible for his and the girls' torture have been dealt with, and later he destroys the Babylon facility itself.

### The moon and the five-core Herrscher

Instead of hiding, Bai Ge makes a highly informed meta-knowledge move: **he takes Benares to the Moon**.

He reaches the Previous Era lunar ruins and finds the fanfic's cache of Honkai/Herrscher power left from the battle against the Final Herrscher. Contact places him before an entity presented as the **Will/God of Honkai**, which supplies him with Previous Era information. Bai Ge then absorbs four additional authorities/cores.

Together with his original **Void core**, he now carries five full historical cores in total. The four Moon additions correspond to the fanfic's versions of the **Gem of Conquest, Gem of Desire, Gem of Serenity, and Gem of Haste**:

| Authority / core | Typical applications shown in Volume One |
|---|---|
| **Void** | Portals, spatial displacement, Imaginary barriers, subspace lances, extreme mobility. |
| **Thunder — Gem of Conquest (征服宝石)** | Electromagnetic attacks, electricity, railgun-like strikes and shields. |
| **Wind — Gem of Desire (渴望宝石)** | Control of air and “ideal fluid,” defensive barriers, pressure, blades and tornadoes. |
| **Death — Gem of Serenity (静谧宝石)** | Biological/cellular manipulation, killing or healing tissue, creating a human body for Bella. |
| **Flame — Gem of Haste (疾疫宝石)** | Extreme heat/fire and manipulation related to molecular motion. |

The text therefore calls Bai Ge a **five-core Herrscher**.

Although he briefly becomes intoxicated by his new strength, he literally forces himself to calm down and reminds himself that beings such as Fu Hua remain dangerous. Bai Ge's meta-knowledge is important to his combat style: unlike the stereotypically arrogant Herrscher, he knows exactly how often overconfidence gets powerful characters killed.

He also has no intention whatsoever of performing Honkai's expected mission. The consciousness associated with Honkai may want him to destroy human civilization; Bai Ge simply refuses.

### Benares becomes Bella

Bai Ge then changes Benares herself.

Instead of following the original-history pattern of distributing pseudo-Herrscher power among several servants, Bai Ge **creates four pseudo-cores from his own Honkai energy—Flame, Death, Wind and Thunder—and gives all four to Benares**. These are newly created **pseudo-cores**, not the four full Moon cores being removed from Bai Ge. He also uses Death authority to create a human female body for the dragon. The resulting being possesses her original Judgment-class Honkai-beast power, four pseudo-Herrscher authorities, intelligence and a human form.

Bai Ge names her **Bella (贝拉)**.

Bella pledges absolute loyalty to Bai Ge, but his later treatment of that loyalty is critical: **he does not regard her as property**. By the end of the volume he explicitly calls her family and eventually orders her to live freely rather than die following him.

On the return to Earth, Schicksal/Anti-Entropy observation forces detect Bai Ge and Bella. A group of supersonic fighters attacks. Bai Ge freezes the surrounding air with Wind authority, traps the aircraft, and then **crushes the planes together with their pilots** through increasing air pressure. The scene is intentionally cruel: Bai Ge has no plan to exterminate humanity, but he is willing to kill attackers he judges culpable and even make them regret attacking him. He subsequently returns to Babylon and uses Thunder authority in a massive electromagnetic attack to destroy the remaining tower.

He reunites with Sirin's group, introduces Bella to them, and renews the promise that they will find somewhere warm and beautiful to live. Sirin immediately displays some jealousy toward the older-looking, beautiful Bella, reinforcing how emotionally attached she has become to Bai Ge.

## Schicksal's pursuit, Otto's hostage strategy, and the rewritten end of the Second Eruption

### Welt and Otto independently decide Bai Ge must be approached

The rise of a human-minded Second Herrscher changes the strategic situation for every organization.

At **Anti-Entropy**, Welt Yang and Einstein review recordings of Bai Ge. They recognize that he unquestionably possesses human emotions because he prioritized protecting Babylon's children. Einstein judges him as dangerous but potentially approachable rather than inherently hostile. Welt decides to contact him personally in the hope that the world may now have **another Herrscher standing on humanity's side**. Tesla prepares Titans as emergency support.

At **Schicksal**, Otto reaches a more self-interested conclusion. Video evidence shows Bai Ge using Void, Wind and Thunder authorities, while his trip to the Moon explains the sudden expansion of abilities. Otto immediately wonders whether Bai Ge also possesses the **Death authority**, because that power might provide a route to restoring Kallen. He therefore expands the force intended to meet or capture Bai Ge: Siegfried, Theresa and Cecilia are not enough; he also contacts Fu Hua and prepares Schicksal's larger military resources and his strongest Soulium body.

### Alexandra and unborn Bronya

Before either faction reaches him, Bai Ge's group enters a poor Siberian town searching for food. He refuses Bella's suggestion that they simply steal supplies from ordinary people, explaining his moral line: **people who deliberately hurt the innocent can be treated ruthlessly, but uninvolved civilians are not targets**.

There they encounter a pregnant woman who sees the hungry children and, despite having limited supplies herself, offers them canned food. She introduces herself as **Alexandra Pavlovna Zaychik**, Bronya's mother. Bai Ge realizes that the unborn child is Bronya.

Sirin and the other Babylon children are initially afraid of Alexandra simply because she is an adult; Babylon has taught them to equate “adult” with “person who hurts us.” Her unconditional maternal kindness is therefore their first major experience of benevolence from an adult other than Bai Ge. They become attached to her extremely quickly.

Before leaving, Bai Ge quietly uses **Death authority in its life-giving direction to improve Alexandra's physical condition**. He treats the act as repayment for the food she shared and as a blessing for both the pregnant mother and unborn Bronya. Alexandra does not know what he has done at the time, but later notices that her previous minor health problems have disappeared. Later continuity confirms that Alexandra survives in the rewritten history.

Bai Ge also encounters Bronya's father and a **much younger Cocolia**. This Cocolia is sharply different from the woman Bai Ge knows in 2014: she is still portrayed as comparatively righteous, opposing illegal conduct and supporting orphanages. Bai Ge briefly considers killing her because he knows what she eventually becomes, but refuses because doing so could also erase or endanger the futures of the children she will later take into her orphanage. This is an early explicit example of Bai Ge recognizing that **meta-knowledge does not make the consequences of preemptive murder simple**.

### Siegfried attacks first

Bai Ge keeps moving because he expects Schicksal and Anti-Entropy to converge on him. His Void authority allows frequent teleportation, while Flame authority lets him keep a warm environment around the young girls despite the Siberian winter.

**Siegfried Kaslana** finds them first and immediately attacks because he has been briefed to regard the Second Herrscher as a dangerous, violent enemy. His opening shots hit Bai Ge's permanent spatial barrier without effect.

Bai Ge and Bella overwhelm Siegfried. Bella's Judgment-class/pseudo-Herrscher physical strength and Bai Ge's portals allow them to attack him from blind angles, while Bai Ge alternates Thunder, Flame and Void attacks. He deliberately avoids lethal options such as simply using Death authority to kill Siegfried.

Siegfried escalates to the greatsword form of the **Judgment of Shamash**. Bai Ge counters with a Wind-authority “ideal fluid” defensive layer so strong that Siegfried cannot physically reach him.

Finally Siegfried decides that defeating the Second Herrscher before Cecilia arrives is worth his life. His motivation is not pride: he believes Cecilia, still weakened after childbirth, will die if forced to fight Bai Ge. He therefore releases **Shamash's catastrophic power at the risk of burning himself to death**.

Bai Ge survives essentially unharmed by combining Flame authority with multiple overlapping defenses—spatial, Wind and electromagnetic. He respects Siegfried more afterward because he recognizes the same willingness to sacrifice oneself for loved ones that drives Bai Ge himself.

Unfortunately, the arriving Cecilia, Theresa and Fu Hua see a heavily injured Siegfried and Bai Ge holding Shamash.

They assume the worst.

### Bai Ge and Bella versus Fu Hua, Cecilia and Theresa

The resulting battle is one of Volume One's largest direct character fights.

Bai Ge recognizes **Fu Hua** as the overwhelmingly dangerous opponent and immediately abandons any temptation to fight her at close range. He repeatedly teleports away while bombarding her with electromagnetic attacks, Wind blades, extreme heat, subspace lances and other ranged Herrscher techniques. His combat advantage comes less from raw superiority than from knowing Fu Hua's strengths beforehand and refusing to play into them.

Bella simultaneously fights **Cecilia and Theresa**. With four pseudo-cores, her Judgment-class base and Bai Ge occasionally teleporting her to favorable positions, Bella holds an advantage even against the two high-level Schicksal combatants.

Bai Ge realizes that Fu Hua can be fought if he never allows her to close the distance. He is especially worried about **Fenghuang Down and Fu Hua's Taixuan Sword God–type ultimate attack**; his contingency plan is characteristically pragmatic rather than heroic—if she uses it, he intends to teleport far enough away, potentially as far as the Moon.

Cecilia intervenes against him using **Abyss Flower** and her Schariac holy blood. The holy blood is especially dangerous because it neutralizes Honkai energy and therefore strips away Bai Ge's Honkai-based defenses. Cecilia nearly creates a decisive opening.

Even then, Bai Ge avoids killing her. A subspace lance could have struck Cecilia at point-blank range; instead he uses his power to throw her away from danger. Fu Hua notices the pattern: Bai Ge's attacks are strong, but he is deliberately aiming to disable limbs or force retreats rather than hit vital areas.

Welt Yang then arrives with a pseudo **Star of Eden**, turning what looks like a three-sided standoff into an opportunity to finally talk. Cecilia lowers Abyss Flower first. Theresa and Fu Hua follow. Bai Ge immediately stops fighting as well, explaining that he never wanted this battle in the first place.

Siegfried then has to admit that **he attacked Bai Ge first**.

### Babylon's truth turns Schicksal's heroes against Otto

When the Schicksal group asks why Bai Ge destroyed Babylon, he reveals what the facility actually was.

Theresa has apparently been told Babylon was associated with legitimate research and treatment. Bai Ge describes the artificial-stigmata human experiments, the children being treated as expendable laboratory animals, and how his own Herrscher awakening resulted from Schicksal scientists torturing and saturating him with Honkai energy. He also points out that he evacuated the surviving children, who can independently corroborate him.

The revelation horrifies Cecilia, Theresa, Siegfried, Fu Hua and Welt. Cecilia, especially affected because she is herself a mother, personally apologizes to Bai Ge for Schicksal's part in what happened. The group concludes that his attack on Babylon was not a Herrscher's indiscriminate assault on humanity but retaliation against the people who created him through atrocities.

Welt had arrived partly hoping to persuade Bai Ge to fight for humanity. Bai Ge refuses—not because he has joined Honkai, but because he believes humanity has no moral right to immediately demand heroic service from a child it tortured into becoming a Herrscher. His position is that Welt was born through experiences that allowed him to believe in humanity, whereas Bai Ge's Herrscher self was **born from humanity's malice**. All Bai Ge wants is to take his family somewhere peaceful and live.

The others reluctantly accept that as more than reasonable.

Then Otto calls.

### Otto learns about the Death authority

Otto introduces himself as Schicksal's Overseer and ultimately acknowledges responsibility for Babylon's existence. Bai Ge reacts with open hatred and insults rather than diplomacy. Otto is not particularly offended; he is more interested in what Bai Ge can do.

Otto eventually asks the real question: **does Bai Ge possess the Herrscher of Death's authority?**

Bai Ge attempts an obvious denial. Otto immediately reads the lie. Bai Ge therefore explains the limitation: Death authority can restore and manipulate biological tissue, potentially rebuilding a dead body, but it **cannot recover a departed soul**. Rebuilding Kallen's body would therefore not resurrect Kallen as Otto understands her.

Otto is devastated but refuses to accept a theoretical answer. If there is even a possibility that research could push the authority further, he wants the Herrscher core itself. Bai Ge refuses to hand it over.

Otto then reveals the real reason he has been confident enough to negotiate with a Herrscher he cannot defeat.

He has **taken Sirin, Avrora, Agata and Galina hostage**.

### Otto attacks Bai Ge's true weakness: people

Otto understands that Bai Ge's humanity is simultaneously his greatest virtue and greatest tactical weakness. Bai Ge could escape almost any physical trap through space manipulation, but he will not gamble with Sirin's life while a weapon is pressed against her head.

The others try to intervene. Otto has prepared for every one of them.

He informs **Welt** that Schicksal launched a major attack against Anti-Entropy minutes earlier. He tells **Fu Hua** that her disciple Cheng Lixue has disappeared during a dangerous mission. He reminds **Cecilia, Siegfried and Theresa** that young Kiana remains on Schicksal's floating headquarters and is therefore within his reach.

In a few sentences Otto effectively immobilizes every major fighter on the field by threatening whatever each values most.

Bai Ge tells them to go. He understands their choice because protecting loved ones is precisely what he has built his own life around. Welt and Fu Hua withdraw to protect Anti-Entropy and Cheng Lixue; the Kaslana/Schariac/Theresa group likewise cannot gamble with Kiana.

Otto orders Bai Ge to remove his Herrscher cores himself.

Bai Ge complies for Sirin's sake and begins physically extracting his own cores, starting with the purple **Gem of Conquest / Thunder core**. The immediate combat consequence is clear: he temporarily loses access to that full Thunder core while Otto next demands the Death core. For end-state bookkeeping, chapter 51 itself is authoritative: it later defines Otto's **net historical haul as exactly four Herrscher cores**, so this intermediate coercion scene should not be arithmetically added on top as a fifth separate final acquisition.

This breaks Sirin emotionally. Once again, the person she loves is mutilating himself because she has become leverage against him. But instead of collapsing into passive despair, she reaches the same psychological turning point that Mei reached earlier in the volume: **if she cannot endure seeing Bai Ge hurt for her, then she must act to protect him, even if acting means dying.**

Sirin deliberately struggles against the weapon at her head. Avrora, Agata and Galina do the same. They are physically incapable of overpowering Otto's Soulium body, but their willingness to die rather than remain useful hostages surprises him for approximately one second.

For the Herrscher of the Void, one second is enough.

Bai Ge teleports the girls out of Otto's control, slices away the weapon-bearing arm with Wind authority and punches Otto's Soulium face inward.

Using Death authority, he temporarily gives Otto's artificial body biological characteristics and deliberately makes its cells repeatedly suffer destruction and regeneration so that Otto actually experiences extreme physical pain. Otto eventually self-destructs that body and transfers his consciousness back to another Soulium body at Schicksal.

### Otto declares Cecilia, Siegfried and Theresa traitors

Otto now abandons negotiation.

He commands his loyal forces to attack Bai Ge and formally brands **Cecilia Schariac, Siegfried Kaslana and Theresa Apocalypse** as having betrayed Schicksal: Theresa is to be captured, while Cecilia and Siegfried can be killed. The three refuse his order to attack Bai Ge after everything they have learned.

Hundreds of A-rank Valkyries and large numbers of Schicksal machines enter battle. Bai Ge, Bella and the former Schicksal combatants dominate the immediate fighting. Bai Ge uses enormous tornadoes, spatial control, flames and other authorities; Bella returns to dragon form and attacks with breath, claws, dives and tail sweeps. Bai Ge is temporarily operating with **four authorities rather than five because the Conquest Gem has already been extracted.**

But the troops are not Otto's true attack.

They are bait intended to keep everyone in one location.

### The fission-missile saturation attack

Otto empties Schicksal's weapons inventories into the area.

Thousands of heavy missiles descend on the battlefield, including **more than a dozen Honkai-energy fission weapons**. The targeting zone is deliberately huge so that simply making a short spatial displacement will not solve the problem. Schicksal's own Valkyries are still inside the impact area; Otto has consciously treated them as expendable.

Bai Ge immediately understands that **he personally can escape**. He can retreat into the Imaginary/spatial domain associated with his Void authority.

But many of the ordinary people around him cannot.

Sirin and her friends cannot safely remain in an Honkai-saturated Imaginary environment. Neither can all the Valkyries—including the same women who were attacking him only moments before—be evacuated in time.

Bai Ge therefore stays.

He expands his spatial and Wind defenses to maximum scale and physically positions himself between everyone below and the incoming arsenal. When he tries to redirect unexploded missiles through portals, Otto remotely detonates them in the air, forcing Bai Ge to absorb the already-released blast, heat and Honkai energy rather than cheaply relocating inert warheads.

His four remaining cores are pushed past their safe output and **begin cracking**. Blood comes from his eyes, ears, nose and mouth. The Valkyries below realize that the “Herrscher enemy” they were ordered to kill is voluntarily destroying himself in order to keep **them** alive.

Even Otto is shaken. Watching Bai Ge sacrifice himself for strangers reminds him of **Kallen**: another powerful, impossibly altruistic person who could have chosen self-preservation but instead died helping others—and whose death Otto himself helped cause. For a moment he genuinely considers stopping.

He nevertheless chooses Kallen over the resemblance. Otto's obsession is not abstract love for saintly people; it is specifically love for **Kallen Kaslana**. If sacrificing another person like her gives him even a possible route to undoing his original failure, he proceeds.

He redirects one remaining fission missile away from Bai Ge's position.

Its target is **布洛克小镇**, rendered here cautiously as **Buluoke Town (布洛克小镇)**, the town where Bai Ge met Alexandra.

### Bai Ge saves Bronya before Bronya is even born

Alexandra sees the missile approaching and instinctively shields her pregnant abdomen despite knowing her body cannot possibly stop it. Her only concern is the unborn **Bronya**.

At the battlefield, Bai Ge realizes where the missile is going. Although his cores are already failing, he forces the Void authority beyond its limit and opens a portal approximately ten kilometers away, catching the missile before it destroys the town.

He no longer has enough power to redirect it somewhere harmless.

The fission weapon explodes **inside his own Void/Imaginary space**, meaning that Bai Ge effectively absorbs the attack into the spatial structure tied to himself. That is the final overload.

The town survives. Alexandra and unborn Bronya survive. The residents never learn that their “miracle” was produced by the dying Herrscher whom Schicksal was attempting to kill.

Bai Ge falls out of the sky.

Cecilia uses **Abyss Flower** to catch him with generated vegetation and desperately tries to heal him. His heart has effectively stopped and the accumulated damage is beyond conventional recovery. Yet Bai Ge opens his eyes long enough to ask the first question that matters to him: **did everyone else survive?**

Once he learns that they did, **Battle Continuation EX activates**.

The system gives him approximately **five additional hours** of functional existence. The implication is explicit: medically or existentially, Bai Ge is already dead; the trait is simply allowing the dead body to keep operating until its timer expires.

This is not a healing effect or a resurrection. The historical body remains beyond recovery. The significance is that the system had guaranteed **before the traversal began** that the death of this historical body would not kill Current-Era Bai. Battle Continuation therefore extends only the amount of work the doomed historical body can finish before the system returns Bai to 2014.

### Cecilia becomes Bai Ge's chosen mother figure

During these final hours, Bai Ge finally tells the Schicksal group his name. Until then, most have called him only “Herrscher of the Void.” He insists that he is **Bai Ge**, emphasizing his insistence on being treated as a person rather than a disaster classification.

He asks Cecilia a painful question: was his wish really unreasonable? He and Sirin's group only wanted a warm, peaceful place where they could live together. Cecilia tells him that nothing about that wish was excessive; the adults and organizations around them are the ones who failed.

Bai Ge asks Cecilia to **take care of Sirin, Avrora, Agata and Galina after he is gone** and offers his remaining cores as payment. Cecilia refuses payment and says she will protect them because Bai Ge has already saved everyone there.

When Cecilia holds him, Bai Ge admits that he has never had parents in either life and has never experienced this kind of maternal warmth. He tells her that **he wishes she could be his mother**. Cecilia, already emotionally devastated by what has happened to him, internally wishes she could accept that role.

This maternal bond is one of the permanent relationships recognized by the simulator and directly contributes to Bai Ge's post-simulation rewards.

### Bai Ge's farewell to Sirin

Sirin refuses to accept that Bai Ge will die. The home full of warmth and flowers was his promise, and in her mind Bai Ge does not break promises. She says that her own wish is even simpler: **she only wants Bai Ge to remain alive and able to smile**.

Bai Ge finally tells her something she can hold onto: their separation is temporary. He promises that **they will meet again in the future**, even if that future is many years away. He renders Sirin unconscious because she otherwise refuses to release him; even unconscious, she clings to him so forcefully that he must use Void authority to separate them.

He kisses her forehead, tells her not to give up on the future he has opened for her, and leaves her in Cecilia's care. The narrative explicitly emphasizes the fundamental rewrite: **Sirin has survived the point where the original Second Eruption should have ended her story. Cecilia has survived as well.**

### Bella is ordered to become free

Bai Ge next releases Bella.

Bella understands herself as his companion and servant and initially refuses to leave a dying master. Bai Ge rejects that interpretation of their relationship. To him, Bella is **family**. He gave her intelligence and human form not so that she could become a disposable servant but so that she could possess a future.

He therefore turns her own loyalty into his final command: **live freely, protect Sirin and the others, and wait until Bai Ge returns approximately fifteen years later**. Bella accepts and promises that however long the wait takes, she will survive until they meet again.

This means that, when Volume One closes, the rewritten history contains a living, intelligent **Bella with a human body, Judgment-class Honkai-beast origin and four pseudo-Herrscher cores**, ordered to protect the family and wait for Bai Ge. Later Volume Three continuity confirms that she survives the intervening years and eventually reunites with him in the Current Era.

### Bai Ge's final raid on Schicksal

With roughly two hours left on Battle Continuation EX, Bai Ge decides that dying peacefully would leave Otto with too comfortable an outcome.

He attacks **Schicksal's floating headquarters**, opening large numbers of portals and summoning a force approaching ten thousand Honkai beasts, mostly high-grade units. He smashes the headquarters' defenses and launches an assault.

Despite calling it revenge, Bai Ge orders the Honkai beasts **not to slaughter ordinary Valkyries**. Their targets are buildings, weapons, mechs and Schicksal infrastructure. He regards most Valkyries as people genuinely risking their lives to protect the world, not as extensions of Otto personally.

The raid devastates Schicksal's already reduced military position. Otto has expended enormous amounts of hardware, lost or alienated his strongest fighters, and is forced to activate additional hidden resources and Soulium combat bodies.

But even this attack is partly misdirection.

Bai Ge is searching for **young biological Kiana Kaslana**.

### Biological Kiana, the future Durandal

Bai Ge finds Cecilia and Siegfried's young daughter inside the family's residence at Schicksal. The text explicitly identifies her as the **“true/biological Kiana,” future Durandal**. This is necessary terminology for translating later chapters because the present-day teenage Kiana whom Bai Ge knows is **K-423**, while this child is Cecilia and Siegfried's biological daughter.

The little girl is approximately two years old, white-haired and blue-eyed, extremely bold and immediately curious about Bai Ge rather than frightened by him. Bai Ge tells her he has come on behalf of her mother and intends to remove her from Schicksal so that Otto can no longer use her as leverage against Cecilia.

They leave together through Bai Ge's spatial movement. Bai Ge lets the child enjoy flying through the sky and notices she carries a **Homu doll** supposedly given to her by a blond man on her mother's behalf.

The “gift” is an Otto trap.

Inside the doll Bai Ge finds a transmitter and a **miniaturized Honkai-energy explosive**. It is already detonating by the time he understands what it is. He shields Kiana with his body, losing his left arm and absorbing most of the explosion, but shrapnel still penetrates both of them and the pair fall from high altitude.

Kiana is critically injured. Bai Ge, now almost completely out of Honkai energy and already sustained only by Battle Continuation, uses **Death authority to heal her instead of saving power for himself**. Her condition stabilizes.

Otto and Amber arrive after the explosion. Otto recognizes that Bai Ge has exhausted even the remnants of his power saving Kiana and no longer poses a conventional combat threat. Yet Otto's reaction is no longer simple triumph. He is genuinely impressed, even ashamed by comparison, because Bai Ge has successfully protected almost everyone he set out to save while Otto's own five-century quest is defined by his failure to save the one person most important to him.

### Bai Ge's final move against Otto

Otto admits that he envies Bai Ge.

From Otto's perspective, Bai Ge actually became what Otto once wished he could have been: **the hero who reaches his loved ones in time**. Bai Ge changed tragedy after tragedy, saved the Babylon children, preserved Sirin, preserved Cecilia, prevented the catastrophic Second Eruption from unfolding in its original form, kept Cheng Lixue and others alive, protected Alexandra and unborn Bronya, and finally saved Kiana even after Otto's trap.

Bai Ge, however, has one final problem: after he disappears, Otto will still control Kiana's immediate surroundings.

So he uses Otto's obsession with Kallen against him.

Bai Ge demands that Otto swear **on Kallen Kaslana's name** that he will never harm Kiana Kaslana. He threatens to self-destruct the remaining Herrscher cores if Otto refuses. Bai Ge reasons that Otto may lie about almost anything, but will not casually profane the one person he has placed above everything else.

Otto gives the oath.

Bai Ge then adds an even more powerful psychological lock. Drawing upon his meta-knowledge of the future, he tells Otto that **a genuine route to bringing his saint back exists somewhere in Otto's future**—but that if Otto harms Kiana, that future miracle will disappear.

For Otto, who will pursue even a one-in-ten-thousand possibility of restoring Kallen, that is enough. Whether Bai Ge can literally enforce the condition is almost irrelevant. Otto believes that a Herrscher connected with Honkai might possess information about the future that he cannot access, and he cannot find evidence that Bai Ge is lying.

Battle Continuation reaches its end.

Bai Ge's past-simulation body dissolves into light. **Four cracked Herrscher gems/cores remain at the site**, and Otto picks them up. He orders Amber to take exceptional care of the unconscious Kiana and concludes, in substance, that Bai Ge has won their contest.

For continuity bookkeeping, use **four** as Otto's final historical core haul. Chapter 51 explicitly says Otto obtained “exactly four Herrscher cores,” and when Bai Ge's traversal body dissolves it leaves **four dim, cracked Herrscher gems** on the ground for Otto to collect. Earlier hostage material shows Bai Ge being forced to begin core extraction, but the novel's own final accounting is four. A translator should therefore record **four as the authoritative end-state** rather than deriving a fifth by summing intermediate handoffs. Later history confirms that Otto uses Bai-Ge-derived Herrscher material in the causal route that creates the rewritten K-423.

## Bai Ge's power and system progression in Volume One

### The starting Current-Era Bai Ge

At the beginning of the novel Bai Ge possesses **knowledge, money and a system that has not finished loading**, but almost no useful combat capability. He is physically an ordinary young man and has essentially no Honkai resistance. His near-death after Mei's first awakening is therefore genuine rather than staged weakness.

The system becomes useful only when the Honkai corruption is already turning him toward a deadwalker/zombie state.

### The first Past Simulator's temporary traits

For the Second-Eruption run the system gives Bai Ge six major temporary traits:

| Temporary trait | Practical function in Volume One |
|---|---|
| **Honkai Energy Adaptability EX** | Lets a human body tolerate Babylon's extreme Honkai exposure and become a viable Herrscher vessel. |
| **“Honkai's Biological/Favored Son” EX (崩坏的亲儿子EX)** | Makes Bai Ge extraordinarily favored as a Herrscher candidate. |
| **Pain Insensitivity B** | Greatly reduces perceived pain during experimentation. |
| **Extreme Pain Insensitivity A** | Further suppresses pain response, allowing him to remain functional through torture. |
| **Battle Continuation EX** | Allows his body to continue acting after otherwise fatal damage; decisive after Otto's missile strike. |
| **Unyielding Will A** | Helps Bai Ge preserve his original self and suppress the newly generated Herrscher personality. |

These belong to the **historical run**, not automatically to the permanent Current-Era package unless chapter 52 separately converts them into rewards.

### Historical Herrscher of the Void

Babylon's experiments unintentionally create the conditions Bai Ge needs for Herrscherization: extreme Honkai compatibility, enormous accumulated energy, severe genuine negative emotion and a body repeatedly pushed beyond normal limits.

When the transformation occurs, Bai Ge reaches roughly **2,000 HW**, regenerates his ruined body, and becomes the historical **Herrscher of the Void**. A Herrscher personality forms, but Unyielding Will and the fanfiction's Elysia-related “human Herrscher” logic allow Bai Ge's own personality to remain in control.

The phrase **人之律者** in this portion is best understood as **human-aligned Herrscher / a Herrscher who chooses humanity**, not as proof that Bai Ge literally occupies Elysia's unique canonical Herrscher title.

### The Moon and the five full historical cores

On the Moon Bai Ge receives four additional full Current-Era-style Herrscher gems/cores from the Honkai/Previous-Era lunar reserve:

| Historical full core | Authority |
|---|---|
| **Original Void core** | Space, portals, Imaginary barriers, subspace attacks. Later continuity identifies the inherited historical Void core inside K-423 as the **Gem of Freedom (自由宝石)** and recognizes Bai Ge as its former bearer. |
| **Gem of Conquest (征服宝石)** | Thunder/electromagnetism. |
| **Gem of Desire (渴望宝石)** | Wind, air pressure, ideal-fluid defense. |
| **Gem of Serenity (静谧宝石)** | Death/life-cell manipulation. |
| **Gem of Haste (疾疫宝石)** | Flame/heat and molecular-motion-related power. |

This is the state correctly described as **five-core Herrscher Bai Ge** during the rewritten Second Eruption.

### Bella's four pseudo-cores are a separate inventory

Bai Ge then spends Honkai energy to **create**, rather than surrender, four pseudo-Herrscher cores for Benares:

- pseudo-Flame,
- pseudo-Death,
- pseudo-Wind,
- pseudo-Thunder.

All four are placed in Benares/Bella. Bai Ge still possesses his own five full cores at this stage. This distinction is important because otherwise a translator can accidentally subtract Bella's pseudo-cores from Bai Ge's inventory.

### Damage and loss during Otto's endgame

Otto's hostage play forces Bai Ge to begin extracting his own full cores, beginning with the Thunder/Conquest core. Later the fission-missile saturation pushes the remaining full cores into cracking failure. The historical body is already effectively dead before Battle Continuation gives it a final few hours.

The final accounting should follow chapter 51: **Otto's net gain from the historical Bai Ge is four cracked Herrscher cores/gems.** The source explicitly says Otto has obtained “four Herrscher cores,” and after Bai dissolves only **four dim, cracked Herrscher gems** remain on the ground.

Do not add the earlier Conquest extraction as a fifth final acquisition. That is an **intermediate possession state** during the hostage scene, whereas chapter 51 gives the authoritative net end-state.

### Chapter 52: permanent Current-Era reward package

When Bai Ge returns to 2014, the system does **not** restore the historical five full cores inside his present body. Instead it converts the run into this permanent package:

| Permanent reward | End-of-Volume-One meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fragmentary Void authority** | Reduced space/portal capability. |
| **Fragmentary Death authority** | Reduced life/death biological control; already useful for healing/reconstruction. |
| **Fragmentary Flame authority** | Reduced Flame-Herrscher capability. |
| **Fragmentary Wind authority** | Reduced Wind-Herrscher capability and defense. |
| **Fragmentary Thunder authority** | Reduced Thunder/electromagnetic capability. |
| **Fused Kaslana + Schariac stigmata** | Bai Ge's personal stigmata structure can combine multiple stigmata inheritances. |
| **Schariac holy blood** | Anti-Honkai property inherited from the Cecilia/Schariac bond. |
| **Kaslana physical constitution** | Major physical potential and durability improvement. |
| **Absolute Honkai adaptability** | Permanently solves the near-zero Honkai resistance that was killing him in Nagazora. |
| **Personal limit breakthrough** | Raises his future growth ceiling. |
| **Super Gaming Talent** | Deliberately comic but mechanically real reward associated by Bai Ge with Cecilia. |

The system explicitly says the five reduced authorities come from his having become a five-core Herrscher during the run, while stigmata/bloodline and miscellaneous rewards are drawn from bonds with people such as **Sirin, Cecilia and biological Kiana/Durandal**.

The reward screen ends with an ellipsis after the named package. As with the later Volume-Two settlement, this is **source-level non-enumeration** rather than evidence that a missing complete reward list must be reconstructed. The explicitly named chapter-52 rewards above are authoritative; later powers should not be back-filled into Volume One unless later prose specifically says they came from this settlement.

The clean translator-facing distinction entering Volume Two is therefore:

**Historical simulation Bai Ge = five full physical Herrscher cores and overwhelming temporary output.**

**Current-Era Bai Ge after chapter 52 = five permanent fragmentary authorities + fused stigmata/bloodline/body rewards, not five full cores.**

## Character and relationship state at the end of Volume One

### Bai Ge

Bai Ge begins as a powerless transmigrator whose main assets are foreknowledge and willingness to intervene. By chapter 52 he has lived and died through an entire rewritten historical crisis and returned almost instantaneously from Mei and Kiana's perspective.

His moral pattern is now clear. He is **not a pacifist**. He can torture or kill Babylon researchers, crush attacking fighter pilots, and punish Otto with extreme cruelty when he judges the target culpable. At the same time he repeatedly spares ordinary Valkyries, civilians and opponents acting under misinformation. His working moral distinction is **culpability rather than faction**.

His franchise knowledge remains diegetic and tactically important. He knows future identities, recognizes biological Kiana as future Durandal, anticipates Fu Hua's methods, understands Otto's fixation on Kallen, and uses that knowledge to change decisions rather than merely comment on them.

### Raiden Mei

Mei has genuine friends for the first time since the collapse of her family's status. She has explicitly admitted to herself that **she loves Bai Ge** and refuses to turn that love into hostility toward Kiana.

Her public “girlfriend” status still originates from Bai Ge's fake-dating protection plan. The correct end-state is therefore two simultaneous truths:

- the relationship began as a protective fiction,
- **Mei's own romantic feelings are completely real**.

She possesses **Pulse Katana Type-19**.

The **Thunder Queen / stigmata consciousness** remains active inside her. It is not destroyed at the end of the aborted eruption. It voluntarily yields control after choosing Bai Ge and Mei's human life over Honkai's command. Later Volumes Two and Three confirm that it remains a distinct personality.

### Kiana / K-423

The Current-Era teenage Kiana is already one of Bai Ge's closest companions. She knows the Mei relationship began as a protection strategy, helps defend Mei, joins the ME Corp infiltration, and fights the Titans beside Bai Ge.

Bai Ge knows she is connected to **K-423**, but at chapter 52 he has not yet worked out the exact revised causal recipe by which K-423 exists after Sirin's history was changed.

**Later-confirmed consequence:** Volume Two has Bai Ge correctly infer, and Volume Three confirms, that rewritten K-423 is constructed from **Bai-Ge-derived historical Herrscher-of-the-Void material plus biological Kiana/Durandal's Kaslana material**. This later confirmation should inform translation continuity without being retroactively inserted into Bai Ge's knowledge before chapter 54.

### Sirin

Sirin never becomes the catastrophic historical Second Herrscher through the original route because Bai Ge takes that role instead.

Her bond with him becomes one of the emotional centers of the volume. He is the childhood companion inserted into her history, the boy who takes Babylon's torture in her place, the person who promises a warm home full of flowers, and the family member whose life she values enough to risk her own during Otto's hostage play.

Her final state is **alive**, under Cecilia's protection, and waiting for Bai Ge's promised return roughly fifteen years later.

**Later-confirmed consequence:** Sirin does survive the intervening altered history and physically reunites with Bai Ge in the Current Era before the end of Volume Three.

### Avrora, Agata and Galina

All three survive Babylon because Bai Ge redirects the human experimentation onto himself. They become genuine family rather than disposable historical casualties.

During Otto's hostage play they join Sirin in choosing to risk death rather than remain leverage forcing Bai Ge to mutilate himself. Cecilia accepts responsibility for them after Bai Ge's impending death becomes unavoidable.

### Bella / Benares

Benares is born as Bai Ge's accompanying Judgment-class Honkai beast. Bai Ge creates four pseudo-Herrscher cores for her, gives her a human body through Death authority, and names her **Bella**.

Her loyalty is personal to Bai Ge, not a generic rule that any Herrscher of the Void can command her. Bai Ge explicitly rejects treating her as property and orders her to live freely, protect Sirin's group, and wait for him.

**Later-confirmed consequence:** Bella survives approximately fifteen years and reunites with Bai Ge in the Current Era. Volume Three also confirms the importance of her personal loyalty when she rejects K-423's newly awakened HoV personality trying to claim Benares' allegiance.

### Cecilia Schariac

Cecilia survives the rewritten Second Eruption. She learns Babylon's truth, rejects Otto's orders, fights on Bai Ge's side after the misunderstanding, tries desperately to heal him, and accepts responsibility for Sirin, Avrora, Agata and Galina.

Her relationship with Bai Ge becomes explicitly **maternal**. Bai Ge admits that neither of his lives gave him normal parental warmth; Cecilia wants to give him that home. The system later recognizes the bond by granting Bai Ge Schariac holy blood and Schariac stigmata inheritance.

**Later-confirmed consequence:** Cecilia remains alive in the rewritten history and reunites with Bai Ge in the Current Era together with Sirin and Bella.

### Siegfried Kaslana

Siegfried attacks Bai Ge first because he is acting on Schicksal's Herrscher threat model. Bai Ge later respects him because Siegfried is willing to burn himself out with Shamash to protect Cecilia.

He survives and refuses Otto's final order to attack Bai Ge, causing Otto to brand him a traitor alongside Cecilia and Theresa.

### Theresa Apocalypse

Theresa learns that Babylon's supposed research mission concealed experimentation on children. She witnesses Otto willing to sacrifice his own Valkyries and eventually refuses his orders.

This becomes the historical foundation for the much more independent **St. Freya** seen in Volume Two, whose policy is to judge a Herrscher by actual allegiance rather than classification alone.

### Welt Yang

Welt approaches Bai Ge hoping that another Herrscher might stand with humanity. After learning Babylon's truth, he accepts that humanity has no right to demand immediate service from the child it tortured into becoming a Herrscher.

He survives this rewritten Second Eruption without the repeated death/reconstruction pattern Bai Ge remembers from the original story.

### Fu Hua

Fu Hua initially fights because of the misunderstanding around Siegfried, but she notices during combat that Bai Ge is deliberately avoiding fatal strikes.

Otto later neutralizes her participation by threatening **Cheng Lixue**. Bai Ge's later internal accounting explicitly treats **Cheng Lixue as surviving** the rewritten Second Eruption.

### Alexandra Pavlovna Zaychik and unborn Bronya

Bai Ge meets pregnant **Alexandra**, who shares scarce food with the Babylon children. In return he secretly uses Death authority to improve her physical health and bless the pregnancy.

Later Otto redirects a Honkai fission weapon toward **Buluoke Town (布洛克小镇)**. Bai Ge catches it and takes the blast into his own spatial domain, saving Alexandra and unborn Bronya at the cost of fatally overloading himself.

**Later-confirmed consequence:** Alexandra survives in the rewritten history and gives birth to Bronya. Bronya's later childhood still becomes traumatic through a different causal route, demonstrating that saving one cause does not automatically erase every future event.

### Young Cocolia

Bai Ge meets a much younger Cocolia who has not yet become the 2014 antagonist he knows. He consciously refuses to kill her preemptively because doing so could erase positive outcomes for children she will later shelter.

This creates a deliberate contrast between **the person Cocolia is in the earlier era** and the woman currently orchestrating Mei's suffering in 2014.

### Biological Kiana / future Durandal

The small Kiana Bai Ge tries to rescue from Schicksal headquarters is **Cecilia and Siegfried's biological daughter**, explicitly identified by the fanfic as future **Durandal**. She is not Current-Era K-423.

Otto's bomb hidden in a HOMU doll injures both Bai Ge and the child. Bai Ge spends his remaining Death authority healing her, but Battle Continuation ends before he can deliver her to Cecilia.

The exact end-state is:

**biological Kiana remains physically on Otto/Amber's side, but Otto has sworn on Kallen not to harm Kiana and believes harming her may erase his future chance to restore Kallen.**

### Otto Apocalypse

Otto loses the Second Eruption strategically even though he remains alive and gains Herrscher material. He loses or alienates top combatants, sacrifices enormous Schicksal resources, exposes Babylon's atrocities, drives Cecilia/Siegfried/Theresa away from obedience, and watches Bai Ge repeatedly protect the people Otto is willing to sacrifice.

Bai Ge then exploits the only value Otto will not casually desecrate—**Kallen**—to bind his future behavior toward biological Kiana.

There is no longer a need to treat “pre-traversal 2014 Otto” and “rewritten-history Otto” as an unresolved paradox. The historical rewrite is real, and later Volume Three explains why Current-Era people do not immediately identify teenage Bai Ge as the same Herrscher they met in 2000: **the system applies cognitive/identity protection that suppresses the easy linkage between Bai Ge's traversal identities without erasing the underlying historical memories**. Otto eventually breaks through by comparing evidence, then misattributes the restriction to a supposed Sentience power.

## Faction state entering Volume Two

### Cocolia's network

Cocolia's attempt to manufacture the Third Eruption in Nagazora has failed. Mei's Thunder Queen chose Bai Ge/human bonds instead of Honkai, and the enormous eruption energy was released rather than used to destroy the city.

Cocolia remains active at the exact chapter-52 boundary and has ordered **Bronya** toward Nagazora. Her removal as an active antagonist does not occur until later.

### Schicksal under Otto

Schicksal has suffered catastrophic material and political damage in the rewritten 2000 history. Otto expends large parts of its arsenal, loses the uncomplicated loyalty of several top fighters, and is exposed as willing to kill his own Valkyries when convenient.

He nevertheless remains Overseer and retains enormous infrastructure, Soulium bodies, information networks and the **four cracked Herrscher cores/gems** obtained from Bai Ge's historical body.

### Theresa / future St. Freya bloc

Theresa's trust in Otto and Schicksal's moral authority is badly damaged by Babylon and the missile betrayal. Several Valkyries who would otherwise have died survive because Bai Ge shielded them.

This is the historical basis for the stronger, more independent St. Freya encountered in Volume Two.

### Anti-Entropy

Welt, Einstein and Tesla now possess a real historical encounter with Bai Ge as a human-aligned Herrscher. They know that the Second-Eruption Herrscher could have attacked humanity indiscriminately but instead prioritized children and civilians.

### World Serpent

World Serpent notices the 2014 Nagazora anomaly through Gray Serpent/Raven surveillance. It does not yet have the later House-of-the-Hearth historical contact created in Volume Two, but Bai Ge has already entered its field of interest.

## Altered history and causal rules established by Volume One

### The Past Simulator changes real history and produces asymmetric memory continuity

The first traversal is not merely a dream whose rewards are copied back to Bai Ge. The system sends him into the past with a local identity/body, allows him to form real relationships, and permits changes to propagate into the Current Era.

The resulting memory model is asymmetric:

- **Bai** remembers the 2014 departure state and the historical life he personally lived;
- people in the rewritten world live forward from the **new history** and retain the resulting historical memories;
- they are not ordinarily presented as consciously carrying an untouched “old canon” memory track alongside the rewrite;
- cross-era recognition of Bai himself is separately affected by system identity protection.

Volume Two later proves real-history propagation decisively when present Bronya acquires the childhood Bai creates for her. Volume One should therefore be read as the **first true shared-history rewrite**, not an alternate dream branch.

### Bai Ge can replace another person's tragic role

The most important structural move of Volume One is **substitution**.

Sirin and the other girls should be Babylon's victims; Bai Ge takes the experiments instead.

Sirin should become the catastrophic Second Herrscher; Bai Ge takes that role instead.

Cecilia and many Valkyries should die under the Second-Eruption disaster; Bai Ge takes the missile damage instead.

Alexandra and unborn Bronya are threatened by Otto's redirected weapon; Bai Ge takes that blast too.

This becomes one of the novel's recurring methods for defeating “fate”: Bai Ge does not always make the dangerous event vanish. He often **moves its cost onto himself**.

### Familiar people can survive through completely different causes

The rewritten Second Eruption removes Sirin from her original catastrophic role, yet K-423 still exists in 2014. Volume Two later explains that sufficiently important people/events can be reconstructed through new causal routes.

In K-423's case, the new route uses **Bai Ge's historical Herrscher material** in the place occupied by Sirin's Herrscher material in the original story.

Therefore a translator should never assume that a familiar character's existence proves the old cause still happened.

### Relationships created by the system are historically real

The system may fabricate a plausible starting identity or pre-existing memory so that Bai Ge can enter a period, but the relationships formed afterward are genuine.

Sirin's love and family bond, Cecilia's maternal attachment, Bella's personal loyalty and the historical Schicksal/Anti-Entropy memories all become real parts of the rewritten world.

### The system protects Bai Ge's cross-era identity

Later Volume Three resolves an apparent problem created here: why do Welt, Otto, Siegfried and others who met the 2000 Bai Ge not immediately identify teenage Bai Ge in the Current Era?

The answer is **system cognitive/identity protection**. People retain their rewritten historical memories, but an invisible restriction prevents them from naturally completing the equation “Current-Era Bai Ge = historical Herrscher Bai Ge.” Strong evidence or sufficiently deep bonds can eventually overcome that protection.

This should be treated as a system rule, not as everyone inexplicably forgetting the Second Eruption.

## System functions established by the end of Volume One

| System function | Established behavior by chapter 52 |
|---|---|
| **Identity/background fabrication** | Can create a legal/social identity for Bai Ge in the Current Era and a plausible local historical identity inside a traversal. |
| **Past Simulator / historical traversal** | Sends Bai Ge into an earlier era through a local historical identity/body; historical death does not kill his proper Current-Era existence; the Current-Era departure point effectively waits; changes can become real shared history. |
| **Temporary traversal traits** | Supplies run-specific abilities such as Honkai Adaptability EX and Battle Continuation EX. They expire with the run unless separately converted into rewards. |
| **Low historical-correction leverage over Bai** | Before the first traversal the system explicitly says history's corrective force has minimal influence over Bai himself. This is the early foundation for his later ability to break fixed/corrective outcomes. |
| **Causal substitution** | Not named as a separate button, but established behavior: Bai repeatedly preserves an important event while moving its required cost onto his protected historical body. Volume Two later formalizes the same logic through X-10. |
| **Bond/reward conversion** | Converts powers acquired during a run and traits associated with people Bai Ge bonds with into permanent Current-Era rewards. |
| **Time compression / suspended departure point** | Bai Ge can experience months in a historical run while less than one external second passes in the Current Era; the source tells him before entry that he will return to the same Current-Era time regardless of historical duration. |
| **Cognitive/identity protection** | Later-confirmed system protection prevents ordinary observers from easily linking Bai Ge's different historical/current identities while leaving the rewritten historical memories themselves intact. |

The **targeted past traversal** mode does not unlock until Volume Two and should not be retroactively listed as a Volume-One function.

## Settled source boundaries — what future work does **not** need to reopen Volume One to answer

The following points are fully settled and should not be treated as open research questions again:

- **Volume One is chapters 1–52 inclusive.**
- The Second-Eruption traversal is a **real historical rewrite**, not a disposable dream simulation.
- Bai's historical death cannot kill his proper Current-Era self.
- The source explicitly says **historical correction has minimal influence over Bai**.
- The Current-Era departure point effectively waits; Bai returns after months of subjective history with **less than one external second elapsed**.
- Bai receives **six temporary Second-Eruption traits**; they are not all permanent after the run.
- Historical Bai becomes the **Herrscher of the Void**, then absorbs four Moon cores for a total of **five full historical cores**.
- Bella's four pseudo-cores are **separate newly created cores**, not Bai's Moon cores transferred away.
- Otto's authoritative final historical haul is **exactly four cracked Herrscher cores/gems**.
- Battle Continuation EX keeps an already-dead historical body functional for roughly five hours; it does not revive or heal it.
- Sirin, Avrora, Agata, Galina and Cecilia survive the rewritten Second Eruption.
- Bella survives with her human body and four pseudo-cores.
- Alexandra survives; unborn Bronya survives.
- Cheng Lixue survives the rewritten outcome.
- biological Kiana/Durandal survives the HOMU-bomb incident and remains with Otto/Amber after Bai disappears.
- K-423 is **not** biological Kiana/Durandal.
- K-423's later Herrscher material is **Bai-derived**, not Sirin-derived; Volume Two infers this and Volume Three confirms it.
- Sirin is a separate living person and is **not** K-423's later HoV personality.
- chapter 52 grants five **fragmentary** authorities, not five full Current-Era cores.
- the chapter-52 stigmata/body package includes fused Schariac + Kaslana stigmata, Schariac holy blood, Kaslana constitution, personal-limit breakthrough, Super Gaming Talent and absolute Honkai adaptability.
- the system's later **cognitive identity protection** explains why historical witnesses do not automatically equate teenage Current-Era Bai with the 2000 Herrscher.
- the Elysia/Origin human-Herrscher mechanism is clarified later; Volume Four shows why human-aligned Herrschers can exist without requiring Volume-One characters to understand that cosmology yet.

There are only a few genuine chronological/source limits:

1. Chapter 52's reward interface ends with an ellipsis, so the novel itself does not immediately enumerate every minor reward beyond the explicit named package.
2. Volume One establishes low corrective leverage over Bai, but the detailed **fixed-point/corrective-fate model** is not explained until Volume Two.
3. Volume One creates the cross-era recognition problem, but the full **cognitive identity-protection** rule is not explained until Volume Three.
4. Volume One invokes Elysia's legacy in human-aligned Herrscher behavior, but the full **Origin miracle** is not reconstructed until Volume Four.

Those are boundaries of when the story explains its mechanics, not unresolved contradictions.

Two additional distinctions are especially important for an LLM translator:

**Full historical core ≠ pseudo-core ≠ fragmentary permanent authority.** Bai Ge has five full cores in the historical run; Bella receives four separately created pseudo-cores; chapter 52 gives Current-Era Bai Ge five reduced authority fragments.

**Sirin ≠ K-423's later HoV.** In the rewritten history Sirin remains a separate living person. Volume Three later confirms that K-423's HoV personality is born from Bai-Ge-derived historical Void material.

The prose also regularly shifts from trauma and self-sacrifice to gamer slang, memes, meta-commentary and insults. Bai Ge's references to the “original plot,” future identities, boss mechanics and character builds are **diegetic memories from his previous life**, not narrator-only exposition. Preserve that tonal whiplash rather than flattening him into uniformly solemn heroic prose.

## Later-confirmed consequences of Volume One's rewrite

These facts are not all known by Bai Ge or the 2014 cast at chapter 52, but later volumes confirm them and they are useful for continuity-sensitive translation:

- **Alexandra survives** the rewritten 2000 history and gives birth to Bronya.
- **Sirin, Cecilia and Bella survive the intervening years** and eventually reunite physically with Bai Ge in the Current Era.
- **Cheng Lixue survives** the rewritten Second-Eruption outcome in Bai Ge's own historical accounting.
- The four-core material Otto retains from the historical crisis becomes part of the altered post-2000 power landscape.
- **K-423's revised Herrscher side derives from Bai Ge's historical HoV material**, while her Kaslana side is tied to biological Kiana/Durandal.
- The system's **cognitive identity protection** explains why historical witnesses do not immediately recognize teenage Bai Ge as the 2000 Herrscher.
- Theresa's experience of Bai Ge as a human-aligned Herrscher contributes directly to St. Freya's later rule that a Herrscher should be judged by actual allegiance rather than exterminated automatically.

These are consequences of Volume One, but they should not be inserted into dialogue or character knowledge before the later chapters actually reveal them.

The immediate opening logic of Volume Two is therefore straightforward. Mei and Kiana experience **no visible time skip**: the Bai Ge who was secretly dying from Honkai corruption appears almost instantaneously healthy, radically stronger and more emotionally affected than before. Bai Ge, meanwhile, has just lived months of torture, family life, Herrscherhood, death and farewell that his present companions did not experience with him.

Volume One establishes the novel's governing formula: **Bai Ge enters a tragedy he remembers, turns fictional foreknowledge into a real relationship, and then changes history by refusing to let another person remain the price of the event.** The system converts those relationships and sacrifices into permanent mechanical growth, so the emotional rewrite of the Second Eruption becomes the literal power foundation from which Volume Two begins.
